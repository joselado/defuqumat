## What was missing, as found

The entry said: symmetrise `chi_0(G, G')`, a rotation of both G labels at once. That is
right, and it is half of it; the other half is **time reversal**, which QE reduces a
non-magnetic grid with on top of the point group. What is in
`tddft/chi0.py:_completed` now:

- an operation `c -> c M + t` acts as `U X U^dagger`, with `U` the cartesian rotation on
  the 3x3 head and a phased permutation `exp(-2 pi i m.t) delta(n, M m)` on the body (the
  Coulomb factor depends on `|G|` alone);
- time reversal acts as `P X^T P`, `P` the reflection `G -> -G` on the body (the
  sphere's `reflection` table, already there for the antiresonant term) and `-1` on the
  head, from `r_G(-k) = conj(r_{-G}(k))` and `r_a(-k) = -conj(r_a(k))`.

The group and its tiling check are `system/wedge.py:wedge_group`, shared with I40, I41,
I46, I47. A response sphere cut through a shell by round-off is refused by name.

Still refused: a magnetic wedge (`chi_0` is `nspin = 1` anyway), a reduced set on a
`nosym` run, a set the group does not tile.

> For the spectrum the reason is that symmetrizing $\chi^0_{\mathbf G\mathbf G'}$ rotates
> two $\mathbf G$ indices at once, and for the conductivity it is that the antisymmetric
> part is an axial vector, which a wedge does not sum to the whole cell.

and the sentences around it ("...the whole k-grid, and a symmetry-reduced k-set gives an

> On a symmetry-reduced k-set both are averaged over the group the k-set was cut with, so

> a k-set that the group does not unfold to a uniform grid, still give an error. Then you

**What the record said is still refused, corrected.** The record and the module docstring
of `system/wedge.py` say a shifted grid is refused as a set the group does not tile. It was
not: on `4 4 4 1 1 1` the 96 operations unfold the 10-point wedge uniformly onto 256 points,
the star closure, which passes the uniformity test, and the completion then sums over the
closure and not over the 64-point grid. Measured (`sh.log`): the shifted wedge against the
explicit 256-point closure 4.2e-6 (the harness printed the gap between bands 19 and 20,
not the cut gap, so whether that 4.2e-6 is a cut multiplet is not settled), against the 64-point shifted grid 0.41. So the completion is right for the sample it
sums, and the sample is not the one the run asked for. `wedge_group` now refuses a
non-scalar quantity when the union of the orbits is not the size of the recorded grid
(`tests/unit/test_wedge_refusals.py::test_a_shifted_wedge_is_refused_and_the_unshifted_one_is_not`).
A collinear `nspin = 2` wedge (which I7 lets into `chi_0`) and a nonmagnetic spinor wedge (I8)
carry no `t_rev`, so `wedge_group` did not refuse them, and no number covers them;
`require_a_sum_over_states_regime` refuses both on a reduced set
(`::test_a_two_channel_wedge_is_refused_for_chi_0_and_the_spectrum`). The magnetic wedge
(`ni-noncol-111-nosource.in`, 13 points) and the `nosym` refusal fire (`g.log`, the unit tests).
