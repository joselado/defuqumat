# I56. Berry-phase finite field (`lelfield`) x streamed wavefunction store: done

**The number.** `tests/regression/test_berryfield.py::test_a_streamed_store_is_the_in_memory_run`, D22
(CPU, one core, `w-inc-finitefield-v3`, log `review/incompat/finitefield/stream-2.log`), one sample: the
field step of the Si8 chain (`efield = 0.001`, `conv_thr = 1e-11`) with `wfc_store = 'stream'` (the states
a numpy array in host memory, walked one k-point at a time, the CPU's `k_batch = 1`) against the in-memory
run. Total **0.0 Ry apart** (bit-identical), `el_pol` **3.9e-14**, forces **4.6e-15 Ry/bohr** (the streamed
one through the chunked route, the in-memory one through the single pass), seven iterations both.
Asserted at 1e-9, 1e-7 and 1e-8. `tests/unit/test_berryfield.py::test_a_host_store_walked_in_chunks_is_the_whole_set`
checks the operator, its per-chunk view and the string phases against the whole-set ones on two channels
with a padded last chunk, without an SCF.

**What was missing** (the entry said "unread"). Three pieces. (1) The operator couples each k to its two
neighbours on the string, and `stream_diagonalize` overwrites the store chunk by chunk, so it has to be
built for the whole k-set **before** each walk, as `h_epsi_her_set` builds every dual before `c_bands`:
`BerryFieldSetup.host_term` builds `u` and `a` a chunk at a time into host arrays (neighbours gathered on
the host by Miller index), and `chunk_term` hands each chunk its rows through a global-to-local map
(`BerryTerm.rows`), since the solve indexes the Hamiltonian by the global k (`indices=chunk_rows`);
`stream_diagonalize` takes a `per_chunk` hook for it. (2) The string phases are walked a chunk at a time on
a host store. (3) The force: `forces/chunked.py` refused, and now takes the chunked route on a streamed
store, carrying the field's ions (`-E (ion_pol + acc)`) in its global term and adding the electrons'
`-E el_pol` to the energy as a constant, which is exact on a norm-conserving dataset (the only one the field
runs on), where the electronic phase does not move with the atoms. Memory: the device holds one chunk's
states, its neighbours, and its rows of `u` and `a`; the host holds `u` and `a`,
`2 x nk x nocc x npol npwx` complex, beside the store, where QE writes the duals to disk.

A trap met on the way: the first version named its local `field`, which shadowed `run_scf`'s
magnetic-field variable read at the end of the run (`AttributeError: 'BerryFieldSetup' object has no
attribute 'site_residuals'`, `auto-stream-1.log`); renamed.

**What stays refused**: k-point pools, in `run_scf` and in the chunked force, both by name, since a pool's
rows do not hold every k-point of a string.

**User guide**, `docs/guide/electric-field.md`: the "Limits" note says nothing about the streamed store, so
there is nothing to delete. If the integrator wants it said, add to the second paragraph: "A streamed
wavefunction store (`wfc_store = 'stream'`) works; k-point pools give an error."
