# I35. Ultracell x saw-tooth field (`tefield`)

Status: done for a saw-tooth across the modulation, with and without `dipfield`. A saw-tooth
along a direction in which the ultracell is longer than one cell, and `lelfield`, still refuse
by name.

## What was there before this unit

The guard was already narrowed on master by `ec98a1f` of the previous batch: the box carries
the supercell's saw-tooth, built by the same `SawtoothField` from the box density, the
ultracell lattice and every atom copy, and `run_ultracell` refuses only when
`supercell[edir] != 1`. Its check never closed in that batch: the `dipfield = .false.` arm
passed (`review/incompat/ultracell/r5-saw.log`) and the `dipfield = .true.` arm failed at the
tiled null, which stalled at dr2 = 4.4e-3 Ry after 200 iterations. The entry's missing term ("a
saw-tooth term in the ultracell potential") is what `ec98a1f` wrote, and it was right.

## Why the dipfield arm failed, and what changed

Not the box. `review/incompat/ultracell2/r1-dip.log` (harness `h35a.py`) scanned `emaxpos` on
the unit cell with `dipfield` and fixed occupations: bulk silicon has no vacuum for the ramp to
sit in, so its dipole correction is large (`E_f` = 0.546 Ry at `emaxpos = 0.6`, against 0.025
without `dipfield`), and it closes the gap: band 4 tops out at 0.474 Ry over the folded
k-points and band 5 bottoms out at 0.380. Fixed occupations fill four bands at every k-point,
which is then not the lowest 4N of the N nbnd folded states the ultracell fills, so the tiled
density is not a solution of the ultracell. Only `emaxpos` = 0.25, 0.75, 0.85 keep a positive
gap, with `E_f` of 6e-3, 2.8e-2 and 3e-3 Ry. The test's `dipfield` arm is now smeared
(Gaussian, 0.02 Ry) at `emaxpos = 0.6`, which keeps the large dipole term in play; a Gaussian
keeps the free energy variational, so the bound from above still holds.

## The number

`tests/regression/test_ultracell_regimes.py::test_a_saw_tooth_ultracell_bounds_the_supercell_from_above`,
silicon (`Si.pz-vbc.UPF`) at `MATCHED_ECUT`, `edir = 3`, `eamp = 0.002`, `emaxpos = 0.6`,
`eopreg = 0.2`, a 2x1x1 ultracell modulated along `a1` on `(1, 2, 2)`, against the explicit
2x1x1 supercell SCF under the same saw-tooth and the same `0.05 cos` modulation. Energy per
cell above the supercell's, D22, single runs:

| arm | run | null | nbnd = 12 | 24 | 48 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `dipfield = .false.` | `r1-saw.log` (126d6ec) | dV 4.4e-16, density 7.3e-8, E -1.6e-14 Ry | +8.092e-5 | +3.528e-6 | +3.636e-7 Ry |
| `dipfield = .true.`, smeared | `r2-dip.log` (126d6ec) | dV 1.8e-15, density 2.3e-6, E +3.2e-12 Ry | +1.952e-5 | +4.408e-6 | +4.789e-7 Ry |
| both arms, the committed test body | `r3-saw.log` (18cc163) | the same as the two rows above | the same | the same | the same, both passed |

Both arms are positive, monotone and below 1e-6 at 48 bands, which is the variational bound
of P88 with the saw-tooth in both functionals. With `dipfield` the field energy per cell
approaches the supercell's, 0.53833529, 0.53838820, 0.53839685 against 0.53840044 Ry. The
null's `E_f` with `dipfield` is 2.0e-6 Ry from the unit cell's (0.5385151688 against
0.5385171800), the frozen states' density error at first order times a dipole term 20 times
the bare ramp's; the test's tolerance on it is 1e-5 for that arm and 1e-8 without `dipfield`.

## The fix round: what the dipfield arm now asserts

A verifier's A/B (`review/incompat/ultracell2-ver/v35c.py`, on 4b87027) held the box saw-tooth
at its first evaluation, the tiled density, so the dipole no longer followed the box density,
and every assertion of the arm still passed: the gaps read +1.954e-5, +4.421e-6, +4.904e-7 Ry
against the box's +1.952e-5, +4.408e-6, +4.789e-7, and the induced density error at 48 bands
4.122e-3 against 4.130e-3. The dipole moves by only 3.4e-5 under this modulation, so the energy
is second order in its response and the arm was a tautology for the term it exists to test.

`E_f` per cell is first order in that response, so the arm now keeps the ladder's results and
asserts that `|E_f(nbnd) - E_f(supercell)/2|` falls with `nbnd` and is below 1e-5 Ry at 48
(1917b5f). D22, worktree `w-inc-ultracell2-v4` (1917b5f), harness `h35d.py`, which calls the
committed test function with `dipfield = True`, once as it stands and once with the verifier's
frozen saw-tooth, single runs (`review/incompat/ultracell2/r4-dip.log`):

| arm | `E_f` per cell from the supercell's 0.53840044 Ry, nbnd = 12, 24, 48 | the committed test |
|---|---|---|
| box dipole | 6.514e-5, 1.224e-5, 3.585e-6 Ry | passed |
| frozen dipole | 1.721e-4, 1.215e-4, 1.118e-4 Ry | fails at `errors[-1] < 1e-5` |

The frozen arm also falls with `nbnd`, so the monotone assertion alone would not tell the two
apart; the bound at 48 does, with the box 31 times below the frozen arm. The `dipfield =
.false.` arm is unchanged: its saw-tooth is the tiled one by construction, and the tiled null
already catches a missing or miscounted saw-tooth.

## User guide

`docs/guide/ultracell.md`, the Limits note. The sentence

> Spin spirals, `K_POINTS gamma` and a saw-tooth electric field (`tefield`) also
> give an error.

becomes

> Spin spirals and `K_POINTS gamma` also give an error. A saw-tooth electric
> field (`tefield`, also with `dipfield`) runs when the long cell is one unit
> cell long along the field, `edir`. This is the slab geometry the saw-tooth is
> for. Along a direction with more than one cell it gives an error, because the
> saw-tooth of the long cell then has a different period. A field from the Berry
> phase (`lelfield`) gives an error.

(The last sentence only if the page does not already say it elsewhere; it does not today.)
