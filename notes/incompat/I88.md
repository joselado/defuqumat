# I88. Elastic constants (strain response) x metal: done after one fix round (smeared norm-conserving metal on a closed nosym grid, whole-k route, empty top band; 1.6e-7 (M-V), 1.3e-6 (M-P), 2.2e-7 (Gaussian), 4.8e-8 (F-D) against a five-point difference of the free energy)

**The number.** The identity is `C_ijkl = (1/Omega) d^2F/dx_ij dx_kl`, with `F = E - TS` the free
energy the SCF reports (it reads -8.33210379934922 Ry on `tests/data/qe/al2-metal.in`, against `pw.x`'s
-8.33210380, `reference.out.al2-metal:342`, smearing term -0.0043717 Ry). The reference is a five-point
second difference of `F` over cells re-converged at `conv_thr = 1e-12` on the same frozen sphere
(`at_strain`), which is what the silicon test beside it uses (209.38 against 209.38 GPa). It shares only
the Hamiltonian with the analytic route, which is one `jvp` of the stress along the strain response.
Measured on D22 (worktree `/l/ladovj1/w-inc-I88` at `ed66e63c`, log `review/incompat/I88/al1.log`,
harness `notes/incompat/I88-harness/harness.py`, one sample each, 185 s on one core), with the strain
response at `tr2 = 1e-24`, `threshold = 1e-12` (converged in 11 passes, last `|ddv_scf|^2 = 4.6e-26`):

| component | analytic (GPa) | five-point, h = 2e-3 | 4e-3 | 8e-3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `C_1111` | 77.315370 | 77.315382 (-1.6e-7) | 77.315382 (-1.6e-7) | 77.315416 (-6.0e-7) |
| `C_1212` | -221.673311 | -221.673277 (+1.5e-7) | -221.672778 (+2.4e-6) | -221.664875 (+3.8e-5) |

The convergence parameter is the finite-difference step `h`. On the shear the discrepancy falls by 15.8 and
then 15.7 per halving of `h`, the `h^4` of a five-point stencil, down to 1.5e-7; on `C_1111` the truncation
is smaller and the floor, 1.6e-7, is reached already at 4e-3 (it is the SCF and response tolerance, not the
stencil). The three-point difference at the same energies reads 77.2996, 77.2523, 77.0630 on `C_1111`,
falling as `h^2` toward the same value. The slow test asserts 2e-6 at `h = 2e-3`, an order above the
converged floor and an order below the `h = 4e-3` truncation on the shear.

**With each term switched off** (same harness, same reference):

- **the Fermi-level shift off** (`SternheimerSolver.fermi_level_shift` patched to return the density
  unshifted): `C_1111` reads **35154.9 GPa, +454 times** the reference. The shear is unchanged to every
  printed digit (-221.673311), as it must be, since the shift is proportional to `Tr T`, so `C_1212` is the
  component that shows the rest of the plumbing intact and `C_1111` the one that discriminates.
- **the `wg`/`wk` split off** (the insulator's single block at `wg`, on the shifted response): `C_1111`
  140.205 GPa, **+81 per cent**, and `C_1212` -146.275, **-34 per cent**.

**Integral identities** (`al1.log`): the states' half of the response density integrates to -1.3e-14 on
`(0,0)` and -3.1e-17 on `(0,1)` (it must be zero), and the total `drho` to -6.000000 on `(0,0)` and
`(1,1)` and -3.1e-17 on `(0,1)`, which is `-N Tr T` with `N = 6`. This is the check that the shift sits in
the right place (see below).

**The split form reduces to the insulator exactly** (`si1.log`): `si-electrostriction.in` through the
doubled band block reads `C_1111` 209.3774293964 and `C_1212` 133.9622230503 GPa, 1.5e-13 GPa from the
single block in both. The insulating code path itself is unchanged (the split is taken only for
`occupations != 'fixed'`).

**The negative `C_1212` is the physics of this input, not of the code.** `C_11 < C_12` and `C_44 < 0` say
the cell is mechanically unstable at `a = 7.50`, 4x4x2 k-points and `degauss = 0.05`, far from aluminium.
`pw.x` itself, on strained `al2-metal.in` cells with its sphere rebuilt at each strain
(`notes/incompat/I88-harness/pwfd.py`, `review/incompat/I88/pwfd.log`, serial `qe-7.5/bin/pw.x`, 17 s),
gives a five-point shear curvature of **-226.25 GPa at h = 5e-3 and -209.62 at 1e-2**, so the sign and the
size are `pw.x`'s too. Its `C_1111` (175.4 and 157.5 at those steps) does not converge in `h`, because a
volume-changing strain moves plane waves across the cutoff at `ecutwfc = 15`; this is the basis jump the
frozen-sphere comparison is built to remove, and the reason that comparison, not `pw.x`, is the check.
None of these `pw.x` runs is committed as a reference; it is a sign check.

**What was missing, as found in the code.** The entry named "the Fermi-level shift and the `wg`/`2 wk`
split carried onto the strain coordinate". Both were right, and two things about them were not in it:

1. *Where the shift sits.* `ef_shift` must act on the **Sternheimer half** of the density only, before
   `frozen_drho` (the `1/Omega` of the strained cell at frozen states) is added. At frozen coefficients a
   strained cell holds N electrons for every strain, so `frozen_drho` integrates to `-N Tr T` over the
   undeformed cell and needs no correction; shifting the sum would impose the wrong count by
   `N Tr T / N(e_F)`. It is the phonon loop's order, where `drhous` is added after the shift.
   `ef_shift_wfc` is applied once after the loop to `dpsi`, as `screening_loop` does it.
2. *The split is not the phonon's two-way split.* `_force_constants` can contract the states' tangent
   against a `wk`-weighted functional because its density is an independent argument. The elastic
   constants let the functional build its own density from `(strain, states)`, which is what makes the
   gradient the stress (the factor-three trap of P26), so a states-only `jvp` at `wk` would evaluate
   Hartree and exchange-correlation at a primal density built with `wk`. The split is written on the
   density matrix instead, `P(s) = sum (wg - wk)|psi><psi| + sum wk |s><s|`, two copies of the band block
   (`response/elastic.py:_split_weights`): at `s = psi` it is the ground state at every strain, and its
   tangent `ds = dpsi` is de Gironcoli's `dP = sum wk (|dpsi><psi| + h.c.)`, which reaches the band
   energies, the density and the density's `1/Omega` together. The cost is the band block twice in the
   elastic `jvp` for a metal; on `al2-metal` (7 bands, 32 k-points) the assembly took 19 s on one core.

> They also need `nspin = 1`, an insulator and the full k-mesh (`nosym`), so a spin-polarized, noncollinear
> or spin-orbit run, a metal and a symmetry-reduced k-set each give an error.

with

> They also need `nspin = 1` and the full k-mesh (`nosym`), so a spin-polarized, noncollinear or spin-orbit
> run and a symmetry-reduced k-set each give an error. A metal works if it uses a smearing. Tetrahedra give

## Fix round (2026-10-19)

**What was refuted.** The verifier (logs in D22 `review/incompat/I88/ver2/`) found the guard admitted every
smearing while the identity had been measured on Marzari-Vanderbilt only, and on the test's own cell with
the smearing line changed it failed, flat in `h`: Methfessel-Paxton `degauss = 0.04` (`v3`) read -4.61e-4 on
`C_1111`, and a Gaussian `0.03` cell at `celldm = 7.60` on a 3x3x2 grid (`v1`) read +8.7e-5, with a
major-symmetry residue of 0.042 GPa, so the analytic tensor was not a Hessian there. The cause was isolated
to `sternheimer._SMALL = 6.96e-5`, QE's `setup_nbnd_occ` value, which sets `Smearing.cutoff` and through it
the band set the `alpha_pv` projector and the level-shift correction run over: the Gaussian and
Methfessel-Paxton tails still have weight past it, the Marzari-Vanderbilt one does not, and `ph.x` makes the
same cut, so the phonon cannot see it. Second, Fermi-Dirac `degauss = 0.05` at `nbnd = 7` (`v7`), with 1.2e-2
in band 7, was admitted with no check and did not return in 31 minutes. Third, two minor points: the walked
metal's message and the proposed Limits sentence said a whole-mesh chunk runs, which is false on an
accelerator in memory mode, where `_streams` walks whatever the chunk; and the 2e-6 tolerance was measured on
two components only.

**What changed.** `Smearing` has a field `small` (QE's value by default) and `smearing_of(..., small=)`
forwards it; the strain response builds its solver at `STRAIN_SMEARING_SMALL = 1e-12`
(`response/strain.py`), while the phonon, the residual solver and everything else keep QE's value, so the
phonon against `ph.x` cannot move. A new guard, `_require_an_empty_top_band`, refuses a metal whose highest
computed band holds an occupation above 6.96e-5 at any k-point (where `ph.x` prints "Possibly too few
bands"), with `v7`'s numbers and "Raise nbnd" in its message. `_NO_WALKED_METAL` now says that on an
accelerator only `memory_mode = 'speed'` runs a metal. The slow test gains an `al2-metal-mp` parametrization
(`tests/data/qe/al2-metal-mp.in`, the verifier's `v3`), and `METAL_ELASTIC_TOLERANCE` becomes per case with
its comment saying which components it was measured on.

With the new cutoff every error grows by about 16 from `h = 2e-3` to 4e-3, the five-point stencil's `h^4`,
so what is left is the reference's own truncation (and a floor near 1e-7 where it is smaller); with it off
the error is flat in `h`, which is the systematic the verifier found, reproduced to the digit on `v3` and
`v1`. The major-symmetry residue on `v1` goes from 0.0419 GPa (off) to 7.9e-7 GPa (new). The second
convergence parameter is the cutoff itself: at 1e-8 every printed digit on `v1`, `v3` and `al2-metal` equals
the 1e-12 value, and on `v7c` the two differ by 3e-8 relative, so 1e-12 is converged. `al2-metal` does not
move at all between the three cutoffs (77.315370, -221.673311, -154.331265 GPa), as the verifier's
reading predicted, so the first round's numbers stand.

The Fermi-Dirac cell needed `nbnd = 16` for its highest band to empty (1.7e-11 there); at `nbnd = 7` the new
guard refuses it before any solve (`guard-v7.log`, 5 s: "band 7 holds an occupation of 1.214e-02"). Fermi-Dirac
is therefore measured, not refused, once the bands are enough, and the case that hung is refused by name.
