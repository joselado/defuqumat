# I77. Ultracell x `mixing_mode = 'robust'` and `'ldos'`: partial (`'ldos'` done, `'robust'` still refused)

**The number.** The entry's check is an identity: the ultracell fixed point does not depend on the
mixer. Measured on D22 (`w-inc-I77-v2` at `507157a`, harness `review/incompat/I77/harness2.py`, log
`harness2.log`, one sample each; `harness1.log` at `ea2ae6a` reads the same to every digit) on a
modulated metallic hydrogen ultracell (`a = 5.5` bohr, `ecutwfc = 15`, Gaussian `degauss = 0.02`,
shape `(2, 1, 1)` on `k0 = (2, 2, 2)`, `nbnd = 8`, an applied `0.05 cos` Ry over the box, `conv_thr =
1e-11`, `states_conv_thr = 1e-10`), `mixing_mode = 'ldos'` against `'anderson'` with `kerker=True`:

| cell | density, max abs difference | as a share of the induced density | total energy | iterations (Kerker, LDOS) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `nspin = 1` | 1.19e-7 | 1.9e-5 | 3.4e-13 Ry | 13, 13 |
| `nspin = 2` (moment 0.62 on the unit cell) | 3.47e-7 | 6.2e-5 | 2.1e-12 Ry | 8, 11 |

The LDOS is live and normalised: `integral D dV` over the box was 27.528 states/Ry at the last mix
against `sum w_k0 delta(e_F - e_j)` = 27.542 over the ultracell's converged levels (8.43333 against
8.43347 polarized; the record is one iteration behind the levels), against `N` times the unmodulated
unit cell's 28.25 and 7.64. On the unpolarized cell the first mix saw only 0.570 states/Ry, the second 18.98 and the third 26.54, while the polarized cell read 9.89 from the first mix. Two readings fit: at iteration 1 the levels are the tiled ground state's under the applied 0.05 Ry, and on a `2x2x2` `k0` mesh with a 0.02 Ry Gaussian the Fermi level can sit between discrete levels where `D` is small (the polarized spectrum is denser), or something at iteration 1 alone is offset. `UltracellResult` does not keep the iteration-1 levels, so I did not separate the two within the budget; the fixed point does not see it either way, the two mixers agreeing to 1.2e-7. The clamp to `D >= 0` removed nothing (`clamped = 0` at every mix), and the
inner conjugate gradients took 2 to 8 iterations a mix, never near `LDOS_MAXITER = 200`.

The slow test asserting this is `tests/regression/test_ultracell.py::
test_the_ldos_mode_reaches_the_kerker_fixed_point` (both `polarized` cases): density within 1.5e-6,
energy within 1e-11 Ry, the box integral within 2e-3 of the Gaussian sum. The fast tests are
`tests/unit/test_ultracell_mixing_modes.py`: on a uniform `D` the box LDOS step is `box_kerker` at
`q_TF^2 = 8 pi D` to 3e-16 (`test_a_uniform_ldos_is_kerker_at_eight_pi_d`, `nspin` 1 and 2), a modulated
`D` is not Kerker and keeps the electron count (`test_a_modulated_ldos_is_not_kerker`), and `D = 0` is
the plain `beta R` exactly. The uniform identity holds only on a residual with its Nyquist planes
removed: on an even box the plane `n/2` puts `G` and `-G` on one point where the box's `|G + Q|^2` is not
symmetric, and the two operators then read 1.4e-4 relative apart; in production a uniform `D` therefore
costs one CG iteration, not zero. Neither treatment of that plane is physics, and the fixed point does not
see it. D22 unit run (`unit2.log`): 62 passed in `test_ultracell_mixing_modes.py`, `test_ldos_mixing.py`,
`test_ultracell_guards.py`, `test_mixing.py`, `-m 'not slow'`.

**What was missing, and where the entry was wrong.** For `'ldos'` the entry was right: the mixer was
built in the ultracell driver and never handed an LDOS. Added: `ultracell/mixing.py:box_ldos`, which is
`scf/mixing.py:ldos_preconditioner` itself with the whole box as its G-set (`_WholeBox`, `|G + Q|^2` of
`Ultracell.g2`) and the box volume `N Omega`, and `ultracell/driver.py:_box_ldos`, the box density with
`w_k0 delta_sigma(e_F - e_j) / N` (a Gaussian of width `max(degauss, LDOS_SIGMA_MIN)`, each channel at its
own level when the moment is constrained, summed over spin) in place of the occupations, handed to the
preconditioner before every mix. `UltracellResult.ldos_record` carries `(states, clamped, CG iterations)`
per mix. Under `'ldos'`, `kerker` is not read (the LDOS solve starts from and is preconditioned by Kerker
at the box-averaged `D`); nothing warns about it, which the integrator may want to change. Without
smearing the mode warns and every step is the plain `beta R`. The cost is a second pass over the states
each iteration, the same as the density's, since the box streams its states and cannot share the
transforms the way the unit cell's `density_and_ldos` does; the wall times above (0.9 to 2.0 s a run)
include compilation and are not a timing.

For `'robust'` the entry's missing term is not what is missing. The supervisor (`scf/robust.py:Supervisor`)
is decision logic that holds no arrays, and its merit function, Herbst and Levitt's `I(rho_in) =
E_KS[P_out]`, which `run_scf` assembles separately (`_robust_merit`), is already what the ultracell loop
computes every iteration (`ultracell_energy(new, potential, potential_out, ...)`, every term at the output
density). So the safeguard (keep a trial or halve the step against the nonmonotone window), the restart
rung and the LDOS rung are wirable onto this loop. What is not is the bottom rung, `HamiltonianDamping`,
which mixes the Hamiltonian rather than the density and has no box analogue here, and the unit cell's
setup-time preconditioner choice from the vacuum fraction. Not attempted inside this item's budget; the
refusal stays, its test is `tests/unit/test_ultracell_mixing_modes.py::test_robust_is_still_refused`.

**What stays refused**, each with its test in `tests/unit/test_ultracell_mixing_modes.py`:
`'robust'` (`test_robust_is_still_refused`); `'ldos'` on an ultrasoft or PAW dataset, where the LDOS
would miss the augmentation charge at `e_F` (`test_ldos_on_an_augmented_ultracell_is_refused`, both
kinds); `'ldos'` on a spinor ultracell, unmeasured (`test_ldos_on_a_spinor_ultracell_is_refused`); and a
pointwise mixer under `kerker=True` (`test_a_pointwise_mixer_under_kerker_is_still_refused`). The old
assertion that `run_ultracell(..., mixing_mode='ldos')` raises was removed from
`tests/unit/test_ldos_mixing.py::test_places_without_an_ldos_refuse_the_mode`; its response-loop and
Newton-Krylov warm-up refusals still fire there.

**User guide**, `docs/guide/ultracell.md`, "Limits" note, the paragraph on ultrasoft and PAW. Replace

> The mixers `mixing_mode='robust'` and `mixing_mode='ldos'` give an error, and a mixer that takes no
> preconditioner needs `kerker=False`.

with

> `mixing_mode='ldos'` works with smearing and a norm-conserving dataset. It gives an error with an
> ultrasoft or PAW dataset and with spin-orbit or noncollinear spin. `mixing_mode='robust'` gives an
> error. A mixer that takes no preconditioner needs `kerker=False`.

**Slow tests for the user to run**: `tests/regression/test_ultracell.py::

`'anderson'` path, which is unchanged apart from the guard order.

**Question for the user** (kept conservative, not decided): whether `'robust'` on the ultracell is worth
wiring as safeguard plus restart and LDOS rungs without the Hamiltonian-mixing bottom rung, or should stay
refused until that rung has a box form.
