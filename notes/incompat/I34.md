# I34. Ultracell x `nosource`

Status: done. A `nosource` unit cell runs on the ultracell, in every spin regime the ultracell
takes, and converges to the `nosource` supercell; the check can tell the box projection from a
projection that misses the modulation.

## What was there before this unit

The guard was already gone on master, removed by `ec98a1f` of the previous batch, which made
the ultracell potential `v_of_rho` on the box's own reciprocal vectors, so that the
`nosource` projection `(G+Q)((G+Q).B)/|G+Q|^2` is taken over the whole box, as the supercell
takes it. The previous batch measured it once (`review/incompat/ultracell/r4-nosource.log`,
passed) but wrote no record, and the night record lists I34 as "not addressed". So the
entry's missing term was wrong in its premise: "the `1/|G|^2` projection couples all cells, so
the potential is not per cell" is true, and it is no obstacle, because the box potential was
never per cell; the per-cell potential the refusal named was Elk's `potxcu`, not this code's
(`defuqumat/ultracell/potential.py` module docstring).

## The number

`tests/regression/test_ultracell_regimes.py::test_a_source_free_ultracell_converges_to_the_supercell`,
a noncollinear `nosource` hydrogen lattice (`H.pz-vbc.UPF`, moment started along `(1,1,1)`,
turned onto `x` by `nosource`), a 2x1x1 ultracell on `(2, 2, 2)` modulated along `x`,
against the explicit 2x1x1 `nosource` supercell at `conv_thr = 1e-13`. The four density
components are compared Fourier component by Fourier component, the charge relative to its
largest component and the three magnetization components relative to the largest of the
three. D22, `r1-nosource.log`, commit 126d6ec (the code path is unchanged in 18cc163), single
run:

| arm | nbnd | charge | m_x | m_y | m_z |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| box projection | 8 | 1.021e-4 | 2.014e-4 | 1.984e-4 | 1.837e-4 |
| box projection | 16 | 7.133e-5 | 1.467e-4 | 1.737e-4 | 1.595e-4 |
| A/B: projection dropped at every `Q != 0` | 16 | 1.087e-3 | 2.513e-1 | 3.603e-4 | 3.342e-4 |

The tiled null: `dV` 2.2e-16, density 6.3e-7, energy +2.4e-9 Ry.

**The A/B is what makes the pass evidence.** The arm patches the box potential in the harness
(`review/incompat/ultracell2/h34.py`) so that the magnetic components at every odd box index
along `x`, the `Q != 0` half, come from the unprojected `v_of_rho` while `Q = 0` keeps the
projection, so the null still holds. That is roughly what a per-cell projection does to the
long-wavelength part of the field. It moves `m_x` from 1.5e-4 to 2.5e-1, 1700 times the box's
error, and the field at `Q != 0` changes by up to 11.2 against a largest `Q != 0` component of
3.6 (in the transform's units). So a code missing the term would have failed this check by
three orders of magnitude.

The `m_y`, `m_z` columns sit at an absolute 2.1e-7 to 2.6e-7, falling slowly with `nbnd`; they
are the components the projection holds near zero (3e-6 at their largest), which is why the
test normalises the three magnetization components by the largest of them.

## Refusals that still fire

None of its own: `nosource` is accepted at `nspin_mag = 4` with LDA
(`test_ultracell_regimes.py::test_what_the_box_still_refuses`, last line). A `nosource` cell
with a GGA is refused by the GGA's own `nspin_mag = 4` refusal (I31), in the same test.
