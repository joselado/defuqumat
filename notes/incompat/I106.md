# I106. Energy derivatives under a field or constrained moment: partial (forces and stress lifted for a uniform `B_field` and the cell-wide `'fsm'` field, each with a finite difference; `'total'`, `'total direction'`, a `reducebf` field and the relaxations take the same term but are still refused for want of a number; every sphere-integrated field or constraint, `dE/dq` and both anisotropy routes still refused)

**The numbers.** The check is the entry's: a central difference of the total *including* the field's
energy, `E + E_field + E_constraint`, over SCFs re-converged from scratch at `conv_thr = 1e-12`, against
the autodiff force or stress at the centre. For the fixed-spin-moment field the differenced quantity is
the Lagrangian `E - B . (m - m0)` (`E + E_field + B . m0`, with `B` the driven field the run ended with),
which cancels the moment's residual against its target to first order. The stress is differenced in the
frozen basis (`Calculation.at_cell`, every Miller index kept), so no Pulay stress enters. All runs on D22,
one core, one sample each, worktree `/l/ladovj1/w-inc-I106` at `9be976e3` (the code is unchanged in the
final commit, only tests and the record moved); harness `notes/incompat/I106-harness/harness.py`, logs
`/l/ladovj1/review/incompat/I106/arm-*.log` and `.json`.

**The stress floor of about 5e-4 Ry is not the field term, and where it is.** It does not fall with the
step from 0.003 down to 0.0005, it does not halve when the field halves (5.5e-4 at 0.02, 5.0e-4 at 0.01,
while the term goes from 0.256 to 0.125), and the control without a field falls to 7e-6. A diagnostic
(arms `diag`, `diag0`, `diagfsm`) differentiates the *frozen* functional at the converged state two ways,
autodiff through `at_strain` against a central difference of the same `energy_at` on `at_strain` and on
`at_cell` (the two agree to 5e-12, so the tables of `at_cell` are not the cause). The field term agrees
with its own difference to **3e-7** (0.2555586 against 0.2555589 at 5e-4). The *total* does not:
Richardson-extrapolated from 1e-3 and 5e-4 it is 7.2e-4 from autodiff under the field (moment 2.81),
3.0e-4 under `'fsm'` (moment 2.00) and 7e-5 without a field (moment 2.50), which accounts for the SCF
floor. What the data shows is 3e-4 to 7e-4 Ry with a field of either sign and 7e-5 without, the field
term itself at 3e-7: it does not follow the moment (the lowest moment, under `'fsm'`, has four times the
control's mismatch). Term by term, the largest step-to-step change that is not `h^2`-like is in `xc`
(+4.4e-4 under the field, +3.3e-4 under `'fsm'`, +1.8e-4 without). One hypothesis, not tested: the
`zeta` clip of the LSDA (`xc/functional.py:471`, `:879`), the clamp trap of the recurring list, met at
more grid points when a field pushes the spin channels apart. Not chased here (budget); it is a property
of the existing stress functional, since the field term is clean.

*The identity.* `energy_at` at the converged state equals `total_energy + field_energy +
constraint_energy` to 2.0e-13 Ry (Fe, field), 9.2e-14 (Fe, `'fsm'`), 2.2e-16 (H2), so the term is the
reported `etcon`/Zeeman energy and nothing else.

**What was missing, as found in the code.** The entry's term is right ("the field or penalty energy
inside the differentiated functional"), and the code around it decides how much of it was missing:

- `energy_at` built `moved.potential(rho)`, which already computes `e_field` and `e_constraint`, and
  dropped both. Worse, it falls back to `moved.magnetic_field`, the *input's* field, which `reducebf`
  and the fixed-spin-moment controller have changed by the end of a run. The fix: `FrozenState` carries
  `magnetic_field` and `field_scale` (from `SCFResult.magnetic_field` and `.field_scale`, the field the
  last potential was built with, since the loop breaks before the post-convergence decay or feedback
  step), `energy_at` passes them explicitly and adds a `"field"` term, and a field-carrying calculation
  with a state that has no field is refused (`_frozen_field`). The in-loop `tstress` state gets the
  loop's own field.
- **The multipliers were already the right ones.** The orthonormality term uses the eigenvalues as
  Lagrange multipliers, and those include the Zeeman shift; with the field's energy outside the
  functional they belonged to a different functional than the one written down. With the term in, they
  match.
- For `'fsm'` the term is the Lagrange multiplier's `-B . m`, and the force at fixed moment is the same
  partial derivative as the force at fixed field (the Legendre identity, `dE/dtau|_m = dF/dtau|_B`). So
  there is no choice to put to the user between the two, and the fsm arm reads it.
- **The atom-resolved schemes are not lifted, and the reason is narrower than the entry says.** Their
  energy depends on the positions through the sphere weights `w_a(r - tau_a)`, whose linear taper has a
  real derivative, and `scf/locals.py:build_local_regions` builds the weights in NumPy for **both**
  schemes; `Calculation._moved_magnetic_field` then returns the field untouched when the geometry is
  traced. So `dE/dm_a . dm_a/dtau` would be dropped silently. (The `locals.py` module docstring says the
  `'smooth'` scheme has "the positions live"; in the code it does not.) Lifting it needs the taper written
  in JAX with the owner map and the image shift held fixed, plus a decision about the default radii, which
  `_moved_magnetic_field` recomputes from the geometry at every concrete step, so the energy a relaxation
  sees moves with `r_m(tau)` while a fixed-radius derivative does not.
- The guard is narrowed, not removed: `reject_magnetic_field(calculation, quantity)` refuses a
  `LOCAL_MAGNETIC_FIELDS` card and every scheme in `ATOM_RESOLVED`, for the force and (through
  `require_a_differentiable_cell`) for the stress. It also refuses, with a message saying only the
  number is missing, the two cell-wide penalties `'total'` and `'total direction'` (the term is in the
  functional and the argument is the uniform field's, but no arm ran them) and a field faded by
  `reducebf` (the functional reads `FrozenState.field_scale`, the scale the last potential was built
  with; the only test of that path, the slow identity test below, was not run). Lifting each needs one
  more harness arm: the O2 cell with `constrained_magnetization = 'total'`, and the same cell with
  `reducebf = 0.9`.
- `run_relax` and `run_vc_relax` refuse a calculation with any field (`_refuse_a_magnetic_field`, new):
  BFGS is handed `SCFResult.total_energy`, which leaves the field out, so with the force now a derivative
  of `E + E_field` the line search and the trust radius would follow a different function than the
  gradient (consistent only for `'fsm'`, by the Legendre identity). Before this item they were refused
  through `compute_forces`; lifting them needs the objective to carry `field_energy + constraint_energy`
  (the Lagrangian for `'fsm'`) and a relaxation under a field measured. `method='analytic'` is refused under any field, because
  `forces.f90` has no field term and on an augmented dataset it would miss the 8.6e-4 above.
- `forces/spiral.py:_require_a_differentiable_spiral` stays: its three routes (whole axis, chunked, split)
  write their own functionals and none carries the term. Not attempted. The two anisotropy refusals stay:
  they are differences of totals and a force-theorem leg, not frozen derivatives, and need a different
  check (a held direction by `'fsm'` with `E` is the fixed-moment answer; by a penalty it leaves a residual
  that is a convergence artefact in the difference).

**Guide.** `docs/guide/forces.md`, "Limits" note: nothing about fields is there now. Add, as a new
paragraph:

> `get_forces()` and `get_stress()` work under a uniform field (`B_field`) and under the fixed-spin-moment
> field on the whole cell (`constrained_magnetization = 'fsm'`). The energy we differentiate is then the
> total energy plus the energy of the field, because this is the energy the SCF makes stationary. The
> printed total energy does not include the field, as in `pw.x`. The penalties `'total'` and
> `'total direction'` and a field that `reducebf` fades give an error, because we have not checked them.
> A field inside the sphere of one atom (`LOCAL_MAGNETIC_FIELDS`) and every `'atomic'` constraint give an
> error, because the moment in a sphere moves with the atom and this part of the derivative is not
> written. A relaxation under any field gives an error, because the relaxation follows the printed total
> energy, which does not include the field. `get_forces(method="analytic")` gives an error under any
> field, because the expressions of `pw.x` have no term for the field.
