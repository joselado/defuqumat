## What was missing

Not "the spin axis of the second-derivative assembly". The solver keeps
`max(occupied_counts)` bands in every channel (static shapes), so the down channel of O2
carries its two empty pi*_g bands inside the block over which
`S'_mn = <psi_m|dS/du|psi_n>` is built (`phonon.overlap_derivatives`). They then entered:

- `orthogonality_states` as partners `m` of an occupied `n`, adding
  `-1/2 psi_pi* <pi*|dS/du|psi_n>` to a first-order state whose pi* component the
  Sternheimer solve already produced (`P_c` keeps a band that is conduction in its own
  channel); this reaches the assembly through `ort` and through `non_variational_response`;
- `multiplier_response`'s gauge correction `1/2 (eps_n - eps_m) S'_mn w_n` as rows.

QE cannot write either down: `compute_drhous.f90` and `drhodvus.f90` loop over
`nbnd_occ(ikk)` of the spin-resolved k-point. It is the bug `_multiplier_response`'s row
mask closed for the Born charges, in two more places. The fix is
`phonon.occupied_pairs`, which masks `S'` to each channel's occupied pairs in both the
whole-k route (`overlap_derivatives`) and the walked one (`chunked_phonon`'s `mixed`
pass). It is `None` wherever the mask would be all ones (`nspin = 1`, LSDA with equal
fillings) and for a metal, so those paths are bit for bit what they were.

The entry and the guard's docstring were wrong in kind: the docstring argued that the
ultrasoft terms "cannot be what blocks a norm-conserving LSDA run", but the cell it measured
is ultrasoft, and those terms were exactly the cause.

## What stays refused

The walked route at `nspin = 2` (a streamed store, memory mode with a chunk smaller than the
mesh, memory mode on a card), by `phonon._require_one_spin_channel`, now called only there.
It carries the same mask but was not run at `nspin = 2`; what it needs is the walked matrix
against the whole-k one on this cell, no new reference. The strain response's own
`nspin = 2` refusal (`strain._require_one_spin_channel`) still fires, and the Raman tensors
still refuse `nspin = 2` through `require_a_sternheimer_regime`. Tests:
`tests/regression/test_lsda_response.py::test_the_walked_dynamical_matrix_and_the_strain_response_still_refuse_nspin_two`
(front door, no SCF) and
`tests/regression/test_phonons.py::test_a_spin_polarized_calculation_is_refused_only_on_the_walked_route`.
