## What was found

The entry's premise, that no real input reaches the refusal (the docstring of
`test_a_box_that_would_fold_two_members_together_is_refused` says the same), is wrong. The
ultrasoft oxygen chain `tests/data/qe/o-chain-spiral-us.in` with `ecutrho = 240` instead of 200
folds at its own `q = b3/4` and at `q = b3/2`: its resident dense box is (60, 60, 25), and 25 is
exactly the resident span `2*12 + 1` along `c` (an odd FFT size the rounding landed on), while the
laboratory sphere `|G - q| <= G_rho` spans 26. Measured by a host-side scan of `ecutrho` 200, 212,
220, 240 and 250 Ry at `q3` 0.25 and 0.5: 212 and 220 (box 25) hold, 240 folds, 200 (box 24) and 250
(box 64, 64, 27) hold. So the fold is a real input regime, odd boxes only.

The missing term is what the entry says: a box one FFT size larger along the folded axis. It is
implemented as

- `defuqumat/basis/gvectors.py:box_holding_displaced`: the resident grid when the displaced
  sphere holds, else each axis whose span exceeds the box taken to `good_fft_order(span, factor)`;
  the enumeration of the displaced sphere is factored into `_displaced_members`, shared with
  `displaced_gvectors`.
- `defuqumat/basis/builder.py:widen_for_displaced_sphere`: rebuilds the dense set (and the smooth
  one where it is a separate grid) on the widened boxes, asserting that the dense sphere is the
  resident one Miller index for Miller index, and returns the basis object itself when nothing
  folds, so no run that held changes.
- `defuqumat/scf/driver.py:Calculation.__init__`, spiral branch: called before the spiral's plane
  waves are built, under the same condition `_spiral_gvectors` builds the displaced sets on (an
  augmented dataset or a separate smooth grid).

The oxygen chain at 240 Ry is widened to (60, 60, 27); the smooth box (40, 40, 18) already held.

## The energy number (added at integration, 2026-10-22)

The check that was written and not submitted ran as Triton job 21126794 (`batch-csl`, 2026-10-22,
at `02a734db`, outputs in `notes/incompat/I122-harness/out/`): task 0 the fast unit file (7 passed,
1 deselected as slow, 4.6 s), tasks 1 and 2 the harness. `o-chain-spiral-us.in` at `ecutrho = 240`,
`q = b3/4`, the box widened to (60, 60, 27), against the four-cell supercell
(`o-chain-90deg-us.in`, (60, 60, 108)), `electronic energy per cell`:

| `conv_thr` | spiral iterations | supercell iterations | spiral minus supercell / 4 (Ry) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1e-9 | 10 | 14 | 2.77e-10 |
| 1e-11 | 12 | 17 | -3.7e-13 |

The convergence parameter is `conv_thr`, and the difference follows it (2.8e-10 to -3.7e-13 for a
100 times tighter threshold), so what is left is the SCF's own error. For comparison the same identity
on the resident spheres reads 1.6e-9 Ry (the existing sensitivity, `QUARTER_TURN_RY`), which the
widened box beats by 6 times at 1e-9 and by 4000 times at 1e-11.

**`WIDENED_QUARTER_TURN_RY` is 1e-11**, not the 1.1e-12 that three times the 1e-11 value would give:
the slow test runs at `conv_thr = 1e-11` (the inputs say so), and the resident quarter turn read
3.5e-12 on D22, so a bound under that would test the machine. 1e-11 is 27 times the measurement and
160 times under the 1.6e-9 of the wrong sphere, so it still tests the sphere. The user decided the
refusal stays for a box handed in and for `at_spiral_q`.
