# I58. Transverse spin susceptibility (magnons) x applied field or constrained moment: done (uniform field)

**The number.** Larmor's theorem, as the Goldstone identity moved to the Zeeman frequency. On
`tests/data/qe/h-fcc-magnon-field.in` (fcc H, `B_field(3) = 0.01` Ry, `degauss = 0.05`, `nbnd = 30`,
`ecut_response = 12`; D22, `w-inc-magnon-v3`, log `review/incompat/magnon/larmor3-1.log`, one sample),
the relative residual of `X_0(q = 0, omega) B_xc = m` is

| omega (Ry) | residual | lambda_max(X_0 F) |
|---|---|---|
| +2B = +0.02 | **0.00585** | **1.01841** |
| 0 | 0.171 | 0.847 |
| -2B = -0.02 | 0.286 | 0.727 |

so the identity closes at `+2B` only, at the same level as the zero-field cell's residual at zero
frequency (0.00571 on `h-fcc-magnon.in`, `lambda_max` 1.01776, same `nbnd` and cutoff). The two other
candidate frequencies, which a missing field in the states (0) or a wrong sign (-2B) would select, read
30 and 50 times worse. The moment in this cell is the field's doing (0.321 mu_B with it, -6e-5 from the
same seed without it), so the state is not a relabelled zero-field state. Through the workflow
(`Calculator.get_spin_susceptibility`, which now forwards the converged field): `.larmor` 0.02,
`.goldstone` 0.00585, `.enhancement` (at zero frequency) 0.847, `.larmor_enhancement` 1.0184, and the
crossing magnon at 0.0182 Ry, 9 per cent below `2B` because `lambda_max(2B)` exceeds one by the
truncation's 1.8 per cent (`goldstone_correction` puts it at `2B` by construction). The bodies of the
slow tests below were run as plain calls on D22 (`slowbodies-1.log`) and pass.

Two earlier runs are the reason the cell is a metal. At `degauss = 0.01` (the original cell) and
`B = 0.002` and `0.02` (`larmor-1.log`, `larmor-2.log`, `fieldcheck.log`), the moment stayed at
0.53125 = 34/64 to 2e-6: no eigenvalue of the 4x4x4 mesh is within a few `degauss` of the Fermi level, so
the field only shifts the channels rigidly (up -B, down +B, spread 7e-8) and the residual at `2B` came
out **identical** to the zero-field one (0.0057079), which checks the sign and nothing else.

**What was missing, and where the entry was wrong.** The entry said "the Zeeman gap; the kernel
`B_xc/m` rests on global spin-rotation invariance, which the field breaks". The kernel was never wrong:
`spinkernel.exchange_field` rebuilds `v_of_rho` without the field, so `F = B_xc/m` is the rotation kernel
of `E_xc` alone, and the field is a fixed external term that does not respond. The Zeeman gap is carried by
the states. What was missing was plumbing and a frequency: `run_spin_susceptibility` and
`run_magnon_dispersion` took no `field`/`field_scale`, so `fixed_density_states` refused, and the
identity was read at `omega = 0`, where under a field it does not hold. With `B_tot = B_xc - B` (the field
enters as `-B`, `E = -int B.m`), the proof gives `X_0(0, omega) B_xc = m - X_0 (omega/2 - B) u`, so it
closes at `omega = 2B` (`-2B` with `flip`). Now: `spinchi0.larmor_frequency`; the workflow adds an
unbroadened Larmor point beside the zero-frequency one, reads `.goldstone` there, keeps `.enhancement` at
zero frequency (a gapped mode reads below one, an antiparallel moment above), reports
`.larmor`/`.larmor_enhancement`, and `goldstone_correction` scales by `1/lambda_max(2B)`.

**What stays refused**, each with a test in `tests/unit/test_magnon_regimes.py`: a constrained moment
(`test_a_constrained_moment_is_refused`, `fe-fsm.in`; a penalty or feedback field changes the functional
the state is stationary for), a `LOCAL_MAGNETIC_FIELDS` card (`test_a_per_atom_field_is_refused`; a field
in spheres has structure in G and no single Larmor frequency), and a field on a spinor run (I59's guard).
`test_a_uniform_field_is_accepted` fires the lifted branch.

**User guide**, `docs/guide/magnons.md`, "Limits" note, fourth paragraph. Replace

> A magnetic field or a constrained moment gives an error, because a field
> breaks the rotation symmetry that Goldstone's theorem rests on, and the magnon in a field has a Zeeman gap that we do
> not include.

with

> A uniform `B_field` works. The magnon at $\mathbf q = 0$ then sits at the Zeeman energy $2B$, not at
> zero, and `.goldstone` is read there. `.larmor` gives this energy. A constrained moment gives an error,
> because it changes the functional. A `LOCAL_MAGNETIC_FIELDS` card gives an error, because a field
> inside spheres has no single Zeeman energy.
