# I45. Phonon at q != 0 x symmetry-reduced k-set (small group of q)

**Status: blocked. The refusal stands; no number was measured.**

## What is missing, as found

The guard (`response/phononq.py:require_a_two_sphere_regime`) fires on
`calculation.use_symmetry`, so every symmetric run is refused, not only a reduced
k-set. The entry names "symmetrisation over the small group of `q`". Reading the loop
shows that this is three pieces, of which none is in the code:

1. **The k-set of the small group of `q`.** `ph.x` re-reduces the grid with the
   operations `S q = q + G` and builds `k + q` for each point of that wedge. Here the
   two-sphere loop takes the run's own k-set; the run's wedge (the crystal's group) is the
   wrong one at any `q` off `Gamma`.
2. **The response density symmetrised inside the loop**, `psymdvscf` at `q`: a Bloch
   field `exp(iq.r) d rho(r)` rotated with the phase `exp(i q.(S r + t - r))`, and the
   `3 nat` displacement patterns mixed by the atom mapping, every pass of the
   self-consistent loop. The `q = 0` version exists
   (`Calculation.symmetrize_atom_displacement`); the phase and the restriction to the
   small group do not.
3. **`symdynph_gq`** at `q`: the dynamical matrix symmetrised over the small group with
   the phases `exp(i q.(R_a - R_{S a}))`. `symmetrize_atom_pair_tensor` is the
   `q = 0` case only.

Each of these is a transcription from `PHonon/PH/` with its own check, and together
they are well past the 90-minute budget of this unit. The completion of an assembled
tensor that lifted I40 to I42 does not apply, because here the sum is inside a
self-consistent loop.
