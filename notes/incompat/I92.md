# I92. Shift current and SHG x DFT+U: done (norm-conserving, collinear)

**The number.** Two checks, both on `tests/data/qe/alas-ldau.in` (new: `alas-raman.in` with
`HUBBARD {atomic}`, `U As-4p 3.0`, the one norm-conserving, gapped, fixed-occupation DFT+U cell in
the suite). D22, CPU, one core, one sample each (deterministic arithmetic, no timing), worktree
`/l/ladovj1/w-inc-I92`, logs `/l/ladovj1/review/incompat/I92/{operator,identity}.log`.

1. **The operator identity the entry names**, `<m|dH/dk_a|n>` and `<m|d2H/dk_a dk_b|n>` by `jvp`
   against a central difference on the frozen sphere (the first of `H(k)` with `vhpsi` in it, the
   second of the `jvp` first derivative), at the generic point `[0.11, 0.23, 0.31]`, six states
   with weight on the Hubbard orbitals, the starting potential and an `ns` that is the starting
   one plus a fixed Hermitian part (the identity holds for any of them, so no SCF). Convergence
   parameter: the step `h`, 4e-3, 2e-3, 1e-3 1/bohr. max|jvp - difference|:

   | kind | first derivative | second derivative | Hubbard term (first, second) |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | atomic | 6.72e-6, 1.68e-6, 4.20e-7 | 7.34e-5, 1.83e-5, 4.59e-6 | 8.54e-2, 4.05e-1 |
   | ortho-atomic | 4.43e-6, 1.11e-6, 2.77e-7 | 2.39e-5, 5.98e-6, 1.49e-6 | 1.02e-2, 2.47e-1 |
   | norm-atomic | 9.88e-6, 2.47e-6, 6.17e-7 | 1.80e-4, 4.50e-5, 1.13e-5 | 7.88e-2, 4.31e-1 |

   Every column falls by 4.00 per halving of `h` (`h^2`), to three figures. **With the term off**
   (the `jvp` of the same cell without its `U`, same states, against the same difference) the
   comparison reads the whole Hubbard term: 8.54e-2 and 4.05e-1 for atomic, and so on, against
   4.2e-7 and 4.6e-6 with it. The Löwdin `O^{-1/2}` of `ortho-atomic` has a custom first-order
   rule and its second derivative goes through `eigh`'s own; at the generic point it converges
   like the rest. Scale of the elements: 0.51 (first) and 2.4 to 2.6 (second) Ry bohr^n.

2. **The shift current's central object**, `r^{c;a}_nm` from the DFT+U `v` and `w` through the
   sum rule, against a parallel-transport difference of the dipole of the dense DFT+U `H`, the
   construction of `test_the_sum_rule_is_an_identity_over_a_complete_basis` (`a = x`, `c = y`,
   the 8x8 low block, entries above 1e-2 of its largest). Convergence parameters: the band count
   (20, 80, 120 and the complete 158) and the difference step `delta`:

   | delta | 20 | 80 | 120 | 158 (complete) | 158 with the Hubbard `w` off |
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|
   | 2e-3 | 7.60e-2 | 5.60e-2 | 5.31e-2 | 6.53e-3 | 4.58e-2 |
   | 1e-3 | 7.59e-2 | 5.58e-2 | 5.30e-2 | **1.63e-3** | 4.37e-2 |

   At completeness the residue falls by 4.0 as `delta` halves, so it is the difference's own
   `O(delta^2)` and the identity holds; the truncated values do not move with `delta`. With the
   Hubbard part of `w` removed (`v`, energies and states keep it) the identity fails at
   completeness by 4.37e-2, 27 times the floor: that is what a missing Hubbard second velocity
   would have done to the shift current's integrand, about the size of the band truncation at
   120. (Plain AlAs reads 2.6e-3 here at `delta = 1e-3`, in that test's docstring.)

**End to end** (`tensors2.log`, `w-inc-I92-v2`), through `Calculator.get_shift_current` and
`get_shg`, which forward `SCFResult.ns` by name: SCF at `conv_thr = 1e-10`, whole 4x4x4 grid,
`nbnd = 14`. Shift current (`broadening = 0.02`, 90 points to 0.9 Ry): peak `sigma^xyz`
2.70e-5 A/V^2 with `U` against 2.96e-5 without; forbidden components 3.1e-14, spread of the six
allowed 7.3e-15 (`-43m` to the zone sum's round-off). SHG (60 points to 0.6 Ry): peak 1128 pm/V
with `U` against 1253 without, forbidden 2.1e-6, spread 1.5e-6. The `U` moves the spectra by
2.4e-5 A/V^2 and 1373 pm/V at their largest (the edge moves). Truncation (`nbnd` 14 against 11):
4.8e-2 and 5.1e-2. This is not a reference number; it is that the assemblies take `ns` where a
user's call sends it and keep the crystal's form.

**The k-chunk walk with `ns` is not measured, and the attempt is a null worth recording**
(`chunks.log`, third D22 run). `get_shift_current`/`get_shg` at `k_batch = "default"`, `None` and
5 gave bit-identical tensors (difference 0.0), which is not evidence of chunk independence:
through `Calculator` the workflow is handed the calculator's `Calculation`, and
`workflows/nscf.py:threaded_calculation` returns that calculation's own `k_batch`, so the
caller's `k_batch` is dropped and all three runs walked the same chunks. (A side finding, not
this item's: the `k_batch` option of these two `get_*` methods is silently ignored.) What argues
for the walk: the operator a chunk builds rebuilds `wfcU` from its own rows' `basis_kpoints` in
`at_kcart`, `ns` is the same small array in every chunk, and the stages are traced per call by
`eager.compiled_function`, which keeps a program by its jaxpr and passes closed-over arrays
(`ns`, like `terms` and `v_scf` before it) as inputs, so a later call with another `ns` does not
reuse a stale constant. The end-to-end run above walked one k-point per chunk (the CPU default)
and came out `-43m` to 3e-14, which a chunk reading the wrong rows' `wfcU` would break. The
direct comparison is `run_shift_current(..., k_batch=None)` against the default with no
`calculation=`, about 30 s; it was not run, being past this item's three D22 runs.

**Trap check** (the skill's list against `6c5b3a36..incompat/I92`): clean. No `abs` or band
diagonal in a differentiated path (the `abs` are in the tests' comparisons); no `KPoints`
constructor in package code (the tests' meshes cross `for_spin` in the workflows); `ns` crosses
as `jnp.asarray` in `VelocityOperator`, no `np.asarray` on a traced value; no dtype literal; no
hand `vmap` over k (the walk is `walk`/`sum_k` as before); the differences in the tests are on a
frozen sphere at a generic point, so no stencil rebuilds a sphere or holds its centre; the two
refusals kept were re-derived for this assembly (the augmented clause is reached only by SHG,
the noncollinear one by both), not inherited.

**What was missing**, as found in the code, and where the entry was wrong. The entry (and the
refusal) said the Hubbard velocity and second velocity were missing. They were not:
`VelocityOperator._operator` builds `at_kcart(kcart)`, which rebuilds `wfcU` from the traced `k`
(`Calculation.at_kcart`), and puts `hubbard_terms(ns)` into the Hamiltonian it differentiates, so
both `matrix_elements` and `second_matrix_elements` carried the term already, and `wfcU` is among
the row fields a k-chunk slices (`forces/chunked.py:ROW_FIELDS`). What was missing was the `ns`
argument: on `shift_current` and `second_harmonic` (each built its operators without one, so a
lifted guard would have met `VelocityOperator`'s `ValueError`), on `run_shift_current` and
`run_shg` (now passed to the fixed-density solve and on to the assembly), and a measurement.
`Calculator` needed no edit, since `_call_options` forwards `ns` once the parameter exists.

**What stays refused**, each with a test that fires it:

- DFT+U on a **noncollinear** run (spinor `wfcU` from the averaged `j` channels, not covered by
  the check): `tests/unit/test_photocurrent_machinery.py::test_each_refusal_names_its_own_missing_term`
  (`hubbard + noncolin`).
- DFT+U beside an **ultrasoft or PAW** dataset, reached only by the second-harmonic tensor (the
  shift current's dataset clause fires first): `wfcU = S phi` puts `dS/dk` into the projector and
  no cell checked it. `::test_an_augmented_dft_plus_u_second_harmonic_is_still_refused`, with the
  same dataset and no `U` passing beside it.
- The control that the lift is real: `::test_a_norm_conserving_collinear_dft_plus_u_run_is_not_refused`.
- The spiral, the metal and the wedge refusals are unchanged and their tests still pass (fast
  set on D22: 60 passed, `fast.log`).

**The question for the user**, kept conservative: whether to measure the augmented case (a
Hubbard ultrasoft insulator would be a new cell, and the operator identity above plus the SHG
path's generalised velocity is the check) and the noncollinear case (the same identity on a
spinor DFT+U cell, for example `alas-ldau.in` with `noncolin`). Neither was attempted.

**Found on the way.** `require_a_conductivity_regime` never refused DFT+U and `run_conductivity`
already passed `ns`, so the optical conductivity has run the Hubbard velocity with no check. The
first-derivative identity above is that check too (for the norm-conserving collinear case).
