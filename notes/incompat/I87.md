**The convergence parameter is the step `h` of the stencil**, and the reference is converged
over the whole range used. The norm-conserving control shows the `h^4` truncation of the
five-point rule falling into that floor: 1.42e-5, 6.2e-7, -5.1e-8 on `C_1111` at `h = 4e-2`,
`2e-2`, `1e-2` (a factor 23 for one halving, against 16 for `h^4`), then flat at about 1e-7 for
`h <= 8e-3`. On the two augmented datasets the coarse steps do **not** fall smoothly: the full
form reads 8.8e-4, 4.1e-3, -1.08e-3 on `C_1111` at `h = 4e-2, 2e-2, 1e-2` (ultrasoft; PAW

amounts (-0.2199, -0.2174, -0.2214), so this is the reference `E(h)` and not the derivative.
`E(h)` of these two datasets is not smooth once the stencil reaches strains of about 2e-2; the
cause was not found (the stored route evaluates `Q_ij(G)` directly, so the `dq = 0.01` table is
not it). For `h <= 8e-3` (strains up to 1.6e-2) the three rungs agree to 1e-7 and that is what
the tolerance is set from. The slow test uses `h = 4e-3`.

**What was missing, as found in the code.** The entry was right about both terms. One detail it
had wrong: `StrainResponse.ort` was not "written, not wired" for nothing, it was already used
inside the strain response's frozen density response, and only `elastic_constants` did not read
it. The second term was absent in the form the entry gave, a strain-coordinate
`multiplier_response`; the strain response also dropped the converged PAW one-centre response
on return, which that function needs. Now:

- `strain.multiplier_response` (`defuqumat/response/strain.py`):
  `dLambda_mn = w_n [<psi_m|dH|psi_n> - 1/2 (eps_m + eps_n) S'_mn]`, with `dH` the bare strain
  perturbation plus the converged induced potential and the converged one-centre response, the
  phonon's expression and its `+1/2 (eps_n - eps_m) S'_mn w_n` correction verbatim. Stored as
  `StrainResponse.multipliers` on the whole-k route; `_self_consistent_response` returns the
  converged `onecentre` for it.
- `elastic_constants`: for an augmented dataset the tangent is `(T_kl, dpsi + ort, dLambda)` and
  the energy takes `multipliers=` (the matrix constraint, primal `diag(w eps)`), so one `jvp` of
  the stress carries both terms; the norm-conserving path keeps its two-argument form (it reads
  `C_11 = 209.377` GPa, the committed 209.38; a bit-level comparison against master was not run).
- `require_a_measured_elastic_regime(calculation, streamed=, response=)` now refuses only the
  walked route (`_walked_elastic_constants`, whose chunked tangent has the diagonal constraint
  and `dpsi` alone, and whose strain response writes no `dLambda`) and a handed-in response
  without `ort` or `multipliers`. `electrostriction` checks it after the route decision and before
  the two responses, so the refusal still costs nothing.

The `deigenvalues` of a PAW strain response are built without the one-centre response
(`_eigenvalue_response` calls `solver.perturbation(dvscf)` with no `dddd_paw`). That is a
diagnostic with no consumer and was left as it is; it is a candidate for the integrator's list.
