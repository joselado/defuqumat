# I103. Berry-phase finite field (`lelfield`) x ultrasoft or PAW: done at `nspin = 1`

**The number.** A direct `pw.x` reference, and it needed a second direction because `pw.x` is
wrong along the first. The cell is `si8-lelfield.in`'s Si8 on `Si.pbe-n-rrkjus_psl.0.1`
(`tests/data/qe/si8-us-lelfield-y.in`) and `Si.pbe-n-kjpaw_psl.0.1`
(`si8-paw-lelfield-y.in`), `ecutwfc = 20`, `ecutrho = 160`, two strings of `nppstr = 5`, the field
along y, `nberrycyc = 3`, `conv_thr = 1e-11`, run as the `pw_electric` chain (plain SCF, the
operator at `efield = 0`, then 0.001, then 0.002, each from the state before it) by serial `pw.x`
7.5 on D22 (harness `review/incompat/I103/qe_us.py`, `qe-2.log`, 42 s). This code on the same
chain (`dq_us.py`, `w-inc-I103-v2`, `dq-usy-2.log` and `dq-pawy-2.log`, D22, one core, one sample
each, the SCF being deterministic):

| | this code | `pw.x` | difference |
|---|---|---|---|
| US, total at E = 0 | -91.08455053846 | -91.08455053 | 8.5e-9 Ry |
| US, total at E = 0.001 | -91.20021423472 | -91.20021423 | 4.7e-9 Ry |
| US, `el_pol` at 0.001 | 0.9809032342 | 0.9809049607 | 1.7e-6 |
| US, total at 0.002 | -91.31686195884 | -91.31686195 | 8.8e-9 Ry |
| US, `el_pol` at 0.002 | 1.9693451668 | 1.9693530037 | 7.8e-6 |
| PAW, total at E = 0 | -373.55268003990 | -373.55268003 | 1.0e-8 Ry |
| PAW, total at 0.001 | -373.66834337750 | -373.66834337 | 7.5e-9 Ry |
| PAW, `el_pol` at 0.001 | 0.9801717842 | 0.9801783473 | 6.6e-6 |

`pw.x` prints the total to 8 decimals, and its own `el_pol` moves by 4.7e-6 between its last two
iterations (0.98090029 to 0.98090496), so every difference is at the size of the reference. The
forces (autodiff route, `forces_us_efield` carried by the gradient) are within 5.7e-8 Ry/bohr (US)
and 8.9e-8 (PAW) of `pw.x`'s, on forces of 0.012 to 0.014. The frozen-energy identity (the force
functional with the Berry enthalpy, evaluated at the converged state, against the SCF total)
reads 1.3e-9 Ry (US) and 9.6e-10 (PAW).

**The convergence parameter is `conv_thr`.** The same field step at `conv_thr = 1e-8` is 2.3e-7 Ry
in the total and 4.6e-4 in `el_pol` from `pw.x` (`loose` in `dq-usy-2.log`), against 4.7e-9 and
1.7e-6 at 1e-11: the energy falls with the square of the state error and the dipole with the
error, as a variational quantity and a first-order one should. The test tolerances (3e-8 Ry,
3e-5 in `el_pol`, 1e-6 Ry/bohr) are the file's printed precision and the NC regression file's,
taken from the 1e-11 numbers.

**The control.** With `q_ij(+-dk)` switched off (`BerryFieldSetup.cross_off`: no cross term in
`M` or in the duals, `S u` kept), `el_pol` is **1.1144460619**, 0.134 (13.6 per cent) from `pw.x`,
and the total 6.7e-5 Ry below it. So the agreement would have read differently without the term.
The forces do not discriminate at this level: `pw.x`'s z run below, whose cross term is wrong, has
forces within 1.1e-7 of this code's right ones, so the field's force through the cross term is
below 1e-7 on this cell, and the force agreement is evidence for the ionic and Hellmann-Feynman
parts, not for `forces_us_efield`.

**`pw.x` 7.5 is wrong along z, and the z run is how it was found.** The first comparison put the
field along z (`si8-us-lelfield.in`, `si8-paw-lelfield.in`, `qe-1.log`): this code read `el_pol`
0.98090 (US) and 0.98017 (PAW) where `pw.x` printed 0.95882 and 0.95832, a ratio of 1.0230 at
both 0.001 and 0.002, with totals 1.1e-5 and 4.4e-5 Ry below `pw.x`'s, which is `-(1/2) d chi E^2`
for a susceptibility 2.3 per cent larger. The cause is `compute_qqc` in `upflib/qvan2.f90:237`:

```fortran
qmod(1) = q(1)**2 + q(2)**2 + q(1)**2
```

`q(1)` twice and no `q(3)` (and the same line in `compute_qqr`, `:194`). `compute_qqc` is also what
`bp_c_phase.f90:421` (`lberry`) and `forces_bp_efield.f90:344` call, so `pw.x`'s ultrasoft Berry-phase
polarization along z is affected the same way; that was not measured here. For `q = dk` along z
this is zero, so `pw.x` uses `Q_ij(0) e^{-i dk.tau}`, the `G = 0` augmentation with the structure
phase, for the cross term; along y it is right, and along x it is `sqrt(2)|dk|`. Put in this code
by hand (the `qzero` run, `dq-usz-2.log`: `Q_ij(0)` in place of `Q_ij(+-dk)`), the z run
reproduces `pw.x`: total -91.20020319541 against -91.20020319 (5.4e-9 Ry), `el_pol` 0.9588155380
against 0.9588236504 (8.1e-6), and at 0.002 1.1e-8 Ry and 4.6e-6. The cell is cubic and the
structure the same along y and z, so this code's z and y numbers agree to 1e-13, and `pw.x`'s y run
agrees with them. The z references are committed (`reference.out.si8-{us,paw}-lelfield{,-zero,-2}`)
to pin this reading of `pw.x`; the inputs say so in their headers.

**The response.** `chi = d el_pol/dE` by Richardson's step on 0.001 and 0.002 (`el = chi E +
chi3 E^3`, the zero-field `el_pol` being 1e-14 here): 979.646 from this code, 979.648 from `pw.x`
along y, so `epsilon_inf = 1 + 4 pi chi/Omega = 12.669` (`Omega = 1054.98 bohr^3`) from both. The
two-point extrapolation removes the `E^2` term by construction, so it is a statement about the
agreement of the two codes, not a convergence test of the step; the per-step deviations from the
extrapolated value are 1.26 and 5.03, which is what `E^2` gives with two points and nothing more.
The ultrasoft silicon dielectric constant from `ph.x` (`reference.out.ph-si-epsilon-us`, 14.33) is
a different functional (PZ), cell and mesh (4x4x4 shifted on the primitive cell, against ten points
here), so it is context, not a check: the mesh, not the step, sets the gap.

**What was missing**, as found in the code. The entry named the cross augmentation `q_dk` with its
structure phase in the string overlap and `forces_us_efield`. Both right, and two more pieces the
entry did not name, both in `h_epsi_her_set`/`h_epsi_her_apply`: the duals carry the cross term too
(`e+ = sum_n S(dk)|u_{k+dk,n}> (M+^-1)_{nm}`, QE's "add US terms into evcm", `q_dkp` with
`CONJG(struc)` for the backward link), and the operator applies `|a><S u| + |S u><a|`
(`h_epsi_her_apply`'s `evct = S evcel` and the `qq` sandwich in `sca_mat`), so `BerryTerm` is handed
`S u` in place of `u` and needs no new code. A neighbour's projections need no Miller gather,
`<beta^{k+G}|psi_{k+G}> = <beta^k|psi_k>`. The cross factors reuse `topology/augmentation.py`
(validated by P16/P47), split into `augmentation_species_at_q` (position-free, host) and the phase,
which `BerryFieldSetup._cross_matrix` applies with `jnp` so that `jax.grad` in the positions gives
`forces_us_efield` through the projectors and the phase of the moved calculation; the Fortran is
not transcribed. The analytic force route (`forces/analytic.py`) adds only `forces_ion_efield`, so
it now refuses an augmented field by name, as does the chunked force on a streamed store
(`forces/chunked.py`), whose comment said the electronic half does not move with the atoms, which
is no longer true. The streamed store's host path (`host_term`, `_link_signs` on a host array)
refuses too.

**Still refused** (each with a test that fires): an augmented dataset at `nspin = 2` or on a spin
