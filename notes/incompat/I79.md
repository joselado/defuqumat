# I79. Gamma phonon x magnetic spinor: partial, the refusal stays

Status: **partial**. The axial symmetrisation of a displacement response is written, checked
and its guard (`Calculation.symmetrize_atom_displacement`) is lifted. The phonon refusal
(`phonon._require_no_spinor`, `nspin_mag = 4`) stays. The collinear limit of a magnetic spinor
reproduces the `nspin = 2` force constants to 8.3e-6 Ry/bohr^2 and not to round-off, and about
2e-6 of that remains when both runs share one ground state, which I could not locate within the
stop rule (three D22 runs). This branch also carries I3's spinor assembly (cherry-picked
`800c8802`, which is on `incompat/I3` and not on master), since I79 needs it; I3's own refusal of
the nonmagnetic spinor is left exactly as I3 left it.

## What was measured, and against what

D22, CPU, one core per job through `review/guide/slot.sh`, worktree `/l/ladovj1/w-inc-I79`,
logs in `/l/ladovj1/review/incompat/I79/`. One run per number (no medians), `conv_thr = 1e-12`,
the default `tr2 = 1e-18`, guards bypassed in the harnesses (`o2.py`, `chains.py`, `ab.py`,
`transplant.py`).

**The identity of the entry's check**: a spinor with the moment along z and no spin-orbit
coupling against the same cell at `nspin = 2`. Both legs at `origin_tangent=False` for O2 (the
convention its LSDA run was checked against `ph.x` in).

| cell | `max|D_spinor - D_lsda|` | scale | note |
|---|---|---|---|
| triplet O2, ultrasoft, insulator (`o2-fixed-lsda`) | 8.275e-6 | 1.679 | all of it in the transverse block, entries 0.010 to 0.019 |
| antiferromagnetic H chain, NC metal (`h-chain-afm`) | 1.864e-6 | 0.0376 | |
| O2 spinor with its 8 magnetic operations against `nosym` | 2.333e-8 | 1.679 | see below for what it cannot see |

Beside the phonon, on O2: total energies -63.3630838548 (spinor) and -63.3630837811 Ry (LSDA),
7.4e-8 apart; `epsilon` diagonal 1.110915839 / 1.198006816 against 1.11091585 / 1.198006981;
`Z*` of atom 0 diagonal 0.133662 / 0.2000839 against 0.1336609 / 0.2000806. For comparison the
nonmagnetic spinor reproduces the scalar run's force constants to 2.7e-10 (I3), and the LSDA run
sits 4.0e-5 from `ph.x` in the transverse block (I2), so the residue is far below `ph.x`'s
distance and four orders above round-off.

**Two A/B runs to locate it.**

1. The spinor's local spin frame clamps `|m|` to `|n|` (`xc/functional.py:local_spin_frame`)
   where the collinear path does not, and O2 has 1504 of 91125 grid points with `|m| >= |n|`
   (counted again here). With the clamp removed the spinor energy is the same to every printed
   digit (-63.3630838548) and the difference is 8.270e-6 against 8.275e-6. **Not the cause.**
   The chain has no such point (0 of 51200).
2. The collinear ground state written as a spinor (up bands `(psi, 0)`, down bands `(0, psi)`,
   density `(n_up + n_dw, 0, 0, n_up - n_dw)`, `becsum` rebuilt from those bands), which is an
   exact fixed point of the spinor SCF, and the spinor dynamical matrix solved on it:
   **2.255e-6** on O2 and **1.430e-6** on the chain. So the SCFs landing on slightly different
   fixed points account for most of O2's 8.3e-6 (the spinor chain also lands with a total moment
   of 2.4e-6 and a transverse one of 1e-6, and its Fermi level 2e-6 Ry away), and about 1e-6 to
   2e-6 belongs to the spinor response route itself. On O2 the transplanted run splits the
   transverse pairs by 3e-5 cm^-1 (-155.582608 and -155.582577), which the LSDA run does not.

**The axial displacement average** (`tests/unit/test_magnetic_displacement_symmetry.py`, fast,
on the helix `h4-cycloid-90` with its 4 magnetic operations, no SCF):

- summed over the displaced atom it equals `symmetrize_directional`, whose axial rule is checked
  against the whole zone on the same helix (2.6e-5 axial, 0.665 with three scalars,
  `test_spinor_response.py`): **7.1e-15** on fields of 10.7. This ties the sign convention to a
  measured one;
- it is a projector (twice equals once to 1e-12) and not the identity, which catches a wrong atom
  label (`irt` where its inverse belongs);
- it differs from the three-scalar average by more than 1e-2 on an arbitrary field, and the
  walked (scan) form equals the batched one.

The O2 symmetrised-against-`nosym` number (2.3e-8) runs the average on the grid and in `becsum`
end to end, but it cannot tell the axial rule from the scalar one: the response of a collinear
state has no transverse magnetization, and on `m_z` alone every sign of the magnetic group is +1.
An end-to-end wedge against the whole zone on the helix (axial, three scalars, none) was queued
in run 1 (`helix.log`); it had not finished when this was written, the `nosym` phonon of the
textured metal alone running past 35 minutes at 4.7 GB.

## What was missing, and where the entry was wrong

- **`nsolv = 2` is not a missing term here.** `ph.x` solves the time-reversed system under
  `domag` (`dfpt_kernels.f90:212-213`, `solve_linter.f90:28-31`, averaged in `drhodv.f90:108`
  and `:217`) because its density response accumulates only `psi^* dpsi` and relies on the
  partner at `-k` for the conjugate half. Here the density response is one `jvp` of the density
  (`SternheimerSolver.response_density`), `2 Re[psi^* dpsi]` with both halves, on the whole k-set
  a magnetic run keeps. That is already checked for a textured metal at `q = 0` by
  `test_chi0_matches_a_finite_difference_for_a_spinor` (four probes, 1e-5). It becomes a term at
  `q != 0` (I82), not at `Gamma`.
- **`int3_nc_save` and `dbecsum_aux`** are, as I3 found for the nonmagnetic case, inside the
  `jvp` of `coefficients` and of `density_at`; O2 is ultrasoft and runs.
- **The axial rotation with `det(R)` and the `t_rev` sign** was the real missing term, in two
  places, not one: `Calculation.symmetrize_atom_displacement` (the grid, the guard of the entry)
  and `BecsumSymmetry.apply_atom_displacement` (`paw/symmetry.py`), which treated the four
  `becsum` spin components as spectators at `nspin_mag = 4` without any guard. The new average is
  `system/symmetry.py:symmetrize_atom_displacement_spin_density`. The same spectator treatment is
  in `apply_directional`, the electric field's `becsum` average; it is behind the textured
  dielectric refusal (I105) and is not changed here.
- Everything else of I3's assembly (`spinors=True`, `qq_so`, the walked-route refusal) was
  needed as it stands.

## What would lift it, ranked

1. Locate the 1e-6 to 2e-6 on a shared ground state. The transplant harness
   (`review/incompat/I79/transplant.py`) is the instrument: run it at `tr2 = 1e-22` with the CG
   threshold fixed (does it move with the loop's stop, which for a spinor is `tr2 / npol` over
   four channels?), and compare the induced densities mode by mode, `m_x` and `m_y` included,
   which should be zero exactly.
2. I3's bismuthene question, since a magnetic spinor cannot be lifted ahead of the nonmagnetic one.
3. I105's 5.3 per cent on `i-atom-soc`, which this identity cannot see: it has no transverse
   kernel and no spin-orbit coupling.

## User guide and README

Nothing changes now, since the refusal stays. Once it is lifted, the sentence in
`docs/guide/phonons.md`, the Limits note,

> The phonons, the Raman tensors, the strain response, the elastic constants
> and the electrostriction tensors give an error for a noncollinear or
> spin-orbit run.

would become

> The phonons at $\Gamma$ work for a noncollinear run, with or without a
> magnetization. The Raman tensors, the strain response, the elastic constants
> and the electrostriction tensors give an error for a noncollinear or
> spin-orbit run.

The README row `| **Phonons at $\Gamma$** | calc.get_phonons() | ✓ | ✓ |` needs no cell change.

## Open question for the user

I3 keeps the nonmagnetic spinor refused although without spin-orbit coupling it reproduces the
scalar run to 2.7e-10. Whether a regime that is measured this well should stay refused because
its spin-orbit sibling (bismuthene) is not is your choice; I left it refused.
