# I105. Dielectric tensor and Born charges x textured spinor: partial, the refusal stays

Status: **partial**. The entry's check, a finite-field difference of the dipole that shares
only the ground state, is built and run on one string of k-points along the field. The
finite field converges onto the Sternheimer `epsilon` as the Berry phase's `dk^2` in both
geometries, field along the moment and across it. Along the moment it converges to an offset
of about -5e-4 (0.16 per cent of `eps - 1`) that I could not locate, and the Born charges are
not checked by anything here. The stop rule fired (three D22 runs, past 90 minutes), so
`require_a_measured_spinor_response` stays, with the numbers in its message. Lifting it for
`epsilon` is the user's decision (below).

The entry's framing was wrong in two ways that matter more than the 5.3 per cent: the
Gamma-only number compared with `ph.x` is not converged in the k-sampling, and `ph.x` is no
reference for this regime at all.

## What was measured, and against what

D22, CPU, one core per job through `review/guide/slot.sh`. The measurements ran from the
worktree `/l/ladovj1/w-inc-I105`, detached at `6c5b3a36` (the harness is standalone and needs
no change to the package); `/l/ladovj1/w-inc-I105-v2` carries the branch commit. Logs in
`/l/ladovj1/review/incompat/I105/` (`z-n3.log`, `z-n6.log`, `z-n9.log`, `z-n12.log`,
`x-n3.log`, `x-n6.log`, `x-n12.log`), harness `harness.py` there (its copy is in this
session's scratchpad). One run per number (no medians). `i-atom-soc.in` with its `K_POINTS`
replaced by one string of `N` points `(0, 0, j/N)`, `conv_thr = 1e-12`, the Sternheimer at
`tr2 = 1e-24`, `threshold = 1e-12` (the `CONVERGED` of `test_spinor_dielectric.py`),
`born_charges=False`.

**The identity.** The electric enthalpy of `scf/berryfield.py` (`lelfield`, `gdir = 3`,
`nppstr = N`, `nberrycyc = 3`) at `+-h`, started from the plain SCF, gives

    eps_zz(FF) = 1 + 4 pi (el(+h) - el(-h)) / (2 h Omega),

with `el` the electronic dipole in Ry a.u. (`berry_dipoles[0]`; `acc = 0` in every run,
`el(+h) = -el(-h)` to 1e-12) and `Omega = 1728` bohr^3. In Ry a.u. `alpha = d/E` with no
factor, checked on the committed Si8 pair: `1 + 4 pi 0.92997/(1054.98 x 0.001) = 12.08` from
`reference.out.si8-lelfield` and `-zero`. The enthalpy fixes the macroscopic field and relaxes
every `G != 0` component, which is what the Sternheimer route differentiates, so on the same
k-set the two differ only by the discretised Berry phase, `O(dk^2)`. The finite field uses no
`dvpsi_e`, no XC kernel and no linear solve. Two guards are bypassed in the harness: this
item's, and `lelfield` with spin-orbit coupling (`require_a_berry_field_regime`), whose
overlaps `<u_k|u_k+dk>` on a norm-conserving dataset do not see `dvan_so`. Nothing checks the
Berry side under spin-orbit coupling on its own, so the convergence of the two routes onto each
other is the evidence for both.

string; the gap between bands 7 and 8 between 0.0125 and 0.0164 Ry.

## What was missing (the entry's term, and what was found instead)

The entry said "unlocated", with the suspects the kernel (cleared along the moment by P104),
the field's source term, and `ph.x`. What the code and the numbers say:

1. **The number compared with `ph.x` is not converged.** `i-atom-soc.in` is one k-point, the
   Gamma point, and along the moment the Sternheimer `eps_zz` is 1.574482 there against
   1.300627 on 12 points. The k-sampling shift is 0.27, more than three times the 0.080 by
   which `ph.x` differed, and the uniaxial anisotropy changes sign with it (zz below xx on the
   string). The 1x1x1 comparison was a test of agreement at one mesh, which the two codes
   still owe each other, but not of a converged quantity.
2. **`ph.x` is not a reference for this regime.** `phq_readin.f90:546` means to refuse it
   ("The phonon code with noncollinear magnetism and electric field is not implemented") and
   never fires, since it tests `magnetic_sym` before `phq_setup.f90:211` sets it. And there is
   a concrete defect behind that refusal: `solve_e.f90:140` calls `dvpsi_e`, which stores
   `P_c^+ [H, r] psi` in record `(ipol - 1) nksq + ik` of `iuebar` (`dvpsi_e.f90:149-150`),
   while `dfpt_kernel` (`dfpt_kernels.f90:212-213`) sets `nsolv = 2` under
   `noncolin .AND. domag` and calls `sternheimer_kernel(..., isolv == 2, ...)`, which reads
   record `nrec + npert nksq` for the time-reversed pass (`response_kernels.f90:206-209`).
   Nothing writes those records for the field; `solve_linter.f90:200-239` writes them for a
   phonon (`dvqpsi_us` with `becpt`, `alphapt`). What the record then holds is not determined
   here.
3. **What is left is the -5e-4 along the moment, and the Born charges.** No term is missing
   in the route as far as the identity can see: both geometries converge, across the moment
   to an indicative 0.7 per cent of `eps - 1` and along it to 0.16 per cent. The offset along
   the moment is a measured, unexplained discrepancy. The Born charges cannot be checked on
   this cell, since one atom has `Z* = 0` by the acoustic sum rule.

## The guard

`defuqumat/response/efield.py:require_a_measured_spinor_response` stays and refuses
`nspin_mag = 4`. Its message now quotes the two ladders, the unlocated offset, that the Born
charges are unchecked, why the `ph.x` number was unconverged and the `solve_e` defect; its
docstring carries the tables.

The sibling refusals still fire: `test_a_textured_spinor_is_refused_by_name` (unchanged, run
locally, passes). The phonon's `_require_no_spinor` (`phonon.py`) is not touched, though its
message still says the dielectric tensor is "5.3 per cent from ph.x for a reason not yet
found", which the integrator may want to point here.

## The decision for the user

Whether to lift the refusal for `epsilon` (with `born_charges=False`), keeping it for the Born
charges, on the strength of the identity above.

- Lift for `epsilon`: buys the dielectric constant of a magnetic spin-orbit insulator, on an
  identity that converges in both geometries; costs an unexplained -5e-4 along the moment and a
  guard split by `born_charges` (the default of `get_dielectric_tensor` asks for the charges,
  so a textured run would still raise until `born_charges=False` is passed).
- Keep the refusal until the offset is located: the experiments that would locate it are the
  same ladder at `screening = "hartree"` and the same ladder on a nonmagnetic spinor of this
  dataset (a filled-shell ion), which says whether the offset belongs to the moment or to the
  finite field.
