# I33. Ultracell x potential-only meta-GGA

Status: done. A TB09 (or BJ06) unit cell on a norm-conserving dataset at `nspin_mag = 1` runs
on the ultracell and converges to the TB09 supercell. A meta-GGA on a magnetic cell or on an
ultrasoft or PAW dataset still refuses by name.

## What was there before this unit

The guard was already lifted on master, by `b89dbff` of the previous batch (`tau` built on the
box from the ultracell states, mixed beside the density, handed to `v_of_rho` on the box), and
`notes/INCOMPATIBILITIES-NIGHT.md` lists I33 as "not addressed" because its check never closed:
`review/incompat/ultracell/r4-tb09.log` read 1.219e-1, 3.119e-2, 1.084e-2 at `nbnd = 12, 24,
48` and 5.415 at 80, where one k-point's frozen solve did not settle in 100 Davidson steps. So
master carried a lifted refusal with no number; this unit supplies it. The entry's missing term
("`tau` from the states rather than the carried density") is what `b89dbff` wrote, and it was
right.

## The number

`tests/regression/test_ultracell_regimes.py::test_a_tb09_ultracell_converges_to_the_tb09_supercell`,
TB09 silicon (`Si.pz-vbc.UPF`, `input_dft = 'tb09'`), a 2x1x1 ultracell on the `(1, 2, 2)` mesh
of `k0`, against the explicit 2x1x1 TB09 supercell SCF under the same `0.05 cos` Ry modulation
(the entry's check, exact by the nested basis of P88). The functional has no energy, so the
quantity is the induced density, compared Fourier component by Fourier component, relative to
its largest component. D22, CPU, one core, single runs (a deterministic loop):

| run | cutoff, boxes | nbnd = 12 | 24 | 32 | 48 | 64 | 80 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `r1-tb09.log` (126d6ec) | 12 Ry, box (30,15,15), supercell (32,15,15) | 1.219e-1 | 3.119e-2 | | 1.084e-2 | 1.080e-2 | |
| `r1-tb09m.log` (126d6ec) | 13 Ry, both (36,18,18) | 1.243e-1 | 2.816e-2 | | 8.144e-3 | 6.218e-3 | |
| `r2-tb09m.log` (126d6ec) | 13 Ry, both (36,18,18) | | | 1.691e-2 | | | 4.482e-3 |
| `r3-tb09.log` (18cc163, the committed test body) | 13 Ry, both (36,18,18) | 1.243e-1 | 2.816e-2 | | 8.144e-3 | 6.218e-3 | |

The tiled null: `dV` 1.44e-15, density 1.15e-6 of its maximum (13 Ry).

What the table says: at 12 Ry the ladder stops at 1.08e-2 between 48 and 64 bands, a floor,
and on the matched boxes the same ladder falls monotonically through 6.2e-3 at 64 to 4.5e-3 at
80 with no floor in sight. So the 12 Ry floor is the two FFT grids, not the box: TB09 is built
from `tau / rho` and `|grad rho| / rho` on the real-space grid and feels a 30 against 32
sampling where the LDA (P88: 9.16e-2, 1.32e-2, 3.33e-3, 2.13e-3, 1.70e-3 at 12, 24, 48, 64, 80,
at 12 Ry) does not. On the matched pair TB09 converges about twice to three times slower than
the LDA at the same rung, which is what a potential built from gradients of truncated states
should do; the ratio is 2.4 at 48, 2.9 at 64 and 2.6 at 80, so it is not growing.

The test now runs at `MATCHED_ECUT` with the ladder 12, 24, 48, 64 and asserts monotone and
below 1e-2 at 64 (the 80 rung is where the 12 Ry cell's Davidson broke; at 13 Ry it ran, but
64 is enough to pass the tolerance with margin).
