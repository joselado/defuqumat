**The convergence parameter is `conv_thr`, and the gap behaves as the approximation predicts.** Two
readings (`round2.log`, `round3.log`):

* From 1e-5 to 1e-6, a double run stopped at the same `conv_thr` (`--baseline`) sits as far from the
  converged double state as the single run does in the eigenvalues and the density, to about two
  digits, on every regime (Si `deig` 8.2e-5 against 8.8e-5; DFT+U `deig` 3.2e-4 both, `drho` 1.4e-3
  both; spiral `dE` 7.5e-5 both, `drho` 7.4e-2 both; TB09 `dE` 2.15e-3 both). The energy of the
  variational regimes, second order in the density, is already at the precision's floor there
  (Si 9.9e-7 single against 7.2e-8 double; PAW 1.1e-6 against 1.1e-7). So above the floor the gap in
  the state is the SCF's own error, and the precision shows only in the energy, at about 1e-6 Ry.
* Below the floor (the `conv_thr` refusal patched out, `--no-conv-floor`), the density keeps
  falling as the SCF's error does, roughly as `sqrt(conv_thr)` (TB09 `drho` 1.2e-3, 3.9e-4, 7.3e-5,
  5.0e-6 at 1e-7 to 1e-10; DFT+U `dns` 7.1e-5, 2.6e-5, 5.9e-6, 1.8e-7), while the energy stops at
  the precision's floor and stays there: `|dE|` between 3e-8 and 3.0e-6 Ry at 1e-8 to 1e-10 on
  every variational regime (ultrasoft -8.9e-7 at 1e-10, PAW -4.9e-7, spinor -1.25e-6, spiral
  -1.5e-7, DFT+U -9.6e-7, spiral + PAW -3.0e-6 at 1e-9). That is the plateau
  `SINGLE_CONV_THR_FLOOR`'s comment quotes for norm-conserving silicon (1e-6 to 2e-6 Ry), so the
  existing floor carries over unchanged and the slow test's energy bound (5e-6 Ry) is set from the
  plateau, about three times the largest value on it, not from one point.

**TB09's energy is the exception, and it is not a precision effect.** Its total is not the value of
any functional (`run_scf` warns), so it moves to first order in the density: `dE` 3.3e-4 at
`conv_thr = 1e-7` follows `drho` down to -1.2e-7 at 1e-10. What a TB09 run is for, the eigenvalues,
lands at 5.5e-5 Ry, against 4.8e-5 for a double run stopped at the same `conv_thr`. The density
of the single run is five times further from the converged one than the double run's at 1e-7 (1.2e-3
against 2.2e-4), and the same factor appears on the supported norm-conserving silicon (5.6e-4 against
7.8e-5): the single run's own residual is noisier, so it stops one iteration earlier or later. This
is the existing behaviour of the mode, not something TB09 adds.

**What the check would read with the missing terms switched off.**

* The casts on `H|psi>`: without them the spinor, spiral and DFT+U runs do not run at all;
  the first Davidson step raises `TypeError: lax.dynamic_update_slice requires arguments to have the
  same dtypes, got complex64, complex128` (`round1.log`, the casts absent, reproduced by
  `tests/unit/test_band_precision.py::test_h_psi_stays_32_bit_for_a_spinor_a_spiral_and_dftu`, which
  fails on all three cases with the casts reverted and passes with them).
* The `becsum` promotion: without it a spiral on an ultrasoft dataset handed the mixer a float32
  `becsum` (`round5.log`, `becsum=float32`), against the rule that the grid side is double. The
  energy did not move beyond the floor's own scatter (spiral + ultrasoft `dE` +9.8e-7 before,
  +1.24e-6 after, at 1e-7; collinear ultrasoft +1.42e-6 before, +4.9e-7 after), so this fix is the
  rule kept, not a measured improvement; `test_becsum_is_accumulated_in_the_grid_precision` fails
  without it.
* The ethr floor (`--no-floor`): on these small cells removing it does not degrade the result (Si
  `dE` 2.7e-7 and -1.4e-7 at 1e-7 and 1e-9, ultrasoft 2.8e-7 and -5.2e-7). Its 5.5e-5 Ry failure was
  measured on eight-atom silicon and is not reproduced on two atoms, so this arm does not discriminate
  here and the floor stays as it was.

**What was missing, and where the entry was wrong.** The entry said "a single-precision check per
regime". The code had three gaps, each found by running the regime with the guard patched out:

1. `Calculation._spinor_hamiltonian` passed `self.kinetic` uncast and `local_terms`'s two noncollinear
   branches (spinor and spiral) did not cast the smooth-grid potential, where the collinear branch
   casts both to `_band_real()`; a float32 spinor times a float64 potential came back complex128.
   Now cast.
2. `HubbardTerm` was built on `self.wfcU`, kept in the cell's precision, so `V_U|psi>` promoted the
   same way. A new `Calculation.band_wfcU()` casts it for the Hamiltonian (both construction sites,
   `hubbard_terms` and the robust mode's mixed-Hamiltonian branch in `run_scf`), and `wfcU` itself
   stays float64, so `ns` is accumulated in double.
3. `becsum` and `spinor_becsum` (`scf/density.py`) took the outer product in the projections' dtype,
   so the sums over bands and k-points ran in float32. The guard's own docstring named this ("becsum
   would be accumulated in the bands' precision"). Now promoted to the weights' precision first
   (`_in_grid_precision`), a no-op in double.

Also found: the spiral's `dE/dq` (`forces/spiral.py:_require_a_differentiable_spiral`) differentiates
its own frozen energy and never passed `reject_potential_only`, so lifting the spiral would have let
`dE/dq` run through float32 bands. The band-precision refusal is split out as
`forces/energy.py:reject_single_band_side`, called by `reject_potential_only` (unchanged behaviour)
and now by the spiral's check.

**The guard now** (`driver.py:_require_band_precision_supported`): accepts an augmented dataset, a
spinor, a spiral, DFT+U alone and a meta-GGA alone, and an augmented dataset under a spinor or a
spiral (measured: spiral + ultrasoft, spiral + PAW, spin-orbit + ultrasoft). It refuses DFT+U or a
meta-GGA together with any other of these regimes, which was not measured
(`test_what_it_has_not_been_checked_on_is_refused`: `alas-ldau.in` made a spinor at one k-point,
through the real cell; `test_the_guard_admits_an_augmented_spinor_and_nothing_beside_dftu_or_meta`:
the whole rule on stubs of the attributes the guard reads, since the harness measured the augmented
spinors with the guard patched out). A PAW spinor without a spiral and an ultrasoft spinor without spin-orbit
coupling are accepted on the strength of the spiral + PAW and spin-orbit + ultrasoft cells, which
run the same augmentation and spinor branches.

**The review round** (the verifier's harness and logs in the session scratchpad, `v119/`; one
sample each, this workstation, cores 8 to 11). It held the three dtype fixes and the measured
regimes, and refuted the admission rule and the energy claim. Per finding:

1. **A constrained moment, an applied field and a Berry-phase field are refused by name.** On
   `h-atom-noncolin.in` at `angle1 = 60`: `constrained_magnetization = 'atomic'`, `lambda = 0.5` lands
   single `dE` +1.19e-4 Ry against +2.45e-5 for a double run at the same `conv_thr = 1e-7` (`drho`
   2.6e-3 against 4.5e-4); `'total'` lands -1.22e-5 against +1.21e-6. Both also failed the
   continuation (finding 2). The guard now refuses `constrained_magnetization != 'none'`,
   `magnetic_field is not None` and `berry_field is not None` before anything else, which also
   refuses a field on a collinear norm-conserving run that the old guard let through.
   `test_the_guard_admits_an_augmented_spinor_and_nothing_beside_dftu_or_meta` (stubs, the four
   cases) and `test_a_constrained_spinor_is_refused_in_single_precision` (the real cell).
2. **The continuation from a single-precision source.** The lifted spinor's source misses one
   electron by 8.3e-7, 83 per cent of `continuation.ELECTRON_TOL = 1e-6`, and the constrained ones
   by 1.2e-6 and 1.1e-6, past it, with a message blaming `z_valence`. A source whose last history
   entry says `band_precision = 'single'` is now held to `SINGLE_SOURCE_ELECTRON_TOL = 1e-4`
   relative (the misses measured are up to 5e-7 relative; a swapped `z_valence` moves a whole
   electron, 1e-2 relative on a hundred-electron cell), and past that the message names the float32
   normalisation before `z_valence`. The density is not renormalised: the miss is 1e-6 of an
   electron and `sum_band` rebuilds it at the first iteration.
   `test_a_single_precision_source_continues_past_its_float32_normalisation`.
3. **The energy claim, restated.** The PAW spinor without a spiral (`o-chain-spiral-paw.in` without
   `spiral_q`) sits on a flat plateau of -4.30e-6 to -4.40e-6 Ry at `conv_thr` 1e-7 to 1e-9 (double
   at 1e-7: +5e-9), and spiral + PAW at -3.0e-6 at 1e-9, so "within 1.5e-6 Ry" was wrong. On a total
   of 40.3 Ry that is 1.1e-7 relative, and the other regimes sit at 1e-8 to 1e-7 of `|E|`; the claim
   is now "about 1e-7 of `|E|`" in the guard's docstring and here. The ultrasoft spinor without
   spin-orbit coupling or a spiral measured -1.33e-6 Ry and continues to 1e-13 Ry in 3 iterations;
   the PAW spinor continues to 1.4e-11 in 4. Both are in the slow test now, and every bound there
   is three times the measured value (continuation bounds not below 1e-11). TB09's continuation:
   4 iterations, eigenvalues 7.05e-8 Ry from the double state, `dE` +4.21e-7, bound 1.3e-6.
   **TB09 stays lifted on its eigenvalues, a reviewer's decision taken for the user** (the question
   this record first put): the guide text above says its total is not a physical quantity in any
   precision and quotes 5.5e-5 against 4.8e-5 Ry.
4. **What `DEFUQUMAT_BAND_PRECISION=single` reached.** It makes every `Calculation` single, and with
   spinors admitted that reached `get_z2`, `get_chern`, `get_orbital_magnetization`, `get_torque` and
   the anisotropy and force-theorem legs, none of which called the gate. Checked each:
   * `get_z2`, `get_z2_3d`, `get_chern` (`run_berry_curvature`), `get_orbital_magnetization`, and
     besides them the Berry-phase polarization, the Kubo conductivities and the realtime hierarchy,
     all take their states from `workflows/topology.py:DFTSource._base()`. It now calls
     `reject_single_band_side(calculation, what=...)`, the gate's function given a name for the
     message. `test_states_at_fixed_density_are_refused_on_a_single_band_side`.
   * `run_force_theorem`, `run_anisotropy`, `run_torque` and `run_orientation_torque`
     (`workflows/anisotropy.py`) build their own calculation in `fixed_density_states` at the default
     band precision. Each now refuses first, before anything is diagonalised
     (`_refuse_single_band_side`, which resolves the default precision against the cell's).
     `test_the_anisotropy_legs_are_refused_on_a_single_band_side` (four cases).
   The realtime hierarchy is the one consumer here that is neither an invariant nor a derivative.
   Refusing it in single precision is conservative and is not measured.
5. **Noted, not chased:** on the constrained `'total'` cell the single run has an empty band
   3.89e-2 Ry off at both 1e-7 and 1e-9 while the double run's is 1.5e-6 off; that cell is refused by
   finding 1.
