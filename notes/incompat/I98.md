**What the residual is.** It does not move with `dt` (1e-3 of itself between 0.2 and 0.1) or with the
propagation time (`exp(-eta T)` is `e^-22` at 0.2 eV), and between 0.2 and 0.4 eV it moves by at most
1e-2. It falls with the k-mesh, 3.9e-2 to 1.7e-2 from 2x2x2 to 4x4x4, and the norm-conserving control,
whose update was validated in `PLAN.md` P136 and has no augmentation at all, carries the same residual
on both meshes: within 0.3 points on 2x2x2 and 0.2 points on 4x4x4. So the residual belongs to the
kick route on a coarse mesh (the velocity gauge's band-curvature term, which on 2x2x2 makes the raw
`eps` itself read -9346, and the local fields' response to it), and nothing of it is specific to the
augmentation. The k-mesh is the convergence parameter; a mesh past 4x4x4 was not run.

**With the term switched off.** Holding the augmentation charge of `rho(t)` at the ground state's
`becsum` (`D(t)` still rebuilt, `Calculation.augmented` patched, which reached the propagation: the
induced potential moved by 1.63e-4 against 2.75e-4 Ry and the energy spread rose from 1.4e-7 to
1.1e-4 Ha) puts the 2x2x2 residual at **+1.79e-1** (ultrasoft) and **+1.69e-1** (PAW) at 0.2 eV,
+1.64e-1 and +1.54e-1 at 0.4 eV, the other side of the static shift and 20 points from the
norm-conserving residual, against the 0.2 to 0.3 points the update with the term reads.

**The second check, the fixed point and the conserved energy** (`notes/incompat/I98-harness/fixedpoint.py`,
the computation of the slow test below, log `review/incompat/I98/fixedpoint-r.log`, `cf200528`): on
the 8 Ry cell at dual 8, Gamma and (0.25, 0.1, -0.05), 40 steps of 0.05 with `potential = 'hxc'`,
`|v(t) - v_scf|` is **5.3e-12** (ultrasoft) and **4.3e-12** (PAW, `input_dft = 'sla+pw'`) with no
field; after a kick of 0.01/bohr the potential moves by 4.7e-3 Ry and the conserved energy by
**1.07e-10** and **9.9e-11** Ha, the norm `<u|S|u>` by 1.1e-11. With the augmentation charge held,
**2.78e-3** and **2.82e-3** Ha. The functional's derivative in `becsum` is `newd(vltot + v(t)) + ddd(t)`,
so this conservation tests the augmentation charge at `k + kappa`, `D(t)` and PAW's one-centre `D(t)`
together. The reason PAW runs this one test under `sla+pw` (a jump of 2.5e-7 Ry in the one-centre
energy where Perdew-Zunger's two branches meet at `rs = 1`) is the first implementer's reading and was
not measured again here; the shift above runs PAW under the dataset's own functional.

**What was missing, and where the entry was right.** The entry's three terms were the missing ones,
and the first implementer's commit `70a615e2` wrote them in `realtime/selfconsistent.py`:
`rho(t)` carries `sum Q_ij becsum_ij(t)` with `becsum` from the projectors at `k + kappa(t)`
(`_Run.density`, `becsum_of`), averaged with the field's little group (`build_becsum_symmetry` with the
field's symmetries, never the crystal's, which would average the response away as `sym_rho` would);
`D(t)` is `newd` of `vltot + v(t)` through `Calculation.local_terms` at every step; PAW's one-centre
`D` is taken in the difference form `ddd_scf + ddd[becsum(t)] - ddd[becsum0]`, for the reason the grid
potential is. Two things the entry did not name were also needed: the augmented step is the
generalised generator `S^-1 [H - kappadot . X]` with the kick crossed by `_Chunk.jump` before the first
density (P141's machinery, which the self-consistent step had not used), and the conserved energy's
band part has to be taken at the bare `D` (plus `ddd_scf - ddd0` for PAW), since at `D(t)` it counts
the augmentation's share twice.

**What stays refused**, each asserted in `tests/unit/test_realtime.py::test_what_is_refused_is_refused_by_name`:
the perturbative orders with the update (`route='orders'`, and through
`propagate_orders_self_consistent`), the frequency-domain hierarchy with the update (`route='hierarchy'`,
and through `hierarchy_linear_self_consistent`), an augmented dataset on spinors, at `nspin = 2`
(written but not measured), `potential = 'hartree'` on PAW (the one-centre energy is Hartree and

realtime unit files pass at `cf200528` (D22, `fast.log` at `70a615e2`, 34 passed, and `fast-r.log`
at the final commit).
