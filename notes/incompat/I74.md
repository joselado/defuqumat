# I74. `mixing_space = 'g'` x magnetic spiral

Status: **done**. The refusal in `run_scf` is gone; a magnetic spiral mixed in G
reaches the real-space fixed point on every cell tried, norm-conserving,
ultrasoft and PAW. Fix round (2026-10-16): the verifier showed the first
check could not tell the new layout from the cutting one, so the check is now
the one at `conv_thr = 1e-13` in "The discriminating number", where the cutting
layout stalls 3.5e-9 Ry off and the new one converges to 1.1e-13 Ry.

## The discriminating number

What separates them is a tighter threshold, `conv_thr = 1e-13`, where the cut
layout stalls. One run each (deterministic on one core), D22 CPU, worktree
`/l/ladovj1/w-inc-I74-v3` at tree `9d76da32` (the fix commit `c7f71242`),
harness `/l/ladovj1/review/incompat/I74/harness3.py`, which is the slow test's
computation (`beta = 0.3`, `max_iterations = 40`) plus the resident-layout
control swapped in for `driver.SpiralSphereLayout`; logs `h3-o.log`, `h3-h.log`.
`max|dn|` is the charge, `max|d|m||` the pointwise length of the moment, the
bound `1e-5 max|n|`.

| cell | layout under `'g'` | converged, iterations (r / g) | `E_g - E_r` (Ry) | `max|dn|` | `max|d|m||` | slow test |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `o-chain-spiral-us.in` | `SpiralSphereLayout` | yes / yes, 14 / 13 | -1.1e-13 | 1.2e-7 | 1.2e-7 | passes |
| same | resident `SphereLayout` (cuts the pair) | yes / **no**, 14 / 40 | **-3.5e-9** | 5.0e-8 | 2.0e-5 | fails (convergence, energy, `|m|` above 8.8e-6) |
| `h-chain-spiral.in` | `SpiralSphereLayout` (whole box) | yes / yes, 12 / 12 | 1.1e-16 | 3.9e-16 | 1.6e-15 | passes |
| same | resident `SphereLayout` | yes / yes, 12 / 12 | 1.1e-16 | 1.9e-12 | 9.9e-12 | passes |

The oxygen chain is the discriminating arm; the verifier's 60-iteration control
read the same stall (60/60, 3.55e-9 Ry, `h2-A3.log`), and at `q_z = 0.4` with TF
and plain mixing 100/100 and 7.1e-9 Ry, while the new layout converged in 13 to
26 iterations to 1e-13 to 8e-12 Ry of `'r'` (`h2-E.log`). The hydrogen chain
only confirms that the whole-box layout is the real-space mixer itself; at dual 4
the resident sphere cuts too little of the pair there for the control to stall.

**The density is compared as `n` and `|m|`, not per channel.** Without
spin-orbit coupling a rigid rotation of every moment costs nothing, so two
converged runs may land on the same state turned by a small angle. On the
oxygen chain at `q_z = 0.4` with TF, `'g'` against `'r'` reads 2.8e-5 in `m_y` and
2.3e-5 in `m_z` at both `conv_thr = 1e-11` and `1e-13`, so it does not shrink with
the threshold, while the charge and `|m|(r)` agree to 2.7e-7 and 7.1e-7 at 1e-11
and to 5.5e-8 and 7.4e-8 at 1e-13; the `'g'` run's total transverse moment has a
2e-7 `m_y` and `m_z` that the `'r'` run lacks, which is the rotation
(`h3-F.log`, verifier). So the earlier run at the default `conv_thr = 1e-6`
(`harness-1.log`, 1.9e-4 on `m_x`, 1.5e-7 Ry) and its pull to 8.0e-7 at 1e-11 is
partly the two mixers stopping at different points of the same basin and partly
this zero mode, which sits in `m_x`, `m_y` and `m_z` and is not a second fixed
point. On the Co helix the 7.8e-6 in `m_z` is the same mode; the charge and the
transverse pair agree to 7.1e-7.

Trap check against the diff: no `abs`, no band diagonal, no k-set built, no walk
over k; the mixer is host-side as the resident layout already was (`np.asarray`
on the packed blocks, outside any differentiated path), and the `1j` in
`unpack_pair` matches the parent's `unpack_half`; dtypes come from the density
(`head.dtype`, `coefficients.dtype`). The shell's `beta_mag` scales `m_z`'s shell
and the pair's shell and leaves the charge's alone. The padded `kinetic` (ones)
only ever multiplies the zero padding, and Anderson, both preconditioners and the
magnetic step keep that padding at zero (pinned by the Kerker/local-TF test).

## What was missing, and where the entry's term was wrong

The entry says "the transverse channel on the sphere displaced by `q`". That is
the core, and it is `SpiralSphereLayout` in `defuqumat/scf/mixing.py`: the charge
and `m_z` stay on the resident half sphere exactly as `SphereLayout` keeps them,
and the transverse pair is stored as the one complex field `m'_+` on the
displaced smooth sphere (rows 1 and 2 of a `(4, L)` block, scaled by `sqrt(N)` so
the flat dot is the real-space dot, since the displaced sphere has no `(G, -G)`
pairing), with its shell the displaced dense list past the smooth prefix,
rebuilt from the mixed `becsum` through the same augmentation the density uses.
Two pieces the entry did not name were also needed:

- **The case with no displaced set.** A norm-conserving spiral at dual 4 builds no
  displaced sphere (`_spiral_gvectors`), and on the hydrogen chain the displaced
  dense sphere does not even fit the 15-point axis (`displaced_gvectors` refuses
  it), so the density's transverse pair is whatever the box holds. There the
  layout keeps `m'_+` on the whole box (`_spiral_pair_index` in
  `scf/driver.py`), which cuts nothing.
- **The history tag.** A saved mixer history is tagged `'r'` or `'g'`; a spiral's
  history is in a layout that depends on `q` and the cutoffs, so the layout
  carries `tag = 'g-spiral-<digest of the displaced sphere>'`, and a resume at
  another `q`, or from a resident-layout history, drops the history with a warning
  instead of combining vectors in two layouts.

`local_tf_preconditioner_g` was changed to pack the screened charge with the
resident packing (`pack_half`), since `pack` on the spiral layout takes the pair
too, and `SphereLayout.history_bytes` now counts the stored block's real size.
`estimate_size` (`defuqumat/sizing.py`) did not use `history_bytes` and still
counted the history head under `'g'` as `nspin_mag ngms`; the fix round makes it
`4 max(ngms, npair)` for a spiral, exact on the whole-box path (`npair` the box)
and with `ngms` standing in for the displaced smooth sphere where one is built.
On the hydrogen chain at `mixing_ndim = 8` the old count was 9282816 bytes
against 12288000 for the stored blocks alone;
`test_the_size_report_counts_the_spiral_block` fails on the old count and passes
on the new.

Tests: `tests/unit/test_spiral_gvectors.py::test_g_space_mixing_on_a_magnetic_spiral_keeps_the_pair_on_its_own_sphere`
replaces the refusal test (it stops at the layout's construction and asserts the
whole-box layout on the hydrogen chain); `tests/unit/test_spiral_mixing_space.py`
pins the round trip on the displaced spheres with the resident layout's loss as
the control, Parseval for the stored dot, the whole-box layout as the real-space
mixer to 1e-12 over four Anderson steps with and without `beta_mag`, Kerker and
local-TF as the real-space operators, and the tag. The whole-run check is the
slow test below. There is no sibling refusal left in this branch of `run_scf`:
the guard covered exactly `space == 'g'` with a spiral at `nspin_mag == 4`, and
that whole regime is now handled.

## User guide

Page `docs/guide/spirals.md`, the "Limits" note. Delete:

> `mixing_space = 'g'` gives an error for a magnetic spiral, because the
> transverse magnetization lives on the sphere displaced by $\mathbf q$ and
> each mixing step would cut it. The default, `'r'`, mixes the whole dense grid.

Replace with:

> `mixing_space = 'g'` works for a magnetic spiral. The transverse magnetization
> is mixed on the sphere displaced by $\mathbf q$, so nothing is cut. It gives the
> same result as `'r'`: on the ultrasoft oxygen chain at `conv_thr = 1e-13` the two
> energies differ by $1\times10^{-13}$ Ry.

The next sentence ("A spiral whose FFT grid would put two plane waves ...") stays.
