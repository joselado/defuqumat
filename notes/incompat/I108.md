# I108. `noncollinear_gga = 'grad_m'` x `nosource`: done (refusal lifted; the projected field is divergence-free on the dense sphere, which is all the run reads)

**The number.** The check is the identity `div B_xc = 0` after the projection, read two ways, and
the collinear limit.

*On the dense G sphere*, which holds every component of the potential that a matrix element
between two states inside the wavefunction cutoff reads, the projected `'grad_m'` field has
`int |div B|^2` at round-off. On a converged texture (D22, worktree `w-inc-sourcefree` at
`a21948af`, log `review/incompat/sourcefree/pbe1.log`, and `w-inc-sourcefree-v2` at `337d3991`,
`pbe2.log`; harness `notes/incompat/sourcefree-harness/run_sourcefree.py`, one sample each): the
four-site hydrogen chain of `tests/data/qe/h4-noncolin-force.in` under PBE, `nosource`,
`noncollinear_gga = 'grad_m'`, `conv_thr = 1e-8`:

| `ecutrho` (Ry) | iterations | total (Ry) | site 0 moment | `int |div B|^2`, projected | unprojected | box residue, projected/unprojected |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 100 | 62 | -3.9097162817 | 0.24319 | 6.97e-31 | 3.456 | 1.92e-3 |
| 200 | 62 | -3.9100005963 | 0.24643 | 2.01e-30 | 10.72 | 7.79e-4 |

The projection-off column is the "term absent" reading: 3.5 and 10.7, against 1e-30.

The adversarial verifier (D22, worktree `w-inc-sourcefree-ver2`, logs
`review/incompat/sourcefree-ver2/j1.log` to `j11.log`, one sample each) read the same thing from the
potential the SCF itself carried, not only from one recomputed at the converged density: on the
chain above `int |div B|^2` of the SCF's own potential is 7.98e-31, and it differs from the
recomputed projected potential by at most 6.2e-13. On a second chain it built (`ecutwfc = 30`,
starting moments in the plane at 0, 60, 120 and 180 degrees, two k-points along the chain;
`j3.log`), `'grad_m'` under `nosource` converged in 80 iterations at `ecutrho = 120` and 56 at 240,
total -3.9256157 against -3.9255923 Ry, site 0 moment 0.3039 against 0.3056, sphere divergence
2.2e-30 against 4.7e-30.

**The converged states are collinear.** The turn is the starting guess, not the answer: on the chain
above the four site moments end at azimuths 39.6, 38.1, -141.6 and -142.4 degrees (up, up, down,
down), and on the verifier's chain at 0.4, -0.1, -179.3 and 179.0. Each is within about 5 degrees of
a collinear up-up-down-down state, so the projection had a texture to act on during the run and
very little at the end.

*On the whole FFT box* the projection leaves the corners outside the sphere alone (by design,
`sourcefree.py` module docstring), so a residue stays there. Only the sphere is read by the run, so
the box residue is not the physical approximation; it is a property of the array. On a hand-built
two-site texture
(`tests/unit/test_source_free_field.py`, comment table, 2026-10-19) it falls faster under `'grad_m'`
than under `'kubler'`:

| `ecutrho` (Ry) | 40 | 80 | 160 |
|---|---|---|---|
| PBE `'grad_m'` | 6.03e-3 | 1.70e-3 | 1.82e-4 |
| PBE `'kubler'` | 4.92e-2 | 2.56e-2 | 1.02e-2 |
| PZ (LDA) | 3.58e-7 | 2.07e-8 | 3.42e-8 |

That is about `ecutrho^-2.5` for `'grad_m'` against `ecutrho^-1.1` for `'kubler'`, whose field has a
kink where `|m|` is small. The `ecutrho^-2.5` stands for this texture only: on the converged chains
the PBE `'grad_m'` box residue falls more slowly, about `ecutrho^-1.3` on the chain above (1.92e-3 at
100 Ry, 7.79e-4 at 200) and `ecutrho^-0.7` on the verifier's chain (5.47e-4 at 120 Ry, 3.34e-4 at
240). At 100 Ry on the chain above it is 1.9e-3 under `'grad_m'` against 0.35 under `'kubler'`
(converged, `j1.log`). Why the sphere number is the physical one and the box number belongs to
the array (an argument, not something the harness measured): with `ecutrho >= 4 ecutwfc`, no pair
of plane waves inside the wavefunction cutoff differs by a G outside the dense sphere, and the
ultrasoft `newd` reads the potential on the sphere only. The test asserts the 40 and 80 Ry values to 2 per cent and that it falls.

*The collinear limit* (`test_grad_m_and_kubler_project_to_the_same_field_when_m_is_collinear`): with
`m` along one tilted axis and no node, the projected `'grad_m'` and `'kubler'` fields agree to 1e-10
(the two branches are the same functional there), and the projected field has a transverse part
above 1e-4 (the projection did something). With a node they differ by more than 1e-6, so the
comparison can fail.

**Run-level observations** (same logs). Without `nosource` the chain converges under neither PBE
branch in 300 iterations (`'grad_m'` cycling at 2e-5 to 1e-4, `'kubler'` at 6e-6), the limit cycle
`h4-noncolin-force.in`'s header already reports for `pw.x` under PBE. With `nosource`, `'grad_m'`
converges in 62. `'kubler'` is slow, not failing: the implementer's run stopped at the 300-step limit
at 9.8e-8, and the verifier's, with 600 steps allowed (`j1.log`), converged at 371 to 8.9e-9 after a
plateau at 3e-7 to 1e-6 from step 40 to 280. It reaches a different state, nearly nonmagnetic: site
moments 0.015 to 0.033 and total -3.9051760 Ry, against `'grad_m'`'s 0.13 to 0.24 and -3.9097163 Ry.
On the verifier's chain `'kubler'` had not converged at 300 (accuracy 1.97e-8 and falling, moments
collapsing to 0.02 to 0.10, `j2.log`) where `'grad_m'` took 80. On bulk Ni (`j8.log`, `j9.log`) both
meta-GGAs converge under `'kubler'` and `nosource` in 22 to 29 steps (`I109.md`). So on PBE chains
`'grad_m'` is 2 to 6 times faster and lands on a more magnetic state.
The torque: plain `'kubler'` gives a parallel fraction of exactly 1.000000 and zero torque (the
LDA-like silence); plain `'grad_m'` already gives 0.99696 and site torques up to 1.6e-4 (its field
is not parallel to `m` before any projection), so `test_the_torque_fires_when_a_moment_is_turned`'s
"the plain one cannot" reading does not transfer to `'grad_m'`; `'grad_m'` under `nosource` gives
0.98865 with site torques up to 3.1e-6 and a total of 4.7e-7. The total does not follow the
convergence: at `conv_thr = 1e-11` (132 iterations, `j7.log`) it stays at 3.58e-7 while the largest
site torque falls to 1.62e-7, and on the verifier's chain it is 4.7e-9 at 120 Ry and 6.7e-7 at
240 Ry (`j3.log`). Without `nosource` the verifier read it at 1e-18. Under `nosource` the field is not the
derivative of any energy, so global rotation invariance does not force the total to zero away from
a stationary point; the 3.6e-7 floor is measured and not diagnosed.

**What was missing, and where the entry was wrong.** Nothing. The entry said "the projection
checked for a field with a transverse part; only a field parallel to `m` was checked". In `v_of_rho`
the projection runs after `v_xc + v_gradient` on the `'kubler'` and the `'grad_m'` branch alike
(`if source_free:` inside the `nspin == 4` gradient block), and `project_source_free` is linear: it
removes `G (G . B(G)) / |G|^2` from whatever field it is given. So the guard covered a verification
gap, now closed by the numbers above. The refusal in `refuse_noncollinear_gga` is removed.

**The default, the user's question.** `resolve_noncollinear_gga(None, ...)` used to return
`'kubler'` under `nosource` only because the refusal threw. Kept at `'kubler'` with an explicit
branch, so that no existing `nosource` run with a gradient functional changes numbers silently; an
explicit `'grad_m'` is honoured. (No committed `nosource` input carries a GGA:
`ni-noncol-111-nosource.in` is PZ, so either choice moves no recorded number.) The question for
the user:

- keep `'kubler'` as the `nosource` default: nothing changes for an existing run, but on the chain
  above it is the slow branch (371 steps against `'grad_m'`'s 62) and it lands on a nearly
  nonmagnetic state (0.015 to 0.033 per site, against 0.13 to 0.24);
- make `'grad_m'` the default under `nosource` too, as everywhere else: the rule becomes one rule,
  and a `nosource` GGA run made before this changes its numbers.
