# I82. Phonon at q != 0 x nspin = 2 (and noncollinear)

Status: done for a collinear `nspin = 2` insulator under the LDA on the whole-k route. Still
refused, each by name with a gate-speed test: a spinor (noncollinear or spin-orbit), a
spin-polarized metal, the k-chunked route at `nspin = 2`, and the electron-phonon coupling at
`nspin != 1`. Newly refused in the fix round, at every `nspin`: a gradient-corrected
functional (see "The GGA refusal" below), which the first version of this record admitted
unmeasured and which a verifier measured wrong by 1e-2 Ry/bohr^2.

## The number

Measured 2026-10-16 on D22, CPU, one core, one run (deterministic, no median), commit
`5814481`, log `/l/ladovj1/review/incompat/I82/h3.log`, harness
`/l/ladovj1/review/incompat/I82/harness.py h3`.

The check is the supercell identity. `h3-chain-lsda-fixed.in` (magnetic insulator, 1 Bohr
magneton a trimer by `tot_magnetization = 1`, fixed occupations, nosym, noinv, 1x1x8
unshifted, LDA) at `q = (0, 0, 1/2)` through `dynamical_matrix_at_q`, against the new
`tests/data/qe/h3-chain-lsda-fixed-x2.in` (the cell doubled along `a_3`, both trimers
polarized the same way, `tot_magnetization = 2`, 1x1x4) at `Gamma` through
`dynamical_matrix`, the route checked against `ph.x` on triplet O2. With `S` the doubled cell's
matrix and `a`, `b` the two trimers:

- max |D(b3/2) - (S_aa - S_ab)| = **1.52e-5 Ry/bohr^2** on force constants up to 0.46;
  max |Im D(b3/2)| = 7.3e-6, asymmetry 2.6e-6.
- The nine frequencies at `q` against their partners among the doubled cell's eighteen:
  worst **0.051 cm^-1** (1452.810 against 1452.759); the others 0.007 to 0.031.
- max |D_Gamma(prim) - (S_aa + S_ab)| = 9.2e-7, so the folding itself is closed.
- T2 on the same state: `q = 0` through the two-sphere route against the `Gamma` route,
  max |D| difference **4.7e-6** on 0.35 (the Ewald swap residue `GAMMA_AGREEMENT` names on
  silicon, 2.2e-6 there).
- Energies: doubled cell -6.3052369141 Ry against twice the primitive's -6.3052369774, a
  gap of 6.3e-8 Ry. The FFT grids are exactly doubled (24x24x18 and 24x24x36, both grids,
  read on this workstation), so the gap is not the grid; its cause is unmeasured, and the
  ground-state Ewald sum's 1e-7 truncation, with a different `alpha` for the doubled charge,
  is the candidate. This is the floor the folding identity sits on.

Where the 1.5e-5 comes from, measured by the verifier (`/l/ladovj1/review/incompat/I82-ver/tr2.log`):
rerunning `D(q)` with `tr2 = 1e-18` and `threshold_cap = None` moves `D` by 2.5e-6 and the
fold residue stays at 1.64e-5 (1.52e-5 at the default), so it is not the `q` loop's stop.
Swapping the arms gives `|D - (S_bb - S_ba)|` = 1.52e-5 again, the wrong sign `S_aa + S_ab`
is 0.39 away, and the mesh pair 1x1x4 against 1x1x2 reads 1.47e-5 (`mesh.log`). The
remaining candidate is the 6.3e-8 Ry ground-state gap between the two cells, unmeasured as
a cause. The frequencies include imaginary transverse modes (-1098, -786, -696 cm^-1 at
`q`, -1143 and -1032 at `Gamma`), because this toy chain is not relaxed and is not stable
sideways; both routes give them identically, so they test the identity as well as the stable
modes do.

Why this number could have read differently: with the channels added before the LSDA kernel
(the code before this commit) the route did not run at all, a shape error in the kernel's
`jvp`; a spin-blind kernel would differ from the doubled cell by the spin part of `f_xc` acting
on a magnetization response that is not zero in this cell.

`nspin = 1` regression on the same commit (`si.log`): silicon at `L` through the same route,
worst **0.0518 cm^-1** against `ph.x` (`reference.out.ph-si-epsilon-unshifted-dispersion`),
where the slow test's recorded figure is 0.054.

The k-chunked route at `nspin = 1` (run 2, commit `46a859d`, worktree
`/l/ladovj1/w-inc-I82-v2`, `streamed.log`): silicon at `L` with a host store, the same
frequencies to the printed digits, worst 0.0518 cm^-1 against `ph.x`, the induced density
`(2, 3, 1, 20, 20, 20)` as before. The non-slow tests selected by
`-k "phonon or elph or lsda or sternheimer or two_sphere"` (`fast.log`): 81 passed, 18
skipped (reference-format skips in `test_basis.py` and `test_geometry.py`, not this change),
52 s.

Unit refusals on D22 (`unit.log`): `tests/unit/test_two_sphere_refusals.py` and
`tests/unit/test_metallic_q_refusals.py`, 27 passed, no skips.

## What was missing

The entry's "the k+q ground state, projector and response density per channel, plus the
Gamma assembly's spin axis" was mostly wrong. The `k + q` diagonalisation (`at_kpoints`), the
two-sphere projector and solve (`TwoSphereSolver`, every mask indexed by spin), the bare term
(`applied_at_q`, one `D` per channel), `induced_perturbation_at_q` (`field[spin]`), the
assembly (`response_force_constants`, contracting the spin axis) and the frozen Hessian (the
`Gamma` route's, validated at `nspin = 2`) all walked the channel axis already. What was
missing, as found in the code:

1. `TwoSphereSolver.response_parts_at_q` added both channels into one sum and
   `finish_response_at_q` returned `(1, ...)`, so the LSDA kernel's `jvp` in
   `induced_potential_at_q` met a one-channel tangent against a two-channel density. Now one
   sum per channel, `(nspin, ...)`, which the k-chunked route's `_finish` also goes through.
2. `dynamical_matrix_at_q` built its solver through `make_sternheimer` without
   `spin_polarized=True`, a second refusal behind the first.
3. `kpoints_plus_q` built the `k + q` set with `KPoints.from_cartesian`, which renormalises to
   the unpolarized convention, so at `nspin = 2` the weights came back doubled (the trap
   list's caller-built k-set). It now takes `for_spin` back from the set it came from. Nothing
   at `k + q` reads those weights today, so this was latent.
4. `hartree_at_q` still returns one channel; the broadcast onto both is right for a collinear
   density (the Hartree term is spin-blind) and is left as it is.

Trap check (the skill's list, on the diff from `126d6ec`): no hit. The one caller-built
k-set (`kpoints_plus_q`) goes through `for_spin`; no `abs`, no `np.asarray` on a traced value,
no dtype literal, no hand-rolled walk over k (the per-channel sums are `sum_k`); the
per-channel contraction was re-derived against `incdrhoscf.f90`'s `drhoscf(:, current_spin)`;
the check is a per-mode matrix against an independent route, not an atom-sum identity; the
spinor message was written for this route rather than copied.

Added insurance: at `nspin = 2` the loop calls `efield._require_a_finite_kernel` on each
induced potential, as the field response does, so a non-finite LSDA kernel raises by name
instead of running the budget. (The first version of this record went on to say that a GGA at `nspin = 2` was admitted
unmeasured; that was refuted, and the GGA is now refused, below.)

## The GGA refusal (fix round)

A verifier measured the `q` route under PBE on the same identity
(`/l/ladovj1/review/incompat/I82-ver/pbe.log`, `input_dft = 'pbe'` added to both cells; both
SCFs and both loops converged): max |D(b3/2) - (S_aa - S_ab)| = **7.97e-3 Ry/bohr^2** and the
worst frequency partner **60.55 cm^-1** off, against 1.52e-5 and 0.051 cm^-1 under the LDA,
with no error raised. The fault is not spin: on a dimerized H2 chain at `nspin = 1` (H at 0
and 1.5 bohr, `c = 4`, 1x1x8, against `c = 8`, 1x1x4) the LDA reads 3.36e-5 (`h2-lda.log`)
and PBE **1.33e-2 Ry/bohr^2, 77.08 cm^-1** (`h2-pbe.log`), where the PBE fold at `Gamma`,
|D(0) - (S_aa + S_ab)|, is 4.8e-4, 30 times smaller, so the error is in the `q` route.

The term, read in the code (not measured term by term): `induced_potential_at_q` takes the
`jvp` of `v_scf` on the *periodic part* of `drho e^{iqr}`. Under the LDA that is the whole
kernel, since `f_xc drho` is pointwise. Under a GGA the kernel differentiates the field, and
every gradient of `drho e^{iqr}` is `e^{iqr} (grad + iq) drho`; `dgradcorr.f90` carries those
through `fft_qgradient` (lines 63, 98) and `fft_qgraddot` (line 199), both called with
`xq`. The `jvp` on the periodic field has no `iq`. This is a pre-existing defect of the
`nspin = 1` route, not of I82, and it belongs in `OPEN.md`: no GGA test at `q` existed in
`tests/regression/test_phonons_at_q.py` or `test_electron_phonon.py`, and every input the
`q` tests use is LDA (`*.pz-vbc.UPF`).

The fix is a refusal, at every `nspin`, in `require_a_two_sphere_regime`, on
`calculation.functional.is_gradient` (QE's `dft_is_gradient`), placed after the meta-GGA
refusal so `tb09`/`bj06` keep their own message. The electron-phonon coupling inherits it
through `dynamical_matrix_at_q`. Tests, all at gate speed in
`tests/unit/test_two_sphere_refusals.py`: the stand-in at `nspin = 1` and `2`
(`test_each_sibling_refusal_still_fires[gga]`, `[lsda-gga]`), the real `Functional` objects
for PBE, PBEsol and revPBE at both `nspin`
(`test_a_real_gradient_corrected_functional_is_refused`), and the real PZ passing
(`test_a_real_lda_functional_passes`), which is what shows the stand-in's flag name is the
real one.

On D22 (commit `ddb4bb1`, worktree `/l/ladovj1/w-inc-I82-v3`; the later commit only shortens
the message), the refusal on real calculations, no ground state (harness
`/l/ladovj1/review/incompat/I82/gga2.py`, log `gga2.log`): `input_dft = 'pbe'` and `'pbesol'`
on `h3-chain-lsda-fixed.in`, its x2 (both `nspin = 2`) and `si-epsilon-unshifted-nosym.in`
(`nspin = 1`) are each refused by `dynamical_matrix_at_q` with the GGA message, and the same
three files under their own PZ headers pass the guard. (The first try, `gga.log`, used the
symmetric `si-epsilon-unshifted.in` and met the small-group refusal first, which is that
guard doing its job, not this one.) `tests/unit/test_two_sphere_refusals.py` plus
`test_metallic_q_refusals.py`: 37 passed, no skips (`unit3.log`); the non-slow
`-k "phonon or elph or lsda or sternheimer or two_sphere or kpoints" tests/unit`: 97
passed, no skips (`fast3.log`, 26 s). The LDA `nspin = 2` identity rerun at the final code commit
`0168fdd` (worktree `/l/ladovj1/w-inc-I82-v4`, log `/l/ladovj1/review/incompat/I82/h3-fix.log`,
one run, 220 s): max |D(b3/2) - (S_aa - S_ab)| = 1.522e-5, the worst partner at `q` 1452.810
against 1452.759 cm^-1 (0.051), max |D(0) - (S_aa + S_ab)| = 9.2e-7, T2 4.68e-6, the same
digits as at `5814481`.

An observation of the verifier's, not a finding: on the H2 LDA cell the worst frequency
partner is 0.2965 cm^-1 at a matrix residue of 3.4e-5, above the slow test's
`MAGNETIC_FOLDING_FREQUENCY` of 0.2, which is tuned to the H3 cell. The matrix assertion
(1e-4) is the one that carries the check.

Refusals kept, with sharpened messages: a spinor (the spinor pair density across two spheres
with four components, the 2x2 `D` across the two spheres' projectors, and the `Gamma` spinor
phonon itself refused); a spin-polarized metal (the smeared two-sphere projector with two
channels has not been run against anything; not measured here, so not lifted);
`_require_one_channel_when_walked` for the k-chunked route (its frozen Hessian is the `Gamma`
walked passes, which refuse `nspin = 2`); and a new `nspin != 1` guard in
`electron_phonon_at_q`, which had none of its own and inherited the old blanket refusal
(`elphsum_simple` counts one channel).

## OPEN.md (for the integrator)

A new entry: the phonon at `q != 0` under a GGA was wrong at `nspin = 1` before I82
(1.33e-2 Ry/bohr^2, 77 cm^-1 on the H2 chain against its doubled cell), now refused; the
missing term is the `iq` in the GGA kernel's gradients (`dgradcorr.f90`'s `xq`), and lifting
the refusal is that term plus the same supercell identity under PBE, which is the check the
verifier's `ver.py` already runs.

A second open observation, not I82's: the PBE fold at `Gamma` on the same H2 chain,
|D(0) - (S_aa + S_ab)| = 4.8e-4, is 125 times the LDA's 3.8e-6, and no test here checks a GGA
phonon at `Gamma` against `ph.x` (`tests/regression/test_gamma_only.py`'s PBE case compares
the half sphere with the whole sphere, the same code twice). So the refusal message and the
guide text do not offer "a supercell at Gamma" as the way around it; they say "use an LDA
functional" only.
