# I5. Linear response with DFT+U at Gamma: partial, the refusal stays

Branch `incompat/I5`. The field response with a Hubbard U (dielectric tensor and Born
charges) is written and runs, its own terms agree with `ph.x`, and it is still refused,
because the check it was to be released on misses `ph.x` on this cell by more than the
U's terms can be told apart from, and it misses by the same amount with no U at all. The
Gamma phonon with a U is not written and is refused as before.

`tests/data/qe/reference.out.ph-bn-ldau-epsilon-us`, `-paw`, `reference.out.ph-bn-epsilon-us-noU`
and the three `pw.x` outputs beside them. Each number is a single run (the response is
deterministic; no median is involved).

## The finding against master, not against I5

The same BN cell with **no U at all**, where none of the new code is used, misses `ph.x`
by more than the U run does: epsilon xx 2.169639 against 2.167560 (2.1e-3), zz 1.238383
against 1.237329 (1.05e-3), Z* worst 2.6e-3 (B zz 0.299845 against 0.30243), with the
ground-state energy exact to 1e-8. It is the same under LDA (2.3e-3 and 9.8e-4), the same
with `nosym` and `noinv` on both sides (2.08e-3 and 1.05e-3, so not the hexagonal
directional symmetrisation), and the ultrasoft and PAW cells with the U shift by the same
1.35e-3 and 7.8e-4 to 2e-5, so it is not the augmentation tables and not the GGA kernel.
`tr2` is not it either: 1e-14 and 1e-16 move epsilon by 3e-6 in xx and 2.7e-5 in zz on
our side and by nothing on `ph.x`'s. What is left that the two datasets share is the cell itself, a monolayer in
20 bohr of vacuum at `ecutwfc = 30` with psl datasets whose N file suggests 44 Ry. This
wants its own entry; until it is found, the U's terms cannot be checked against `ph.x`
directly to better than about 7e-4, which is why the refusal stays.

## What was missing, as found in the code

The entry named one term (`adddvhubscf`: the self-consistent `dns` and its `dV`). There
were four for the field response, and the entry's `dnsbare` belongs to the phonon only.

1. The unperturbed operator of the solve had no Hubbard term: `dielectric_tensor` called
   `calculation.hamiltonian(v_scf, ddd_paw)` without the converged `ns`, so the states
   were not its eigenstates. Now `hubbard_terms(ns)` goes in (`ns=` argument,
   `Calculator.get_dielectric_tensor` forwards `result.ns`).
2. The velocity `[H, r]` is built from (`commutator_Vhubx_psi`) had no Hubbard term:
   `VelocityOperator` already carries `dwfcU/dk` once handed `ns`, and was not handed it.
3. `adddvhubscf` with `dnsq_scf` and `sym_dns`: `dns` is one `jvp` of the raw occupation
   matrix along `dpsi`, averaged as a polar vector
   (`NsSymmetry.apply_directional`, new), its potential one `jvp` of `hubbard_potential`,
   applied as `|wfcU> dv <wfcU|psi>` (`efield._HubbardResponse`). Rebuilt each pass from
   the previous pass's `dpsi` and not mixed, as `dfpt_kernels.f90:269` does.
4. For Z*: `forces.energy.energy_at` measured `ns` through the scalar group average,
   which projects the field's `dns` to zero on BN (E' and A''2 hold no invariant), so the
   term `f''(ns) dns_E dns_u` vanished silently. `energy_at` and `frozen_energy` take an
   `ns=` override now, and `born._hubbard_ns` builds it raw plus an offset with the
   field's share made full-zone, the `_full_zone_field_response` trap one level over.
   The bare `dns/du` (`dnsbare`) is the builder's own tangent and is not written.

The phonon needs, on top: the bare `dV_hub/du` in `_bare_displacements` (through
`moved.hubbard_terms(ns)`), a per-mode `dns` with `dnsbare` in the self-consistent loop,
symmetrised in the pattern basis, the `ns` override in `_force_constants`, and the
multiplier response. Its unmet check is `reference.out.ph-bn-ldau-epsilon-us:1509-1514`,
52.860373, 52.860373, 59.720250, 851.448923, 1366.914112, 1366.914112 cm-1 (7.5; the
6.5 benchmark reads 53.263278, 60.936434, 851.502844, 1366.905789).

## Where the refusal lives now

- `response/sternheimer.py:require_a_sternheimer_regime` takes `hubbard=False`; with
  the default it refuses as before (the message now names `dnsbare` and the Hubbard
  dynamical matrix), and with `hubbard=True`
  `require_a_hubbard_response_regime` refuses a metal, non-atomic projectors,
  `nspin = 2`, spinors and `lda_plus_u_kind = 1`.
- `response/efield.py:HUBBARD_FIELD_RELEASED = False` refuses the field response with a
  U, the message quoting the 1.3e-3 and the no-U 2.1e-3. Releasing it is that one flag.
- Test: `tests/regression/test_hubbard_response.py::test_the_responses_with_a_hubbard_u_are_refused_by_name`
  (unit, no SCF) asserts both refusals and the argument checks;
  `::test_the_hubbard_terms_of_the_field_response_are_ph_x_s` (slow) sets the flag and
  asserts the decoded `dns` to 1e-4 and the U's change of Z* to 5e-4.

## User guide and README

Nothing to change while the refusal stays. The sentence in `docs/guide/phonons.md`'s
Limits note, "It also gives an error for a Hubbard $U$ and for a potential-only meta-GGA
(`tb09`, `bj06`), which has no total energy to differentiate.", stays true. If the
integrator wants the reason in it, the replacement is: "It also gives an error for a
Hubbard $U$, because on the BN cell of `ph.x`'s own check the dielectric constant misses
`ph.x` by $2\times10^{-3}$ even without the $U$, so the $U$'s terms cannot yet be checked
directly, and for a potential-only meta-GGA (`tb09`, `bj06`), which has no total energy
to differentiate." The README feature-table row "**Dielectric constant** and **Born
effective charges**" (README.md:694) is unchanged.
