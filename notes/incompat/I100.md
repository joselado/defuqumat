# I100. Real-time propagation x DFT+U

Status: done for a collinear (`nspin` 1 or 2), norm-conserving DFT+U ground state with
`atomic` or `norm-atomic` projectors, at a frozen potential and a frozen occupation matrix,
through `propagate`, `propagate_orders`, `run_realtime` and `run_realtime_dielectric`
(so `Calculator.get_realtime` and `get_realtime_dielectric`, whose `_call_options` passes
`ns` to any entry point with that parameter). Refused by name, each with a test: no `ns`
(the other harmonic routes: `get_hhg`, the perturbative orders and the third harmonic, the
frequency-domain hierarchy), `ortho-atomic` projectors, spinors, ultrasoft and PAW, and a
U with `potential = 'hartree'` or `'hxc'`.

## The number

The identity of `tests/regression/test_realtime_regimes.py::test_the_first_order_is_the_kubo_sum_plus_the_band_curvature`
with a Hubbard U: `sigma_RT(z) = sigma_Kubo(z) + i D/(Omega z)`, where `sigma_RT` is the
first order of a kick (`propagate_orders`, one `jvp` in the amplitude), `sigma_Kubo` the
resolvent sum over every eigenstate of the dense `H(k)` with the Hubbard term, with the
velocity of `VelocityOperator(calc, v_scf, ns=ns)` (the optical conductivity's operator,
which differentiates `Calculation.at_kcart` and so rebuilds `wfcU` by the radial
transform), and `D` a central second difference (step 3e-4) of the occupied dense
eigenvalues with `wfcU` rebuilt at each `k`. The real-time side builds `wfcU` from a
Chebyshev table; the two share only the definition of the projectors.

Cell `tests/data/qe/si2-ldau-nc.in`: two-atom silicon, `Si.pz-vbc.UPF`, `ecutwfc = 12`,
`HUBBARD {atomic}`, `U Si-3p 6.0` eV; at the superposition of atomic charges, with
Hund's-rule `ns` (`n_m = 1/3` on the 3p block, so `v = U (1/2 - 1/3)` = 0.0735 Ry), on two
points of no symmetry, `(0.1, 0.05, 0.02)` and `(0.25, 0.1, -0.05)` in `2 pi/alat`;
`eta = 0.02` Ha, 30 frequencies on 0.02 to 0.6 Ha, duration `22/eta`. Figure:

The convergence parameter is the step. The step's share, the difference from the
`dt = 0.025` value, is 5.2e-9 at `dt = 0.1` and 2e-10 at 0.05: a factor of about 25 on a
halving, the fourth-order Taylor step's `dt^4` (16) to `dt^5` (32). It falls onto a floor
of 8.07e-7 that does not move with the step and moves by 7 per cent when `eta` goes from
0.02 to 0.05 (so it is not the window, which is `exp(-22)` either way); with a negligible
U the same floor reads 3.1e-7. I did not separate it further. The curvature stencil (a
second difference at 3e-4) is the likeliest owner, and it is the same in the
norm-conserving test that reads 5e-5 as its tolerance. The test tolerance is **2e-6**,
2.5 times the converged floor; the two A/B arms read 4.5e5 and 3.5e5 times above it.

## What was missing

The entry says the missing term is "the Hubbard projectors' row at `k + G` moving with
`k + kappa` at a frozen `ns`". That was half of it. **The propagated chunk carried no
Hubbard term at all**: `_Chunk.build` made its template with
`row.hamiltonian_from(terms)[channel]`, whose `hubbard` is `None`, so with the guard lifted
the states would have been propagated without `V_U` even at `kappa = 0` (the "term dropped"
row above, 0.69). And nothing on the real-time path could build `wfcU` at `k + kappa`
inside the kept block: `atomic_wavefunctions` needs the pseudopotentials, which cannot ride
on a pytree argument.

What was added:

- `defuqumat/realtime/radial.py`: `atomic_table` and `atomic_layout`, the Chebyshev table
  of `chi_l(q)/q^l` laid out as `atomic_wavefunctions` orders its columns (the `_fit` of
  `radial_table`, shared).
- `defuqumat/realtime/hubbard.py`: `HubbardMotion`, the table, the `i^l` phases, the atom
  gathers, the Hubbard columns and the frozen `v` per channel, carried on the chunk as an
  argument of the kept block. `wfcU(k + kappa)` is `phi` for `atomic` (norm-conserving,
  `S = 1`) and `phi / |phi|` column by column for `norm-atomic`.
- `defuqumat/realtime/propagate.py`: the chunk's `hamiltonian`, `kinetic_nonlocal` and
  `band_form` carry the term at `k + kappa`, so the step, the energy and the current's
  `jax.grad` all hold it; the spectral bound adds the extreme eigenvalues of
  `G^(1/2) v G^(1/2)` to the nonlocal ones (Weyl); the checkpoint signature includes `ns`.
  `ns=` on `propagate`, `propagate_orders`, `largest_stable_step`, `_prepare`, and in
  `defuqumat/workflows/realtime.py` on `run_realtime`, `run_realtime_dielectric` and the
  fixed-density solve behind them.

Memory: per k-chunk the orbitals of the whole crystal at `k + kappa`,
`16 nk_chunk npwx natomwfc` bytes, twice a step; `natomwfc` is 8 on this cell. No timing
against Elk was taken.

Why `ortho-atomic` stays refused: the Löwdin `O^(-1/2)` goes through `eigh`, and
`_inverse_sqrt`'s custom rule repairs the first derivative only. A kick's first order
already differentiates the current (a `grad` in `kappa`) inside a `jvp` in the amplitude,
which is a second derivative of `O^(-1/2)` and differentiates `eigh` inside the rule,
with `1/(w_i - w_j)` at the degeneracies symmetry puts in `O` (`O` at Gamma on two-atom
silicon holds two threefold blocks). A matrix function without an eigendecomposition
(Newton-Schulz, say) would lift it; it is not done.

## A known defect met on the way, not fixed here

This is `OPEN.md`, "Found in passing, 2026-10-06", the item "The Hubbard projectors have
the first-order form of the same defect, and nothing repairs it", measured again here on a
norm-conserving cell. That item ends "the real-time refusal of DFT+U waits on it"; it does
not any more, since the real-time route builds `wfcU` from the table, whose solid-harmonic
form has the tangent at the origin (the chunk's current agrees with a central difference
outside the guard to 1e-8, below). The integrator may want to strike that clause.

**`VelocityOperator` with `ns` is wrong at Gamma**, and so is every velocity-based
response with a U on a mesh containing Gamma (the optical conductivity, and anything
else that reads `dH/dk` with the Hubbard term). The Hubbard projectors are differentiated
through `atomic_wavefunctions`, whose `modulus` and `real_spherical_harmonics` both guard
the `k + G = 0` row by zeroing, so the `l = 1` tangent there is lost: the trap
`pseudo/projectors.py:_with_origin_rows` repairs for the beta functions and that was
never carried to the atomic orbitals. Measured on the cell above at Gamma, on random
states, `<dH/dk_x>`: the table 0.41777899, `VelocityOperator` 0.41577097 (0.48 per cent
off); a central difference of `<psi|H(k)|psi>` with `at_kcart` gives 0.41777898 at a step
of 1e-3 and 0.41577097 at 1e-4, where `|k + G|^2 = 1e-8` is inside the guard. In the
identity on `KSET` (Gamma and one generic point) it reads **1.638e-2 at `dt` = 0.1 and
0.05 alike** (`harness1.log`, commit `b6609f25`), against 1.626e-6 with U = 1e-8 on the
same k-set (`harness2.log`). That is why the check runs off Gamma. The fix is
`_with_origin_rows` with `origin_series` of the orbitals in `atomic_wavefunctions`, which
moves the ground-state Hubbard path's tangents and its primal on rows inside the guard;
it is that `OPEN.md` item's repair, not this unit's.
