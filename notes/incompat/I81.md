# I81. Phonon at `q != 0` x ultrasoft or PAW (and the electron-phonon coupling on it): partial, refusal kept

No number. The refusal stays, now fired by a test, and its message is resized
to what the route actually lacks, which is a second implementation rather than
the four terms the entry names. Marked partial and not blocked: the refusal is
size, not physics, and `ph.x` does it.

**Where the entry undercounted.** Its four terms (`addusddens`'s `Q_ij(q+G)`,
`adddvscf`'s `int3` with `e^{iqr}`, `dvanqq`'s `int1`/`int2`, the multipliers)
are the `q`-specific half. Read against `PHonon/PH` and the code around the
guard, the route lacks two more things:

- **All of P39's orthogonality set, at `q`.** The Gamma route carries
  `dpsi^ort`, `drhous`, `becsumort`, `dLambda` and `dbecsum` in the assembly
  (`phonon._whole_k`); `phononq.dynamical_matrix_at_q` carries none of them. At
  `q` they are cross-sphere objects: `compute_drhous.f90:99-114` builds `vkb`
  at `ikq` and pairs `evq` at `k+q` with `evc` at `k`, so the Gamma code's
  `overlap_derivatives`, which pairs `becp` at one k-point, cannot be reused.
  `drhodvus.f90:81-84` integrates `dvscf` against that `drhous` in the
  assembly, and `drhodvnl.f90:90-145` contracts `deff` and `int1`/`int2`
  against `dbecq`: neither has a counterpart on the `q` route.
- **A frozen Hessian that depends on `q`.** `frozen_force_constants` rests on
  "`dynmat_us` is the same matrix at every `q`", read in `dynmat_us.f90:105-124`.
  That holds for the norm-conserving routine only: `dynmat_us.f90:236` then
  calls `addusdynmat`, whose `int2` and `int5` terms (`addusdynmat.f90:141-195`)
  are built in `dvanqq.f90:106-191` from `vlocq(G+q)` of the moving atom `na`
  and `Q_ij(G+q)` (`qgmq`, via `setqmod`) of atom `nb`, with the phase
  `eigqts(na) conj(eigqts(nb))`. They enter `dynwrk(nu of nb, nu of na)`,
  off-diagonal in the atom. So with every response term written, the frozen half
  would still be wrong at `q != 0` for an augmented dataset.
- **For PAW, the one-centre response at `q`** (`drhodvus`'s `int3_paw`), on top.

**Why this is a second implementation.** At Gamma every augmented term falls out
of one `jvp` of the force's gradient (P39, `phonon._force_constants`), and the
three wrong answers on the way there (-504.3 against +513.3 cm^-1, the diagonal
multiplier's 1.7e-2 sum-rule residue, the 0.26 column) were caught because that
`jvp` had a finite difference to agree with. At `q != 0` there is no cell energy
to differentiate: `response_force_constants` is the hand-transcribed `drhodv`
contraction, so every term above is a transcription with nothing to cross-check
it except the end-to-end identity. That did not fit the stop rule, and no part
of it was started.

**The check, for whoever does it.** A commensurate `q` (`(1/2, 0, 0)` on
two-atom ultrasoft silicon, `si-epsilon-us.in` with `nosym`, `noinv` and an
unshifted grid) against the Gamma phonon of the doubled cell through P39's
route, which is validated against `ph.x` (`reference.out.ph-si-epsilon-us`).
Insulator only, since I86 refuses an ultrasoft metal at Gamma. A cheaper
intermediate check is `q = 0` through the `q` route against P39 on the same
cell, which tests every term except the phases.
