# I121. Spiral `dE/dq` x tabulated cross augmentation

**Status: done.** The refusal in `Calculation.at_spiral_q` (`defuqumat/scf/driver.py`, the
`rebuild_basis = False` branch, "dE/dq for a spiral whose augmentation charge is tabulated is
not implemented") is gone. `dE/dq` on a cell whose stored `Q_ij(G)` is over
`DEFUQUMAT_AUG_MAX_BYTES` now runs through the tabulated displaced table and gives the stored
route's gradient to the four-point stencil's interpolation error, 3.8e-11 Ry per unit of `q`
at the default `AUG_DQ = 0.01` on the ultrasoft oxygen chain, against 1.78e-04 that the
missing term is worth there.

## What was missing

The entry's account is right, and it is smaller than "a missing term": nothing in the
physics or the differentiation was missing. `_build_tabulated_augmentation` sizes its radial
table to `qmax = cell_factor sqrt(ecutrho) + |b|`, with `|b|` read by
`float(np.linalg.norm(shift))` on the host, and under `jax.grad` the shift `-q` is a tracer,
so the read cannot happen. The rest of the tabulated route (the stencil, the chunked scans,
the phases at `G - q`) was already traceable in `q`.

The fix is a host-side `shift_extent` argument on `build_augmentation` and
`_build_tabulated_augmentation` (`defuqumat/pseudo/augmentation.py`), and `at_spiral_q` passes
`|spiral_qcart|`, the modulus of the wavevector the frozen sphere and the calculation's own
table were built at. The knots are then exactly those of the untraced build at that
wavevector. A traced `q` reads `|G - q| <= G_rho + |q - q0|`, inside the table for
`|q - q0| < (cell_factor - 1) G_rho`, one `G_rho` at the default `cell_factor = 2`; past that
the stencil returns NaN (by the module's design, never a clamp). A relaxation never gets
there, since it takes the gradient on the frozen basis and moves with a rebuilt one, so `q0`
follows the walk; a NaN gradient is the symptom if the frozen-basis path is driven by hand
that far. Without `shift_extent` a traced shift still fails with JAX's concretization error
on the host read, and a fast test asserts that.

## The number

The check is the stored route (`Q_ij(G - q)` assembled from the radial transform on the whole
sphere, the route every validated spiral gradient was measured on) against the tabulated one,
from the same converged state, with the knot spacing `AUG_DQ` as the convergence parameter.
Harness: `notes/incompat/I121-harness/harness.py`, run on this workstation on one core
(`taskset -c 8-11`, `OMP_NUM_THREADS=1`), `k_batch = 1`; the log is `chain.log` beside it.

**The cell is the ultrasoft oxygen chain, `tests/data/qe/o-chain-spiral-us.in`
(`q = (0, 0, 0.25)`), and not the silicon cell `AUGMENTED_SILICON` of
`test_spiral_relaxation.py`.** On the silicon cell the seeded moment converges away, the
transverse `becsum` is zero, and holding the displaced table at `q0` under the traced `q`
moves the gradient by 4.4e-16 (`silicon.log`): an agreement there would have been read with
the term missing, so it is not a check. The silicon numbers (tabulated against stored
1.6e-09, 8.3e-11, 7.4e-12 at `AUG_DQ = 0.04, 0.02, 0.01`) measure the interpolation of the
rest of the table only.

On the chain (transverse moment `sum |m_x| / N = 0.31`):

| | `dE/dq_z` (Ry per crystal unit) | against stored |
|---|---|---|
| stored | 3.10043422e-03 | |
| displaced table held at `q0` (the term switched off) | 2.92235173e-03 | 1.78e-04 |
| tabulated, `AUG_DQ = 0.04` | 3.10043525e-03 | 1.03e-09 |
| tabulated, `AUG_DQ = 0.02` | 3.10043411e-03 | 1.03e-10 |
| tabulated, `AUG_DQ = 0.01` | 3.10043425e-03 | 3.77e-11 |

The difference falls by 10 from 0.04 to 0.02 and by 2.7 from 0.02 to 0.01, where it reaches a
floor near 4e-11, the same size as the tabulated gradient's disagreement with its own central
difference below; I did not separate that floor further. At 0.01 the tabulated functional reproduces the SCF total to 4.0e-11 Ry and its
gradient agrees with a central difference of itself (`h = 1e-5`) to 3.4e-11. The converged
difference is 4.7e+06 times below the term that was at stake. Wall time 124 s, peak RSS
1.82 GB, for the SCF, the stored gradient, the switched-off gradient and three tabulated ones.

The table's own derivative, with no SCF (`tests/unit/test_spiral_tabulated_augmentation.py`,
silicon at `ecutrho = 40`, a general `q`, the scalar `Re + 0.3 Im` of `sum_ij` of the
`D_ij` cross integrals of a fixed random potential; harness `unit_numbers.py`): largest
component 110.5, tabulated against stored 5.3e-05, 2.6e-06, 4.1e-07, 8.5e-08 at
`AUG_DQ = 0.04, 0.02, 0.01, 0.005`, falling by 21, 6.4 and 4.8, so it flattens near 1e-7
(relative 8e-10). With the table's `q` dependence dropped the derivative of that scalar would
be zero.

## Tests

- `tests/unit/test_spiral_tabulated_augmentation.py::test_a_traced_shift_without_its_extent_is_still_refused`
  (fast, 6 s): the host read is still there without `shift_extent`, and fails loudly.
- `tests/unit/test_spiral_tabulated_augmentation.py::test_the_tabulated_derivative_converges_to_the_stored_one`
  (slow, about 15 s): asserts a fall of 8 and 4 between the three spacings and
  `< 1e-8` of the scale at 0.01 (measured 3.7e-9).
- `tests/regression/test_spiral_relaxation.py::test_a_tabulated_augmentation_table_gives_the_stored_gradient`
  (slow, replaces `test_a_tabulated_augmentation_table_is_refused`): the chain, tabulated
  against stored at `abs = 5e-10` (thirteen times the converged 3.8e-11, 3.6e+05 below the
  term), the tabulated functional against the SCF total, and a central difference.
- `tests/regression/test_spin_spirals.py::test_what_a_spiral_refuses` (slow): its last block
  asserted the refusal and now asserts that `at_spiral_q(rebuild_basis = False)` takes a
  jitted `q` on the tabulated route and returns a finite `TabulatedAugmentation`; the block run
  as a script (`refuses_block.py`) passes. The docstring paragraph that called this "the one
  that survives" is rewritten. The sibling refusals in that test (`lspinorb`, symmetry beside
  `soc_scale = 0`, a missing `noncolin`) are untouched.

The guard has no sibling of its own: the `nosym` refusal and the `widths` with
`rebuild_basis = False` error in `at_spiral_q` are unchanged, and the magnetic-field refusal of
`dE/dq` lives in `forces/spiral.py` and is not touched.

## Where it ran

Both harnesses ran locally, not on Triton: the chain finished in 124 s at 1.82 GB peak on one
core, inside the brief's local limits (one core, under 4 GB), so no Triton round was needed.
