## The number

The check is the entry's own: at the SCF's own k-points the fixed-density source is the last
SCF iteration over again, so its manifold must reproduce the converged eigenvalues, and the
invariant must be an integer. Cell: `test-suite/pw_scf/scf.in` (two-atom Si, `Si.pz-vbc.UPF`,
12 Ry, two k-points) under `input_dft = 'tb09'`, `conv_thr = 1e-10`, `nbnd = 10`, one run each
(deterministic, no median taken).

| case | largest deviation e_source - e_scf over k and the manifold (Ry) | test tolerance |
|---|---|---|
| norm-conserving, `nspin = 1`, `nocc = 4`, `c = 1.00845` | 1.07e-7 | 5e-6 |
| norm-conserving, spinor (`noncolin`), `nocc = 8`, `nbnd = 20` | 8.92e-7 | 5e-6 |
| PAW `Si.pz-n-kjpaw_psl.0.1.UPF`, `conv_thr = 1e-9`, `c = 1.11513` | 2.48e-6 | 2e-5 |

The tolerance is the self-consistency error the band path already measured on the same cell
(`test_a_band_path_needs_the_converged_tau`, ~7e-7 Ry): the SCF's eigenvalues are those of the
input (mixed) density's potential and the source's of the output density's.

The alternative hypothesis reads differently, which is what makes the agreement evidence
(`alt.log`): the same source with `tau` scaled by 1.01 moves the manifold by 2.95e-3 Ry, by
1.10 by 2.91e-2 Ry, and with the Thomas-Fermi `tau` of the density by 6.87e-2 Ry. So a `tau`
that is wrong by one per cent is 2.8e4 times the measured agreement.

The integers: the `fhs` Chern number on a 6x6 plane (`axis = 2`) is 2.1e-18 (largest plaquette
phase 1.1e-9); the spinor Fu-Kane parity Z2 of the `kz = 0` plane is `nu = 0`, with every parity
eigenvalue +-1 and an even count of -1 at each TRIM (Gamma all +1, as Gamma_1 and Gamma_25' are
even). Both zeros are forced by symmetry (nonmagnetic silicon, no spin-orbit term in the
scalar-relativistic dataset), so these are checks of the machinery under the functional, not
physics; the sharp part is the eigenvalue table. No external reference exists: neither QE nor
Elk computes these invariants, and `pw.x` has no noncollinear meta-GGA.

## What was missing

The entry said "none named; validation only". That was wrong in two places, both in
`defuqumat/workflows/topology.py`:

- `DFTSource` had no `tau` slot, so `calculation.potential` was called without it, and a
  Becke-Roussel potential cannot be built at all without `tau` (the guard sat in front of that
  error). Added `DFTSource.tau`, forwarded through `_source`, `run_berry_curvature`, `run_z2` and
  `run_z2_3d`; `Calculator` supplies it from `SCFResult.tau` through `_STATE_ARGUMENTS` with no
  edit, since it reads the signatures. Run once on D22 (`calc2.log`): `Calculator.from_file`
  on `tests/data/qe/si2-tb09.in` (30 Ry, 6x6x6), then `get_berry_curvature(shape=(4, 4),
  nbnd=10)` and `get_chern(...)` with no `tau` given, both -6.3e-18, largest plaquette phase
  1.1e-8; without the forwarding the call would have raised. The trap-check list was walked
  over the diff and found nothing: every `np.asarray` and `abs` added is host-side in a test,
  no dtype literal, no new walk over k, and the refusal kept for the two siblings is theirs
  word for word.
- `DFTSource._ddd_paw` called `onecenter(becsum)` with no `meta_c`, which raises under a
  meta-GGA (`Calculation.onecenter`), so PAW would have failed even with `tau`. It now takes the
  frozen potential's `meta_c`.

The guard stays for a source without `tau` (now the `nscf.py` form, "pass tau =
scf_result.tau"). The Berry-phase polarization and the orbital magnetization go through the same
source and are **not** lifted: neither was measured here and neither has an entry in
`notes/INCOMPATIBILITIES.md`, so each keeps the old refusal word for word through
`_refuse_potential_only_meta`. Both are candidates for the same check later; the polarization
one is a difference of Berry phases and needs no energy either.
