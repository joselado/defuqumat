# I59. Transverse spin susceptibility (magnons) x noncollinear ground state: partial (collinear spinor lifted, texture refused)

**The number.** The entry's first check, a collinear moment run as a spinor without spin-orbit coupling
against the `nspin = 2` run of the same cell. `h-fcc-magnon.in` with `noncolin = .true.`, `angle1 = 0`
(D22, `w-inc-magnon-v4`, log `review/incompat/magnon/spinor-1.log`, one sample, `ecut_response = 12`):
the two SCFs agree in energy to all ten printed digits and in `m_z` to 1e-8 (`max |m_x|, |m_y|` 8.6e-8);
`lambda_max(X_0 F)` at `omega = 0` over `q = 0, (0,0,1/4), (1/4,1/4,0)` differs by at most **1.1e-5**
(80 spinor bands against 40 per channel) and 3.9e-5 (60 against 30, where the 60 lowest spinor bands are
31 to 32 up-type per k, not 30); `X_0(G=0, G'=0)` at `omega = 0.02` differs by **2.4e-5** relative at both
band counts, a floor set by the two SCFs rather than the band sets. The spinor run's own Goldstone residual
is 0.005745 against 0.005708 at `nspin = 2` (60 and 30 bands), and 0.005375 against 0.005369 (80 and 40). At 60 bands 19 spinor bands were
mixed up and down by the degenerate solver (rule D4), and the numbers are unaffected, as the sum over all
band pairs is a trace over the set. The slow test's body passed on D22 (`slowbodies-1.log`).

**What was missing, and where the entry was right.** The entry's "4x4 spin-density response" is the
missing term for a texture, and it stays missing. Its cheap half is not the spiral (that is MAGNETISM-NEXT D)
but the collinear spinor, which needs no new physics: with the moment along z the `+-` block of the spinor
response is the sum over all spinor band pairs at `k` and `k + q` of
`<psi_n^up| e^{-i(q+G).r} |psi_m^dn>`, term by term the `nspin = 2` sum. Implemented: the guard admits a
spinor run without `lspinorb`, with a magnetization, on a norm-conserving dataset, with no `U` and no field;
`transverse_response` takes the up and down components of one band set (`flip` swaps them);
`spinkernel.collinear_channels` turns `(n, m_x, m_y, m_z)` into the collinear pair and **refuses a texture
where the density is read** (`max |m_x|, |m_y| > 1e-6 max |m_z|`); `exchange_field` of a spinor run is
`B_z` of its own potential (so a `noncollinear_gga` functional is read as the states felt it); the default
band count doubles. The spiral branch of the guard was unreachable behind the noncollinear one and now
fires first with its own reason (`test_a_spiral_is_refused_for_its_own_reason`).

**Why partial.** A texture (Elk's `ncmag` with non-parallel moments) still needs all sixteen blocks of
`genspchi0` and a 4x4 kernel; T4 against Elk tasks 330/331 was not attempted. The guard stays for it, and
for a spinor run with spin-orbit coupling (`test_spin_orbit_coupling_is_refused`, `ni-soc-nosym.in`), an
ultrasoft spinor run (`test_an_ultrasoft_spinor_run_is_refused`, `ni-noncol-111-nosym.in`; the spinor
augmentation pairs components through `becsum`'s spin blocks), a spinor run with a U or a field, and the
texture (`test_a_texture_is_refused_where_the_density_is_read`). `test_a_collinear_spinor_run_is_accepted`
fires the lifted branch.
