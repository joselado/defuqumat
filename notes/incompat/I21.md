# I21. Stress x spin spiral: done (norm-conserving)

**Measured.** NiI chain at `q = b3/4` against its four-cell supercell, both at
one density (as in I20): the stress tensors agree to **7.8e-10 Ry/bohr^3** on a
stress of 2.80e-2 (diagonal -2.8036e-2, -2.7990e-2, -2.4288e-2). One D22 run
(`review/incompat/spiral/v3-nii.log`), and the first run at the SCF's own
states already gave 8.0e-8. Test:
`tests/regression/test_spiral_supercell.py::test_the_spiral_stress_is_the_supercell_stress`
(slow), and `tests/unit/test_stress_machinery.py::test_a_norm_conserving_spiral_strains_both_spheres`
(no SCF: at zero strain the rebuilt `|k +- q/2 + G|^2` is the unstrained one, and
an isotropic strain scales it by `(1 + eps)^-2`).

**What was missing, and where the entry was wrong.** The entry says "q is in
lattice coordinates and turns with the strain", as if that were the missing
term. It is the right thing to hold fixed, not a term to add: a commensurate
spiral is a supercell whose moments sit on its atoms, and straining it carries
the atoms in crystal coordinates, so `q` stays fixed in lattice coordinates
exactly as `k` does. What was missing was only that `at_strain` rebuilt the
kinetic table and the projectors on the `nk` list `_kcrystal @ bg` instead of
the doubled `(k +- q/2) @ bg`. The `'grad_m'` laboratory gradient already read
`q` in lattice coordinates on the strained cell. The nonlocal term is I20's
pair contraction.

**Still refused**: an ultrasoft or PAW spiral (`at_strain` does not rebuild the
displaced table `Q_ij(G - q)` on the strained laboratory sphere; the refusal is
in `at_strain` and in `require_a_differentiable_cell`), a spiral with DFT+U, and
`at_cell` (a moving cell, `_moving = True`) of a spiral, which rebuilds the
projector core the constructor's way on the unshifted list.
`tests/regression/test_stress.py::test_an_input_asking_for_an_impossible_stress_warns_rather_than_raising`
now uses `o-chain-spiral-us.in` as its refused regime.
