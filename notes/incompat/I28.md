# I28. DFT+U x spin spiral: done (norm-conserving SCF)

**Measured.** The NiI spiral at `q = b3/4` with `U = 4 eV` on the nickel 3d shell
(SCF converged in 29 iterations at `conv_thr = 1e-9`), against the four-cell
supercell with the same `U` on its four nickel atoms, diagonalised at the
unfolded density and at the spiral's occupation matrix turned onto each nickel
(`ud` block times `e^{-i q.R_j}`, `du` its conjugate), with the spiral
re-solved at the same `(rho, ns)`:

- the occupation matrix the supercell's states measure equals the turned
  spiral one to **1.2e-10** (transverse blocks of 0.224), so the turn, and with
  it the spiral's laboratory `ns`, is self-consistent;
- the lowest 80 levels at each supercell k-point equal the four folded spiral
  k-points' to **2.3e-13 Ry**.

The turn of the opposite sense misses by 0.24 in `ns` and 3.7e-2 Ry in the
levels. One D22 run (`review/incompat/spiral/v3-hubbard.log`). Test:
`tests/regression/test_spiral_supercell.py::test_the_hubbard_spiral_is_the_supercell`
(slow). The entry said "Test: none": a no-SCF probe on the
workstation confirmed the guard fired (`Calculation` of `nii-chain-spiral.in`
plus a `HUBBARD` card raised "DFT+U together with a spin spiral is not
implemented") before it was changed; the check replaces it.

**What was missing, and how little.** "A Hubbard projector built on each
sphere" is right, and it was already built: `build_hubbard_projectors` on the
doubled `basis_kpoints` gives the `'updown'` spinor set at both `k + q/2` (row
`ik`) and `k - q/2` (row `nk + ik`). `_build_hubbard_projectors` now splices the
up half of row `ik` with the down half of row `nk + ik`. Every column is pure up
or pure down, so nothing is lost, and the Löwdin orthogonalisation is block
diagonal in spin on a norm-conserving dataset. The rest (the occupation matrix,
the energy, `v_ns`, the Hamiltonian term) is the ordinary spinor DFT+U unchanged,
because the energy is invariant under a global spin rotation and the spliced
projector measures the laboratory `ns` of the home cell.

**Still refused**: DFT+U on an ultrasoft or PAW spiral (`_setup_hubbard`: `S`
on each sphere inside the orthogonalisation); the force and stress of a DFT+U
spiral (`forces/energy.py:reject_spinor_spiral`) and its velocity operator
(`response/velocity.py`), which have no check; and the first-order spin-orbit
energy with a `U` (`workflows/spiral_soc.py`), sharpened.

**User guide**, `docs/guide/spirals.md`, Limits: replace "DFT+U on a spiral
gives an error." with "DFT+U on a norm-conserving spiral works, and the Hubbard
projector of each spinor component is built on its own sphere. On an ultrasoft
or PAW spiral it gives an error, and so do the forces, the stress and the
first-order spin-orbit energy of a spiral with DFT+U." `docs/guide/dft-plus-u.md`
has no spiral sentence to change.
