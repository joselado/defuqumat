# I32. Ultracell x DFT+U

Status: done for a norm-conserving, unpolarized cell with `'atomic'` projectors; every other
Hubbard regime is refused by name.

## The number

`tests/regression/test_ultracell_regimes.py::test_a_hubbard_ultracell_bounds_the_hubbard_supercell_from_above`,
silicon with `HUBBARD {atomic}` and `U Si-3p 2.0`, `ecutwfc = 14` (unit cell on `(18, 18, 18)`,
the three-cell supercell on `(54, 18, 18)`, the same box), a 3x1x1 ultracell on the `(1, 2, 2)`
mesh of `k0`, against the explicit 3x1x1 DFT+U supercell SCF of the same modulation at
`conv_thr = 1e-11` and `nbnd = 18` (the check of the entry, exact by the nested basis of P88).

D22, CPU, one core, `review/incompat/ultracell/i32fix/base.log` at `9542257`
(`w-inc-ultracell-v7`), one run, 30 s together with the three neighbouring refusal tests,
which pass:

- tiled null: dV 6.66e-16, density 4.26e-7, total energy -1.42e-14 Ry from the unit cell's,
  the Hubbard energy within 1e-8 Ry of the unit cell's (3.9e-9 apart at `2da8251`, first order
  in `ns` where the total is stationary), and every copy's `ns` within 1.43e-7 of the unit
  cell's `ns`;
- energy above the supercell, per cell, at nbnd = 12, 24, 48: +4.892e-6, +7.900e-7,
  +6.248e-8 Ry, asserted monotone, positive and below 1.5e-7 at 48;
- each copy's occupation matrix against the supercell's, at nbnd = 48: the largest element of
  `|ns_c - ns_sup(d)|`, with `ns_sup(d)` the supercell's `ns` on its two atoms at shift `d`:

  | copy c, shift d | 0 | 1 | 2 |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 0 | 3.58e-7 | 3.52e-3 | 3.51e-3 |
  | 1 | 3.52e-3 | 4.94e-7 | 3.35e-3 |
  | 2 | 3.51e-3 | 3.35e-3 | 4.60e-7 |

  The closest pair of supercell shifts is 3.35e-3 apart, and the copies' mean is 2.05e-3 from
  the farthest of them, so each copy matches its own atoms to 5e-7, four orders below what an
  average or a relabelling leaves. The test asserts each diagonal entry below 0.2 of its row's
  other entries, below 0.2 of the closest pair and below 0.2 of the mean's distance.

What the check tells apart, measured in the same round with the verifier's two mutants of
`defuqumat/ultracell/hubbard.py` (`i32fix/m-avg.log` and `m-phase.log`, worktree
`w-inc-ultracell-v7-mut`, restored clean afterwards):

- one `ns` averaged over the copies: +5.116e-6, +1.028e-6, +3.014e-7 Ry, still positive and
  monotone, so the energy's sign and its fall do not exclude it; it fails the 1.5e-7 bound,
  and its per-copy distance is the 2.05e-3 above;
- the phase of the projection onto a copy with its sign flipped: the energy ladder is
  bit-identical to the correct code, because the same projection feeds the occupations and the
  matrix, so a consistent flip relabels copy `c` as copy `-c`; the per-copy table then has
  rows 1 and 2 swapped (copy 1 at 4.60e-7 from shift 2 and 3.35e-3 from shift 1) and the test
  fails there. So the sign of the phase, which is what labels `ns_c` by its position `R_c`, is
  fixed by the per-copy comparison and by nothing else. The earlier form of this record, and of
  the test's docstring, said that three cells let the energy see a sign error, and that is
  false; three cells are still needed for the per-copy check, since at two `R_1` and `-R_1` are
  the same copy.

The supercell's slots for shift `d` are `2d` and `2d + 1` because `_silicon` writes the atoms
shift by shift, and copy `c` is the shift `R_c = ultracell.q_triples[c]`. The per-copy matrices
are `UltracellResult.hubbard_ns`, `(N, nspin, nslot, ldmx, ldmx)`, added in this round (the
mixed `ns` the reported density goes with, the last output once converged).

## What was missing

The entry's term is right: one occupation matrix per atom copy. What the code needed beyond
it is that nothing has to be built on the box. The supercell Bloch sum of copy `c`'s orbital at
`k0` is `Phi^c = N^-1/2 sum_Q exp(-i (k0+Q).R_c) phi_{k0+Q}`, so each frozen state's projection
onto copy `c` is the unit cell's own `<wfcU|psi>` at the folded points times a phase
(`defuqumat/ultracell/hubbard.py`). Each copy carries its own `ns`, mixed beside the density in
one packed vector, its own `v_ns` minus the reference's in the matrix, and `eth` and the Hubbard
deband per cell in the energy.

## What still refuses

In `require_an_ultracell_regime` (`defuqumat/ultracell/driver.py`), each by name: an ultrasoft
or PAW dataset (the projection carries `S`), a spinor, a collinear spin-polarized cell (added
here: the two channels' `ns` per copy are built, and the only supercell check is unpolarized
silicon), a `TENSOR_MOMENTS` constraint, and any projector but `'atomic'`.
`tests/regression/test_ultracell.py::test_the_hubbard_refusal_fires` fires the `'ortho-atomic'`
and the `nspin = 2` refusals with no SCF.

## User guide

`docs/guide/ultracell.md`, the Limits note. Replace

> DFT+U gives an error, because its occupation matrix is per atom and the long
> cell has $N$ copies of each atom.

with

> DFT+U runs, with one occupation matrix for each of the $N$ copies of each atom.
> It needs a norm-conserving dataset, a cell with no spin polarization and
> `'atomic'` projectors. Other projectors, an ultrasoft or PAW dataset, a
> spin-polarized or noncollinear cell and `TENSOR_MOMENTS` give an error.
