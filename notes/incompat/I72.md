# I72. Residual (Newton) solver x spin spiral: done

**The number.** Harness `review/incompat/I72/harness.py` on D22 (CPU, one core, `w-inc-I72`, log
`review/incompat/I72/harness-1.log`), one sample per cell: `run_scf` with the mixing loop against
`scf_solver = 'newton-krylov'` (`forcing = 0.5`, `warmup = 6`, finite-difference Jacobian, Kerker), both
at `conv_thr = 1e-9`, `mixing_beta = 0.3`, from the same atomic start. The identity checked is that two
solvers of `rho = F(rho)` land on the same fixed point, to `conv_thr`.

| cell | dE (Ry) | dr2(rho_N - rho_mix) | (charge, magnetic) | d int abs(m) | mixing iters | Newton F evals (JVPs) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `h-chain-spiral.in` (NC) | -4.6e-9 | 2.96e-9 | 5.8e-10, 2.37e-9 | 4.5e-4 | 9 | 23 (7) |
| `o-chain-spiral-us.in` (US) | 8.3e-10 | 1.74e-10 | 9.7e-11, 7.7e-11 | -2.8e-5 | 10 | 29 (10) |
| `o-chain-spiral-paw.in` (PAW) | 2.3e-10 | 2.33e-11 | 7.6e-12, 1.6e-11 | -1.9e-5 | 10 | 42 (16) |

`dr2` of the difference is the loop's own measure (`scf_accuracy_split`, Hartree weight on the charge,
constant weight on `m`), asserted below `10 conv_thr`; the energy at `TOTAL_ENERGY_RY = 1e-6`. The moment is
asserted against the Cauchy-Schwarz bound that `dr2` puts on it,
`V sqrt(10 conv_thr / (V/2 x 8 pi/(2 pi)^2))` = 4.8e-3 on these cells: the hydrogen chain's 4.5e-4 is
inside it and outside a naive 1e-4, which is the stopping rule's looseness on `m` and not a different
state (both runs individually at `dr2` 2.3e-10 and below 1e-9). `_weights` (the residual's traceable
occupations) against `Calculation.occupations` on the converged spiral eigenvalues: 0.0 on all three.
Wall time: Newton 3.9, 20.4, 19.4 s against mixing 3.5, 10.7, 11.9 s, first calls included, so not a
timing claim.

**What was missing** (the entry said "unread"). Nothing in the map. The guard was one line with no
reason, and every call in `ScfResidual.step` is the mixing loop's own `Calculation` method (`potential`,
`onecenter`, `hamiltonian`, `diagonalize`, `becsum`, `density`); the spiral lives inside them (the two
spheres at `k +- q/2`, the rotated-frame density, `_addusdens_spiral`'s displaced table). Three things that
could have differed were read and are consistent: the real-space `kerker_preconditioner` at
`nspin_mag = 4` screens channel 0 only and gives `m` the plain `beta`, so the transverse pair on the
displaced sphere is never cut (unlike I74's G-space mixing); `accuracy_of` is `scf_accuracy` on the
resident dense sphere, the same as the loop's `dr2`; and `_weights` and `Calculation.occupations` both
take the unshifted `system.kpoints.weights` with the eigenvalues per unshifted k. The guard is deleted
(`defuqumat/scf/residual.py:make_residual`).

**What stays refused, each with a test**: a spiral under a field or a constraint (the field guard before
it, `test_a_spiral_under_a_field_is_still_refused`), and `jvp = 'sternheimer'` on a spiral
(`_refuse_sternheimer`'s magnetic-noncollinear clause, with `require_a_sternheimer_regime`'s spiral clause
behind it, `test_the_sternheimer_jacobian_is_still_refused_on_a_spiral`). `jvp = 'autodiff'` on a spiral
was not measured; it differentiates the same Davidson and is not refused.

**Tests.** `tests/unit/test_residual_spiral.py`: three fast ones (the packing is `(4,) + grid`, and the
two refusals above; 1.4 s together), and the slow
`test_newton_krylov_finds_the_mixer_s_fixed_point_on_a_spiral[norm-conserving|ultrasoft|paw]`, the

`tests/unit/test_residual_spiral.py tests/regression/test_scf_solvers.py tests/unit/test_fsm_residual.py
-m 'not slow'`, 12 passed, no skips.
