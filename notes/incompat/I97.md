# I97. ALDA kernel x gradient-corrected functional

Status: done for a one-component density and an unpolarized `nspin = 2` one. The refusal of a
gradient-corrected functional in `defuqumat/tddft/kernels.py:alda_matrix` is removed; the
`alda` kernel with a GGA is now the adiabatic GGA kernel with its gradient terms. A meta-GGA and a
GGA on a four-component density keep their refusals, each with a sharpened message and a test.

## The number

The check is the entry's identity: the static Dyson solution with the adiabatic PBE kernel
against the code's own PBE Sternheimer `epsilon_infinity` (`response/efield.dielectric_tensor`,
`screening = 'full'`, one `jvp` of `v_of_rho` with the gradient correction in). Cell:
`tests/data/qe/si-epsilon-unshifted-nosym.in` with `input_dft = 'pbe'` (an LDA dataset under a
PBE functional, which an identity between two routes of the same code does not see), SCF
`conv_thr = 1e-12`. Measured on D22, CPU, one core, one run per point (deterministic, no median),
on the library code of f50c9c18, which the final commit does not change. Logs
`/l/ladovj1/review/incompat/I97/{pbe2,pz2,pbe3,pz3}.log`, harnesses in `notes/incompat/I97-harness/`.

Sternheimer reference, PBE: **21.83184** (`tr2 = 1e-14`; 21.83178 at 1e-16 and 1e-18, so the
reference moves by 6e-5, not the residue). RPA reference (`screening = 'hartree'`): 20.40700.

| 12 | 168 | +0.0261 | -0.2598 | +0.0119 | -0.0032 |

At 60 bands: PBE +0.0273, +0.0226, +0.0112, **-0.0020** (the test's point), -0.0073 at 8, 12, 16,
20, 24 Ry; RPA vs Hartree -0.012 to -0.014 throughout. Against bands at 8 Ry the PBE residue
*rises*: +0.0153, +0.0273, +0.0312 at 30, 60, 90 (run 1 of the earlier implementer, on 328999fc,
reproduced to the last digit on f50c9c18 at 60 and 90).

What this says:

- **The convergence parameter is `ecut_response`, not `nbnd`.** The residue at 8 Ry is a kernel
  that couples the response body to G-vectors outside the sphere (the Sternheimer solve screens
  on the whole dense grid), and band truncation, which is what RPA shows, has the opposite sign.
  The residue falls through zero between 20 and 24 Ry and settles at -0.004 at 90 bands, where the
  RPA pairing's band truncation is -0.0045, so at a converged cutoff the GGA identity is as good as
  the RPA one.
- **The gradient terms are measured, not assumed.** The local part of the PBE kernel alone (the
  truncation the old refusal named) misses by -0.26 to -0.27 at every cutoff, 60 to 120 times the
  converged residue. The full xc screening is 1.42 (21.83 against 20.41), so the gradient terms are
  19 per cent of it.
- **The master LDA identity's residue has the same cause.** `IDENTITY_TOLERANCE`'s comment in
  `tests/regression/test_tddft.py` says what is left at 60 bands is band truncation; the PZ column
  shows it is mostly the response cutoff (+0.0126 at 60 bands and 8 Ry, +0.0003 at 16). I did not
  change that comment; it is for the integrator.

Tolerance: the test asserts the PBE identity at 60 bands and 20 Ry to **1.5e-2** (measured
-0.0020; the 60-band floor at a converged cutoff is -0.0073, with RPA's -0.014 as its upper end),
and at 8 Ry to 4e-2 (measured +0.0273), and the local part's miss below -0.15 (measured -0.271).

Unit checks (gate speed, `tests/unit/test_tddft_machinery.py`, 23 passed on D22 on f50c9c18,
`unit2.log`):

- the column route run on LDA equals ALDA's `G - G'` matrix to 1e-11 relative, which pins the
  phase convention, the `1/N`, the row gather and the `1/nspin` tangent;
- the PBE kernel is Hermitian to 1e-12, is not a function of `G - G'` (differs from its local part
  by more than 1e-3 of its scale), and an unpolarized `nspin = 2` density gives the `nspin = 1`
  matrix to 1.2e-6 of its largest entry (bound 1e-5: PBE's spin branch takes the polarized PW
  correlation, whose `zeta = 0` potential already differs by 2.4e-7 Ry from the unpolarized one);
- the meta-GGA (`tb09`) and the four-component GGA density still raise.

## What was missing

The entry says the missing term is "an adiabatic GGA kernel with the gradient terms (the `dgcxc`
route the Sternheimer stack already differentiates)". That is right about the physics and the
route. What it does not say, and what the code needed, is that such a kernel is **not a function
of `G - G'`**, so it cannot come from one transform of `dv_xc/drho` as ALDA's does. It is built a
column at a time: column `G'` is `dv_xc` applied to `e^{i G'.r}` (two real `jvp` of
`v_of_rho` minus its Hartree term, so the gradient correction is the SCF's `gradcorr` with the core
charge on both grids), transformed and gathered on the response sphere
(`kernels._kernel_columns`, `_gga_columns`, `_adiabatic_gga_matrix`). Cost: `2 nbody` `jvp` of
the potential on the dense grid, one column at a time under `lax.map`; 3.7 to 5.9 s on the first
call per shape on D22 (compile), 0.34 to 0.6 s after, up to nbody = 644.

## Regimes not covered, refusals kept

- A meta-GGA: its potential depends on `tau`, which comes from the states, so `dv_xc/drho` at
  fixed `tau` is not its kernel (the `tau` response is the missing term). Test
  `test_the_alda_kernel_still_refuses_a_meta_functional_and_a_gga_spinor`.
- A GGA on a four-component density: the noncollinear gradient correction rotates onto `|m|`, and
  with a moment the kernel is a 4x4 tensor in `(n, m)` that a charge-only `chi_0` cannot use. Same
  test.
- A polarized `nspin = 2` density stays refused by `refuse_a_polarized_alda`, for every functional,
  as before.

## The guide and the README

`docs/guide/optics.md`, the Limits note, delete:

> The `alda` kernel gives an error with a gradient-corrected or meta-GGA functional, because the
> ALDA kernel is local in the density and a GGA kernel is not.

and replace it with:

> With a gradient-corrected functional, such as PBE, the `alda` kernel is the adiabatic kernel of
> that functional. It keeps the gradient terms. Its static limit agrees with the dielectric
> constant of the Sternheimer solve to 2e-3, if `ecut_response` is 20 Ry. At 8 Ry the difference
> is 3e-2. The `alda` kernel gives an error with a meta-GGA functional, because its potential
> depends on the kinetic energy density of the states. It also gives an error with a GGA on a
> noncollinear density.

README feature table, row **Optical absorption spectra with excitons**: no cell changes. The row
names no kernel and has no footnote, QE has no such route and Elk's tick stays. The README prose
at line 491 ("no adiabatic local kernel can do" it) stays true: the adiabatic GGA kernel also has
no head and no wings, so it cannot bind an exciton either.
