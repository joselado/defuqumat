# I114. `soc_scale` strictly between 0 and 1 x ultrasoft or PAW: done for ultrasoft (the slope at 0 against Hellmann-Feynman to 1.4e-5 meV, the second-order law to the pw.x-validated endpoint); fully-relativistic PAW still refused

**The number.** Tetragonal cobalt, `tests/data/qe/co-tetragonal-relaxed-mae.in` (one atom,
`Co.rel-pbe-nd-rrkjus`, ultrasoft and fully relativistic, 3x3x2 `nosym`, `noncollinear_gga =
'kubler'`, the functional the committed `pw.x` pair uses), every direction one self-consistent
run through `run_relaxed_direction` at `conv_thr = 1e-12` (D22, worktree `w-inc-I114` at
`8865841d`, `review/incompat/I114/harness.py` and `hf.py`, one sample each, about 10 s an SCF on
one core with the kernels cached; logs `run1-*`, `run2-*`, `run3-*`). The convergence parameter
is `s` itself.

The **slope at 0** is checked against an independent route, the Hellmann-Feynman expectation of
the first-order operator `H(1) - H(0)` at the self-consistent `s = 0` states
(`frozen_expectation` on the converged four-component `s = 0` density, turned rigidly, which
at `s = 0` is the `s = 0` state along that axis): `E1(z) = 0.010135`, `E1(x) = 0.011906` meV.
With `D(s) = (E(s) - E(0))/s` and the Richardson estimate `2 D(s) - D(2s)`:

| axis | `E1`, Hellmann-Feynman | Richardson `(0.01, 0.02)` | off | Richardson `(0.0625, 0.125)` | off | ratio |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| z | 0.010135 meV | 0.010121 | 1.4e-5 | 0.009581 | 5.5e-4 | 40 |
| x | 0.011906 meV | 0.011898 | 8e-6 | 0.011532 | 3.7e-4 | 47 |

An error `O(s^2)` predicts the ratio `(0.0625/0.01)^2 = 39`, so the discrepancy falls the way the
approximation says, and the slope of the self-consistent total equals the expectation of the
derivative of the Hamiltonian, which is what a variational blend means.

The **anisotropy** `MAE(s) = E_x(s) - E_z(s)` with `a = E1(x) - E1(z) = 1.771e-3` meV:

| `s` | `MAE` (meV) | `MAE/s^2` | `(MAE - a s)/s^2` |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.01 | 0.0000643 | 0.6434 | 0.46630 |
| 0.02 | 0.0002218 | 0.5546 | 0.46602 |
| 0.0625 | 0.0019271 | 0.4933 | 0.46499 |
| 0.125 | 0.0074646 | 0.4777 | 0.46356 |
| 0.25 | 0.0292355 | 0.4678 | 0.46068 |
| 0.5 | 0.1146657 | 0.4587 | 0.45512 |
| 0.75 | 0.2545797 | 0.4526 | 0.45022 |
| 1 | 0.4473017 | 0.4473 | 0.44553 |

So `MAE = a s + b s^2 + c s^3` with `b = 0.4663` meV and `c` about -0.021 meV, the coefficient
falling linearly in `s`, and the `s = 1` end is the 0.447302 meV that
`test_the_relaxed_anisotropy_agrees_with_pw_x` holds against `pw.x`'s 0.4474 (both totals
bit-identical to that test's, -74.4057695967 and -74.4058024728 Ry). Easy axis `c` at every `s`,
no drift (0.0 degrees), the moment 1.7867 to 1.7837 mu_B. The spin-orbit energy along z,
`(E_z(s) - E_z(0))/s - E1(z)`, is `-6.04 s` meV at small `s` and -5.97 meV at 1, the 0.44 mRy
P115 recorded. A three-parameter fit of `MAE` to the coarse points 0.25, 0.5, 1 alone gives
`a = 2.28e-3` meV, of the size P120 measured on the two-file route (1.79e-3), where the
small-`s` and Hellmann-Feynman route gives 1.771e-3.

**With the term switched off.** `qq_so` left at the coupled overlap at every scale while the
other three sandwiches blend (`harness.py ... qqfull`): the total at `s = 0.02` sits 0.42 meV
below the blend's, so `D(0.02)` reads **-21 meV against the Hellmann-Feynman 0.010**, and
`MAE(0.25)` reads **0.128 meV against 0.0292** (`(MAE - a s)/s^2` = 2.04 against 0.46). The SCF
also takes 41 iterations at 0.02, 30 at 0.5, and does not reach 1e-12 in 400 at 0.25 (2.2e-11,
the z leg; the x leg converged in 13). The refusal's own number, -132 meV at 0.25, is 4500 times
the measured `MAE(0.25)`.

**What the check does not discriminate.** The pre-P118 `becsum`, the `s = 0` map at every scale
below 1 while the other three blend (`harness.py ... old`), moves the totals by only 6.5e-10 Ry
(z) and 1.9e-9 Ry (x) at 0.25, 6e-12 Ry at 0.02 and 1.5e-9 Ry at 0.5, so 1.7e-5 meV on
`MAE(0.25)` and 0.04 per cent on the slope: on this converged state the full and reduced `becsum`
maps differ by very little, and neither curve would have caught it. That alternative is held by
the unit identities in `tests/unit/test_soc_scale.py` (charge and `newd` derivative at 0.5,
which P118 measured 3 and 24 per cent off on the old code).

The error follows the accuracy at every scale, second order in the residual, with one outlier:
the `s = 0.5` run stopping at iteration 12 (`conv_thr = 1e-10`) is 3.1e-9 off, 60 times its
accuracy, and the pre-P118 `becsum` run stopping at the same iteration is 3.07e-9 off its own
`1e-12` total, so it is that iteration of the trajectory and not the functional; the runs at
1e-9 (iteration 11) and 1e-11 (iteration 17) sit at the accuracy.

**What was missing: nothing in the code, and the entry's stated term was wrong.** The entry
names "a blend of the spin-orbit overlap `qq_so`; only 0 and 1 are defined". `qq_so` has blended
as `T + s (F - T)` since P58 (`pseudo/spinorbit.py:SpinOrbitCoupling.qq_so`), `dvan_so` and
`newd_so` blend the same way (`_newd_noncollinear`), `becsum_transform` since P118, the
ultracell's `spinor_ultracell_deeq` too, and the PAW small component is scaled linearly (P117).
The refusal rested on the -132 meV at 0.25 measured with the reduction P115 replaced, which
P115's own record and `OPEN.md` Part XIX item 2 already said, keeping it "until a blend is
measured". The fix is the lifted guard in both places (`scf/driver.py:Calculation.__init__`,
`pseudo/spinorbit.py:SpinOrbitCoupling.__init__`), now refusing only a fully-relativistic PAW
species (`is_paw and has_so`); a scalar-relativistic PAW species, which nothing in the scale
reaches, is admitted.

**Fully-relativistic PAW stays refused, and it is a decision, not a missing term.** Its
one-centre energy carries the Dirac small component's magnetization, which `build_paw` scales by
`soc_scale` linearly as the strength of that term rather than as a blend of two energies; P117
says that is "a definition to revisit with the rest of the intermediate-scale refusal". Taken the
conservative way: the question for the user is whether an intermediate scale on PAW should mean
that linear strength (then the same check on `ni-tetragonal-relaxed-mae-paw.in` would lift it;
the unit test on one `nh = 34` sphere already peaks at 7.5 GB, above a 6 GB D22 slot, so the SCF
needs sizing first) or something else. The message names it.

> For ultrasoft and PAW datasets it must be exactly 0 or 1, because a blend in between is not the
> overlap of any set of projectors. When every dataset is norm-conserving, any value in $[0, 1]$
> is allowed.
