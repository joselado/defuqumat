# I116. Ultracell `seed_from_field` x collinear reference

**Status: done.** `seed_from_field=True` now seeds a collinear (`nspin_mag = 2`) run with
`sign(B) sign(M)`.

**The entry's check is false as stated, as physics.** The entry asks that "the converged
state does not depend on the seed". On the cell measured it does, because the cell has two
self-consistent states under the field. What the seed does is reach the lower of the two,
the one the explicit supercell has lowest. The tiled start reaches the other one.

**The default is unchanged.** A collinear run still starts tiled unless `True` is passed.
That is the response about the reference's own state, and changing the default is the
user's decision (see the question below).

**Refusals kept:**

- `True` on a reference with no moment or a compensated one ("seed cannot be taken").
- `True` with a `seed_magnetization`, or with no field.
- `True` at `nspin_mag = 1`.

**Read aloud.**

- The field's seed converges onto the supercell's lowest state from above, and the gap
  falls by 6.6x over the `nbnd` ladder, the method's convergence parameter.
- At `nbnd = 16` the gap is the same truncation as the zero-field ladder of

- The tiled start lands on the supercell's ferromagnet instead. That is 3.81 mRy per cell

- The smooth hand seed `cos(pi x)` reaches the same state as the field's seed, to 2e-12 Ry.
- The wrong-sign seed `-sign(B)` reaches the supercell's "against" state. At `nbnd = 16`
  it is 1.5e-4 above it.

**The lifted driver route** (`seed_from_field=True`, job 21118983, `i116c.py`) gives the
same numbers to every printed digit:

- 1.5426e-4 and 6.8355e-5 above the aligned supercell at 0.002 Ry;
- 1.6561e-4 and 7.2340e-5 at 0.02 Ry;
- `seed_origin = "magnetic_field"`.

**Which basin the seed finds, and why.** A reviewer in this session read the first round as
a shallow, field-polarized basin, and kept the refusal for that reason. That reading came
from two errors of mine in the first-round harness `i116.py`, not from the physics:

- **The harness halved its energies.** `UltracellResult.total_energy` is already per unit
  cell, and `i116.py` divided it by two again. Its "1.830 and 5.024 mRy below tiled" are
  really 3.66 and 10.05, and its "0.73 and 7.24 mRy" for the wrong sign are 1.46 and
  14.5.
- **The cell moments are not the atoms' moments.** The field's seed reaches the full
  antiferromagnet. Its cell moments, ±0.074, look small only because a unit cell of the
  box straddles two atoms of opposite sign. The same ±0.074 appears for the smooth
  zero-field antiferromagnet in the existing test's docstring ("0.0739 against the atoms'
  0.365").

The doubled energies agree with the full antiferromagnet. Without the field term, the
seeded state is 2.93 mRy per cell below the tiled ferromagnet at `nbnd = 16`, against the
zero-field gap of 3.085 minus that rung's 0.153. The site moments (a 1.5 bohr sphere) are
±0.243 at `B = 0.002`, and `|m_Q| = 0.369` against the zero-field supercell's 0.365. The
wrong-sign state has the same field-free energy to 2e-7. Its `E + E_field` is higher by
1.46 mRy, which is twice `B |m_Q|` (2 x 0.002 x 0.369 = 1.48e-3): the antiferromagnet with
the opposite phase.

The seed's amplitude is the reference's full moment (`|s| = 1`, reversed where `B < 0`),
so it starts at a full-moment domain state. The sphere moments are 0.243 against the
supercell's 0.252. The sphere is a crude partition on two different FFT boxes: the tiled
ferromagnet reads 0.354 against the ferro supercell's 0.368 while their energies agree to
4e-8. So the energy is the number, and the sites only confirm the pattern and the sign.

## What was missing, as found in the code

The entry said "an amplitude or staggered seed". The collinear seed machinery already
existed (`seeded_density` and `seeded_becsum` at `nspin_mag = 2`). What was missing was:

- the scalar form of the field's seed, `seed.collinear_field_seed`;
- the driver branch, which was gated on `nspin_mag == 4`.

**The entry does not mention that the reference's sign matters.** `v_up -= B`, so on a
reference that converged with its moment down, `sign(B)` alone would seed against the field
everywhere. Hence `sign(B) sign(M)`. At a node of the field the seed is 1, as in the vector
form.

## Guide and README

`docs/guide/ultracell.md` does not mention `seed_from_field`, so there is no Limits
sentence to delete. Add one to the Limits note of that page:

> On a collinear cell, `seed_from_field=True` starts the loop with the moment turned over
> where the field is negative. On two-cell hydrogen this start ends in the
> antiferromagnet, which is the lowest state of the supercell under the field. The normal
> start stays in the ferromagnet, 3.8 mRy per cell higher. So if a cell can have two
> solutions under the field, compare `total_energy + field_energy` between the two starts.

field's seed, and QE has no ultracell (`QE n`).

## A question kept for the user

Should a collinear run under a field be seeded by default, as a noncollinear one has been
since 2026-10-10? Kept as the tiled start. The noncollinear default exists because the tiled
start cannot leave a flat manifold there. Here the tiled start converges fine, to a
different state, and that state is the response about the reference's own ferromagnet.
