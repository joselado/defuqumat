# I96. Sum-over-states `chi_0` x metal: done (smeared occupations); tetrahedra stay refused

**The numbers.** Aluminium, `al-conductivity.in` (one atom, `ecutwfc = 15`, `nosym`, whole
unshifted grids). D22, CPU, one core, one sample each, worktrees `w-inc-chi0` and `-v3`, logs in
`review/incompat/chi0/`.

*1. The Drude weight is the optical conductivity's, an identity* (`h1-drude.log`). 4x4x4,
`marzari-vanderbilt` 0.05, `nbnd = 12`, `eta = 0.01` Ry, `omega` = 0.05 to 0.8 Ry: the head-only
`eps = 1 - X_head` of `chi_0` against `OpticalConductivity.dielectric = 1 + 4 pi i sigma/w` with
the intraband term at `gamma = eta` (`relaxation = eta`) on the same states and Fermi level:
**max difference 3.8e-13** on values of order 5 to 680 (`eps_xx(0.05) = -511.53 + 447.07i`).
With the Drude block switched off the same difference is **872**, and the difference between the
two `chi_0` runs is `wp^2/(omega + i eta)^2` with `wp^2` = 2.267354 Ry^2 (20.49 eV, the
conductivity's own number on this mesh) to 5.0e-10 relative. The two expressions are the same
sum rearranged (`W_n(1 - f_m)` against `wg_i - wg_j`, Elk's resolvent pair against
Adler-Wiser's), with the same degenerate-pair partition, so this checks the prefactor and the
partition, not the physics of the intraband term; check 2 is the one that does.

*2. The f-sum rule converges onto the diamagnetic weight* (`h3-*.log`, `h3b-*.log`). The
spectral weight in closed form, `C = -Y^2 X_head(iY)` at `Y = 1e4` Ry (the asymptote `C/z^2`,
correction `(e_max/Y)^2` below 1e-6), against `(8 pi/Omega) sum_k sum_n wg_n <n|d2H/dk_a^2|n>`
from `VelocityOperator.second_matrix_elements` (1.3566 Ry^2, 0.949 of the free-electron 16 pi n =
1.4297, the nonlocal pseudopotential), on the 8x8x8 SCF density with the states solved on each
grid. In the continuum `sum_k w f' (de/dk)^2 = -sum_k W d2e/dk2`, so interband plus Drude is the
diamagnetic weight; on a mesh the difference is the k-sum of `d(f de/dk)/dk`, and with a finite
band set it is the band tail.

Two convergence parameters, each shown at several values and falling as predicted: at the wider
smearing the k-sum is converged to 5e-6 between 10 and 12 (exponential in the mesh, as for a
smooth periodic integrand), and what is left is the band sum, falling as about `nbnd^-1.4`
towards one. At the input's 0.05 Ry the delta is not resolved on any of these meshes (the energy
step `v_F dk` is 0.3 Ry at 8x8x8) and the ratio oscillates by 2 to 4 per cent about the same
value, which is why the check is made at 0.2. **Without the Drude block** the ratio is 0.33 to
0.37 at the converged meshes: aluminium is nearly free-electron, and the intraband term is two
thirds of its spectral weight.

**What was missing, as found in the code.** The entry named the intraband (Drude) term, and that
is half of it. The other half is I95's: the pair list was the occupied-empty rectangle, so a
metal's partly filled bands had no pairs among themselves at all, and `_occupied_bands` would have
refused the state anyway. The changes, all in `defuqumat/tddft/chi0.py`:

- `_band_windows` and `_pairs(low, high, nbnd)`: every `i < j` whose fillings can differ.
- `_pair_terms(..., tol)`: on a smeared run a pair closer than `DEGENERACY_TOL` (the
  conductivity's, 1e-5 Ry) carries no interband weight, the division made safe with `jnp.where`.
  Its two orderings together are finite and equal the Drude form `-2 f' Re(v_a v_b*)/z^2`, so it
  is moved, not dropped.
- `_drude_block`: `(8 pi/Omega) sum_k w_k sum_nm delta(E_F - e_n) [|e_n - e_m| <= tol]
  Re(v_a^nm v_b^mn) / z^2` added to the head of each k-point's matrix, derived as the `q -> 0`
  limit of the `i = j` pairs (the first order in `q` is odd in `v` and cancels between `k` and
  `-k`; the wings are first order and cancel the same way, since `rho_ii(-G) = conj rho_ii(G)`;
  the body is order `q`). The multiplet block rather than the diagonal (rule D4), the same
  partition as `conductivity._plasma_sum`. `delta` is `conductivity._fermi_surface_delta`, the
  smearing's own `w0gauss` at `(E_F - e)/degauss`, one Fermi level or one per channel.
- `ChiZero.smeared`, and a `ValueError` for a frequency with `omega + i eta = 0`, where the Drude
  term is infinite.
- `run_absorption`: on a smeared run the residual's internal point gets the broadening too, and
  the bootstrap kernels and `static_residual=True` are refused (both need the static `eps_M`, which
  for a metal is `-wp^2/eta^2`, set by the broadening). This is the conservative choice of the two
  (the other is to switch `static_residual` off by itself on a metal); the user's to revisit.

The Drude weights are built on the host (`np.asarray` of the eigenvalues), like the existing
`weights`, so no derivative passes through the delta; that is the same level of differentiability
as before, not a new break.

**What stays refused**, each with a test: tetrahedron occupations (the tetrahedron method has no
smeared delta for the Drude weight; `al-tetrahedra`); a scissors shift on a smeared run; the
bootstrap kernels and the static residual on a smeared run; the siblings on smeared cells
(textured spinor `ni-soc-nosym`, spiral `h-chain-spiral`, Hubbard `h-fcc-magnon-ldau`, ultrasoft
`co-hcp-anisotropy-sr`, two-channel wedge). A collinear `nspin = 2` smeared run is refused too
(`h10-chain-lsda`, new message "a smeared chi_0 at nspin = 2 is not implemented"): the window,
the Drude block and the per-channel Fermi level are written for it, but no number was measured,
so it stays refused rather than lifted by composition.
