# I9. Berry-phase polarization x `nspin = 2`: done

The refusal at `defuqumat/workflows/polarization.py:run_polarization` is lifted for
`occupations = 'fixed'` at `nspin = 2`, and checked against two `pw.x` numbers, one of which
can tell the two channels apart.

  Both channel phases are also the molecular identity `-N_s z_c` mod 1 with `z_c = 0.115`

  one count for both channels would read 0.39 or 0.85, and a fold of the sum -0.38. They do
  **not** discriminate which Hamiltonian a channel reads, since any isolated manifold of a
  centrosymmetric molecule centres at `z_c` (the up Hamiltonian's lowest five bands also give
  0.425). So the same test also builds each channel source on the O2 ground state and checks
  its seven lowest eigenvalues at Gamma against `SCFResult.eigenvalues_by_spin[spin]` to 1e-6 Ry,
  with the fifth band split between channels by more than 1e-2 Ry, which a swapped or
  duplicated channel index fails. Test:
  `tests/regression/test_polarization.py::test_triplet_o2_matches_pw_x_channel_by_channel`
  (slow, 23 s on D22, 1e-5 on the phases, the resolution pw.x prints).
- **AlAs at `nspin = 2` converging to `m = 0`** (`alas-raman.in` with
  `starting_magnetization(1) = 0.5`, `tot_magnetization = 0`), the entry's own check, against

## What was missing, against the entry

The entry said "a channel axis on the string loop, two folded phases summed with the
`nspin = 1` doubling applied once". The combination was already written:
`combine_string_phases(..., nspin=2)` folds one channel with no doubling and
`polarization_quantum(nspin=2)` returns 1. **No doubling enters at `nspin = 2` at all**: QE
(`bp_c_phase.f90:759`) doubles only in its `nspin == 1` branch and at `:775-782` folds each
channel onto `[-1/2, 1/2)` and adds them, with no second fold. What was actually missing:

1. **A second guard**, `DFTSource.__post_init__` in `defuqumat/workflows/topology.py`, refused
   every `nspin = 2` source. It is shared with the Chern, Z2 and orbital-magnetization routes,
   whose refusal is correct (two independent invariants) and stays: the source gains a
   `spin` field, refusing at `nspin = 2` only when no channel is named
   (`tests/unit/test_polarization_machinery.py::test_a_two_channel_source_needs_its_channel_named`).
2. **One channel's Hamiltonian per solve**: with `spin` set, `DFTSource.states` diagonalises
   `(hamiltonians[spin],)` only, and the caller-built k-set goes through `for_spin`.
3. **A per-channel occupied count**: `_occupied_bands` is `nelec / 2`, wrong for a magnet. The
   counts are `occupied_counts` (`(NINT(nelup), NINT(neldw))`), which is what
   `bp_c_phase.f90:439` reads off `wg` per channel; `nocc` takes a pair at `nspin = 2`.
4. **The loop over channels** in `run_polarization`, the two sources sharing one `Calculation`,
   `Polarization.strings` holding the up strings then the down ones (pw.x's table order), and a
   new `Polarization.strings_by_spin`. The `clear_caches` period counts (channel, string), so a
   second channel does not halve the mapping bound.

Still refused, by name: `nspin = 2` with `occupations = 'from_input'`
(`test_two_spin_channels_from_input_occupations_are_refused_by_name`), since an input list can
leave a channel with a partly filled band; the metal and spin-spiral refusals are untouched.

AlAs at `tot_magnetization = 2` was tried first as the discriminating case and is unusable:
its three-band down channel cuts the p-like triplet at Gamma, and pw.x prints a string phase
of exactly 0.00000 there (the overlap determinant fell below its `eps`), which `_check_gap`
would refuse on this side.
