## What was missing, as found

The entry said "a rank-3 point-group average; `symmetrize_cartesian_tensor` exists".
That is half of it. QE reduces a non-magnetic grid by time reversal on top of the point
group, and the two consumers' per-k terms transform differently under it
(`response/photocurrent.py:wedge`): the shift-current integrand is even under `k -> -k`
point by point, so a time-reversed image is a plain rotation of the rank-3 tensor; the
SHG coefficient matrices go to their complex conjugates over the same resonance, which
is not a rotation of the summed tensor, so `response/shg.py:_chi_at_k` takes the real
part of its coefficients per k-point on a wedge (`time_reversed=True`), which is the
average of the pair `k, -k`, and then the rotation average completes it. Still refused,
by `system/wedge.py:wedge_group`: a magnetic group, a reduced set on a `nosym` run, a set
the group does not tile (a shifted grid).
