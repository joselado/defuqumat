The convergence parameter is `nbnd`, and the residual does what a truncated sum over empty states
does, which is what P94 showed it did not: negative and falling to zero, by 3.0, 2.2 and 2.9 over
the steps 30, 45, 60, 70, where P94's column rose (+0.25, +0.48, +0.54, +0.59). The
norm-conserving control on the same cell falls by 4.8 from 30 to 60, the ultrasoft one by 6.5.
The control columns are the check that the instrument could have read otherwise: the plain head
reproduces P94's own +0.2497 and +0.5403 to 2e-4, and the body without `Q_ij(G)` reads -2.46 and
-2.04, so both new terms move the number, and only the two together fall onto zero. Raising
`ecut_response` to 16 moves the residual by -0.02 at both band counts, the local-field effect of
the extra G-vectors; the `nbnd` limit at `ecut_response = 8` therefore sits at about +0.01 to
+0.02 rather than at zero (extrapolated, not measured), which is a response-cutoff convergence of
0.04 per cent of 56.65 and not a missing term. The 80-band run did not complete: the Sternheimer
referee raised "the screening kernel dv_of_drho came back non-finite ... For nspin = 2 ..." on
this `nspin = 1` cell, at the same density that passes at 70 bands (not investigated; the
norm-conserving cell at 90 bands was refused by the Davidson room check, 2 nbnd > 169).

The test asserts both columns falling by more than 4 and below zero, and |residual(60)| < 0.08
(measured 0.0522), and the control's +0.2497 and +0.5403 to 2e-3.

**What was missing, as found in the code.** The entry named `Q_ij(G)` in every pair density and
said it did not close twice; P94's note added that the head lacked `-e_n dS/dk` and `dpqq`. The
head was the missing term. P99 (after P94) built the generalised velocity
`VelocityOperator.generalised_matrix_elements`, `<n|dH_a - e_m dS_a|m> + (e_m - e_n) K^a_nm`,
whose `K` is the augmentation dipole (`dpqq` and `<d beta/dk|psi>`), for the conductivity. The
head of `chi_0` is that object divided by `e_i - e_j`, which is the dipole of the true states
`T|psi>`; it is built in the conductivity's two stages (`tangent_elements`, then `connections`,
joined by `with_connections` with the unshifted eigenvalues). The body is P94's three lines:
`spinchi0.augmentation_factors` at `q = 0` on the response sphere, `state_projections` per chunk,
and `sum_ab conj(<beta_a|psi_i>) q_ab(G) <beta_b|psi_j>` added to the pair density before the
Coulomb factor. P40's finding 2 (the head's `dpqq` moved the number by 0.0015) was taken with the
dense-table body, which P94 already showed was the wrong half.

**What stays refused**, each with a test: PAW, now for its own reason (the one-centre Hartree
and exchange-correlation terms are a different term from `Q_ij(G)`;
`test_an_ultrasoft_insulator_is_accepted_and_paw_is_refused_by_name` on
`si-epsilon-paw-unshifted-nosym`); an ultrasoft run that is smeared, polarized or a spinor, for
want of a number (the same test, smeared `si-us-nosym`, and `co-hcp-anisotropy-sr` in the
sibling test). The `alda` kernel on an ultrasoft run is reached through this path and was not
measured; the RPA identity above is what is checked.

**User guide**, `docs/guide/optics.md`, the "Limits" note, first paragraph. Replace

> Ultrasoft and PAW
> datasets give an error, because every matrix element would need the
> augmentation charge, and so do a noncollinear magnetization, a spin spiral
> and a Hubbard $U$.

with

> An ultrasoft dataset works for an insulator without spin. Then every matrix element has the
> augmentation charge, and the optical matrix elements use the velocity of the generalised
> eigenproblem. On silicon the static value in RPA is 0.05 from the Sternheimer solve at 60
> bands, out of 56.6, and the difference goes down with `nbnd` as for a norm-conserving
> dataset. An ultrasoft dataset with smearing, with `nspin = 2` or with a spinor gives an
> error, because we have not checked these cases. PAW gives an error, because its one-centre
> terms are not in the matrix. A noncollinear magnetization, a spin spiral and a Hubbard $U$
> also give an error.
