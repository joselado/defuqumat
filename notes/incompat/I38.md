What the check can tell apart, measured by mutation (D22, one run each):

- the conjugate half zeroed (`I38-verify/mutA.log`): every unfold consumer fails, the
  vertical transmission by a factor 244, site `<S>` by 4.3e-2;
- the conjugation dropped, `c(-G) = c(G)` (`run3-mutB.log`): projected DOS 0.95,
  STS and vertical transmission 0.14, site `<S>` 5.3e-2, all failing. On the first
  version of the test, with the single atom of each cell on an inversion centre of the
  origin (the sheet at z = 1/2, h-fcc at 0), the coefficients were real and this
  mutation passed the sheet and the h-fcc tests (`I38-verify/mutB.log`), so both atoms
  are now translated off it, which is the same physics.
- `eps_M` and `X_0^{+-}` read the same under both mutations by construction: those two
  workflows re-solve the bands at fixed density on the whole sphere
  (`whole_sphere_calculation`) and never unfold; their check is that the re-solve at a
  half-sphere SCF's density gives the whole-sphere answer. The library-level
  `independent_response` does unfold, and that path is covered only by the unit test
  `test_gamma_basis.py::test_the_unfolded_states_are_the_half_sphere_states`.
- with the eight consumer files reverted to master (`I38-verify/revert.log`), the
  refusals fire again by name.

## What was missing

The entry said `psi(r)` needed the 2 Re(sum) minus G = 0 form in each consumer. What
was done instead is one unfold, `defuqumat/scf/whole_sphere.py:unfold_gamma_states`,
which writes the conjugate half out by Miller index (`c(G)` stored, `conj(c(-G))` not,
G = 0 once), after which each consumer's own full-sphere contraction is the rule by
construction; the cost is the factor of two in plane waves, for the consumer only. The
entry's guard list was also incomplete: the site angular momenta and the Loewdin charge
(`projwfc/angular_momentum.py`) refused separately and are covered too, and the STM
image needed nothing (its density routine already carries `g_to_r_gamma`; its refusal was
a guard, not a missing term). The analytic forces and stress keep `refuse_gamma_storage`
(`forces/analytic.py:76`, `stress/analytic.py:110`), and the velocity operator and the
field responses keep theirs; none of those is in this item.
