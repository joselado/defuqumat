# I22. Velocity operator x spin spiral: partial (the operator is done, its consumers are not)

**Measured.** The velocity operator `dH/dk` on the NiI spiral at `q = b3/4`
against the four-cell supercell, at one density. The gauge-invariant trace
`T_ab(k) = sum_mn f_m f_n Re <m|v_a|n><n|v_b|m>` (rule D4 does not touch it)
at each supercell k-point equals the sum over the four spiral k-points that
fold onto it (`k + q/2`), to **5.5e-10 relative** (1.1e-7 on entries of 204,
two k-points). The control, the four k-points folded the other way, misses by
6.5. One D22 run (`review/incompat/spiral/v3-nii.log`). Test:
`tests/regression/test_spiral_supercell.py::test_the_spiral_velocity_is_the_supercell_velocity`
(slow).

**What was missing.** Not a new term: `at_kcart` refused a spiral, and with it
the velocity operator. It now takes the doubled list `k + q/2, k - q/2` (and
raises on an `nk`-row list), and `VelocityOperator` defaults its `kcart` to
`spiral_kcart`; the `jvp` tangent is broadcast over all `2 nk` rows, which moves
both spheres by the same `dk` and leaves `q`. `band_velocities` on the SCF's own
k-points runs through it.

**Still refused, each sharpened to the term that is missing:**

- optical conductivity (`response/conductivity.py:require_a_conductivity_regime`)
  and the shift current and SHG (`response/photocurrent.py:require_a_velocity_sum_regime`):
  their sums walk the k axis in chunks (`with_rows`, `kcart[rows]`) on a list of
  `nk`, where a spiral's rows are `ik` and `nk + ik` of `2 nk`, and their NSCF
  with empty states needs `at_kpoints`, which a spiral refuses;
- effective mass (`response/effmass.py`): the stencil is an NSCF at new k-points,
  `at_kpoints` again;
- an ultrasoft or PAW spiral, and a spiral with DFT+U, in the operator itself
  (no check).

So the shared missing piece of the rest of I22 is `at_kpoints` for a spiral (a
rebuild of both shifted spheres at a new k-list), then the doubled-row chunk walk.

**User guide.** `docs/guide/bands.md`, Limits: replace "`get_effective_mass()`
gives an error on a spin spiral, because the two spinor components sit on
plane-wave spheres centred at $\mathbf k + \mathbf q/2$ and
$\mathbf k - \mathbf q/2$, and a k-stencil moves both of them." with
"`get_effective_mass()` gives an error on a spin spiral, because its stencil
needs the bands at new k-points, and a spiral cannot yet move its two spheres
to another k-list. The band velocities on the k-points of the SCF work."
`docs/guide/optics.md` and `docs/guide/nonlinear.md`: keep the spiral
sentences (they still refuse).

**README feature table.** No cell changes (the conductivity, shift current, SHG
and effective-mass rows still refuse a spiral).
