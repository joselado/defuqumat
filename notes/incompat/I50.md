# I50. Spin spiral x crystal symmetry

Status: done for a norm-conserving spiral without DFT+U, on an automatic k-grid; the
other regimes stay refused by name (listed below).

## The number

The check is the `nosym` run: the same input on the full grid against the spiral on the
wedge of its spin space group. Measured on D22, one CPU core, one sample each
(`/l/ladovj1/review/incompat/I50/harness2.log` and `harness3.log`, worktree
`/l/ladovj1/w-inc-I50-v2`):

| cell | group | nk wedge / full | conv_thr | E_sym - E_nosym (Ry) | abs. mag. sym - nosym | abs. mag. |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| fcc H, `q = b3/8` (`h-fcc-spiral-scan.in`) | `C3v`, 6 ops, `f = 0` | 20 / 64 | 1e-10 | 1.1e-12 | 1.1e-7 | 0.406 |
| zigzag H chain, `q = c*/4`, 5 bohr spacing | 4 ops, two with `q . f = 45 deg` | 16 / 36 | 1e-11 | 5.9e-11 | 1.4e-4 | 1.252 |
| same | same | 16 / 36 | 1e-13 | 2.3e-12 | 3.0e-6 | 1.252 |
| H chain (`h-chain-spiral.in`) on 3x3x4 | `C4v`, 8 ops, `f = 0` | 12 / 36 | 1e-11 | 1.3e-13 | 2.9e-6 | 0.575 |

The zigzag gap at 1e-11 closes with `conv_thr` (1.4e-4 to 3.0e-6 in `|m|`, 5.9e-11 to
2.3e-12 Ry), so it is the convergence of a soft mode of the chain and not the group; the
test runs that cell at 1e-13. The two atoms' site moments are equal to 1e-15 on the wedge
and 2.6e-6 apart on the full grid at 1e-13, which is the full grid's own noise.

The rule is a discriminator and not a null result: the seed density is invariant to
round-off (`< 1e-12`) under the identity on the components, and its magnetization
residual is above 0.1 under the axial rule `det(S) S` on both fcc and the zigzag, and
under a leftover `Rot_z(q . f)` on the zigzag (`tests/unit/test_spiral_symmetry.py`).
A first run on two cells that came out nonmagnetic (`|m|` 5e-5 on fcc at `degauss = 0.05`,
2e-5 on a 4-bohr zigzag) was caught by the test's `|m| > 0.1` assertion and the cells were
replaced.

Tests:

- `tests/regression/test_spiral_symmetry.py::test_the_spiral_on_its_wedge_is_the_nosym_spiral`
  (slow; energy to 1e-10 Ry, `|m|` to 2e-5). It passed on D22 for fcc and the chain at the
  commit before the last; the zigzag's final form (`conv_thr = 1e-13`) was run as the

- `tests/unit/test_spiral_symmetry.py` (no SCF, 7 s locally, 9 tests): group counts and

  that already carries the wedge. The DFT+U guard fires before the older

Wall times, one sample each and including compilation (`harness2.log`): zigzag 22.1 s on
the wedge against 30.0 s on the full grid, chain 11.0 against 14.6 s, fcc 2.8 against 3.0 s.

## What was missing, as found in the code

The entry says "operations with `S^T q = q` acting with their own spin rotation". That is
right in the laboratory frame and the identity on the fields the SCF holds: with no
spin-orbit coupling, `{S|f}` with `S q = q` is a symmetry of the spiral together with the
laboratory spin rotation `Rot_z(q . f)`, and the spiral's own phase cancels it on the
rotated-frame `n, m_x, m_y, m_z`, so the group permutes them as four scalars. Three things
were missing, not one:

- the group: `defuqumat/system/spiral.py:spiral_symmetries`, from the crystal's group by
  `S q = q` (tested on the cartesian rotation, exact rather than mod G) and an *unrotated*
  permutation of the moments and applied fields (Elk's `findsym.f90` with the identity as
  the global spin rotation). It is used in all three places a group is decided
  (`build_system`, `System.symmetry_group`, `System._respin_kpoints`), so `grid_symmetry`
  and the NSCF and tetrahedra grids follow; time reversal is off.
- the symmetriser: `Calculation._spin_rotations` gives the identity for a spiral in place
  of `det(S) S`, read by the density's symmetriser and by `symmetrize_directional`.
- a k-set check: `defuqumat/scf/driver.py:_require_a_spiral_wedge` compares the k-set with
  the wedge of this group on the same grid, so a `System` built by `dataclasses.replace`
  from a non-spiral one, or at another `q`, is refused.

Still refused with symmetry, each tested to fire: an ultrasoft or PAW dataset (`becsum` is
symmetrised as an axial vector in `paw/symmetry.py`), DFT+U, `lspinorb` at `soc_scale = 0`
(the reader; the coupling `spiral_spin_orbit_energy` adds breaks the group), an explicit
k-list, a k-set that is not this group's wedge, and `at_spiral_q`, hence `dE/dq`, the
relaxation of `q` and the scans (another `q` has another stabiliser, and the wedge's
`dE/dq` is not completed). Every other consumer of a spiral was not audited for a wedge;
`projwfc/angular_momentum.py` already refuses a spiral, and the spin torque is built from
the symmetrised density.

## User guide

Page `docs/guide/spirals.md`, Limits note. Replace

> A spiral needs `nosym` and the full k-mesh, because the spin space group is not in the code.

with

> A spiral uses symmetry. The code keeps the operations that leave $\mathbf q$ unchanged and that map each atom's moment onto the moment of its image. It reduces the k-mesh with these operations, without time reversal. Some runs give an error when symmetry is on: an ultrasoft or PAW dataset, DFT+U, a fully relativistic dataset, and an explicit list of k-points. A change of $\mathbf q$ also gives an error, so $dE/d\mathbf q$, the relaxation of $\mathbf q$ and the scan need `nosym`.
