# I50. Spin spiral x crystal symmetry

Status: done for a norm-conserving spiral without DFT+U, on an automatic k-grid (SCF,
forces, stress, DOS); the other regimes stay refused by name (listed below).

The 5.6e-8 force gap is the `nosym` arm's own `z` component on the zigzag, which the
group forbids and the wedge returns as an exact zero; the `nosym` stress carries
off-diagonal elements of 6e-11 the same way. The DOS gap is 1.3e-6 of its peak at the
same energies (1748 and 2006 points). The pre-merge numbers on the old base
(`harness2.log`, `harness3.log`) are reproduced to the last digit printed for all three
energies and moments.

The zigzag needs `conv_thr = 1e-13`: at 1e-11 (old base, `harness3.log`) the two arms
differ by 1.4e-4 in `|m|` and 5.9e-11 Ry, which closes to 3.0e-6 and 2.3e-12 Ry at
1e-13, so it is the convergence of a soft mode of the chain and not the group.

The rule is a discriminator and not a null result: the seed density is invariant to
round-off (`< 1e-12`) under the identity on the components, and its magnetization
residual is above 0.1 under the axial rule `det(S) S` on both fcc and the zigzag, and
under a leftover `Rot_z(q . f)` on the zigzag (`tests/unit/test_spiral_symmetry.py`).
A first run on two cells that came out nonmagnetic (`|m|` 5e-5 on fcc at `degauss = 0.05`,
2e-5 on a 4-bohr zigzag) was caught by the test's `|m| > 0.1` assertion and the cells were
replaced.

## What the merge made reachable

The old record said every other consumer of a spiral was not audited for a wedge and
that `projwfc/angular_momentum.py` refuses a spiral. Master (I1 to I48) lifted several
spiral refusals for a norm-conserving dataset, each written against a `nosym` spiral on
the whole grid, so each now meets a wedge for the first time. What each does now:

- forces, stress (and the relaxations built on them): run, measured above. Both are
  symmetrised as a polar vector and tensor with the spatial operations, which is right for
  the spin space group without spin-orbit coupling.
- DOS and the NSCF grid: run, measured above (the denser grid is reduced with the same
  group through `System.grid_symmetry`).
- site angular momenta (`projwfc/angular_momentum.py:_refuse_what_is_not_written`): now
  refused with symmetry on. `_symmetrise_axial` turns the site spin with `det(S) S`, but
  the site spin of a spiral is the laboratory one at the home-cell atom, which an
  operation of the spin space group turns about the spiral axis by `q . (f + R)`. The
  orbital part alone would be right; the spin part is not written.
- atomic projections and the PDOS (`projwfc/projections.py:atomic_projections`): refused
  with symmetry on and `symmetrize = True`. `build_projection_symmetry` turns each spinor
  column with the SU(2) image of the spatial rotation, which the spin space group does not
  carry.
- a `System` with `lspinorb` built without the reader (`dataclasses.replace` from a
  `nosym` one): now refused in `_require_a_spiral_wedge`, as the reader refuses it.
- the velocity operator: lifted on master for a norm-conserving spiral, but every
  consumer of it on a spiral (the optical conductivity, the effective mass) still refuses
  a spiral, and a band velocity on a caller's k-list meets the explicit-list refusal.
- band structure along a path: refused with symmetry on (explicit k-list); `nosym` as before.
- refused for any spiral already, unchanged: real-time propagation, the Sternheimer
  stack, `chi_0` and the spin `chi_0`, the Berry-phase field and polarization, nesting,
  the orbital magnetization, the anisotropy workflows, continuation, `at_kpoints`.

## What was missing, as found in the code

The entry says "operations with `S^T q = q` acting with their own spin rotation". That is
right in the laboratory frame and the identity on the fields the SCF holds: with no
spin-orbit coupling, `{S|f}` with `S q = q` is a symmetry of the spiral together with the
laboratory spin rotation `Rot_z(q . f)`, and the spiral's own phase cancels it on the
rotated-frame `n, m_x, m_y, m_z`, so the group permutes them as four scalars. Three things
were missing, not one:

- the group: `defuqumat/system/spiral.py:spiral_symmetries`, from the crystal's group by
  `S q = q` (tested on the cartesian rotation, exact rather than mod G) and an *unrotated*
  permutation of the moments and applied fields (Elk's `findsym.f90` with the identity as
  the global spin rotation). It is used in all three places a group is decided
  (`build_system`, `System.symmetry_group`, `System._respin_kpoints`), so `grid_symmetry`
  and the NSCF and tetrahedra grids follow; time reversal is off.
- the symmetriser: `Calculation._spin_rotations` gives the identity for a spiral in place
  of `det(S) S`, read by the density's symmetriser and by `symmetrize_directional`.
- a k-set check: `defuqumat/scf/driver.py:_require_a_spiral_wedge` compares the k-set with
  the wedge of this group on the same grid, so a `System` built by `dataclasses.replace`
  from a non-spiral one, or at another `q`, is refused.

Still refused with symmetry, each tested to fire: an ultrasoft or PAW dataset (`becsum` is
symmetrised as an axial vector in `paw/symmetry.py`), DFT+U, `lspinorb` at `soc_scale = 0`
(the reader; the coupling `spiral_spin_orbit_energy` adds breaks the group), an explicit
k-list, a k-set that is not this group's wedge, and `at_spiral_q`, hence `dE/dq`, the
relaxation of `q` and the scans (another `q` has another stabiliser, and the wedge's
`dE/dq` is not completed). The consumers audited after the merge are listed under "What the merge made
reachable"; the spin torque and the STM charge image are built from the symmetrised density.

## User guide

Page `docs/guide/spirals.md`, Limits note. Replace

> A spiral needs `nosym` and the full k-mesh, because the spin space group is not in the code.

with

> A spiral uses symmetry. The code keeps the operations that leave $\mathbf q$ unchanged and that map the moment of each atom onto the moment of its image, and it reduces the k-mesh with them, without time reversal. The energy, the moments, the forces, the stress and the density of states are then the same as with `nosym`. Some runs still need `nosym`: an ultrasoft or PAW dataset, DFT+U, a fully relativistic dataset, an explicit list of k-points (so also a band structure along a path), the site angular momenta and the projected density of states. A change of $\mathbf q$ also needs `nosym`, so $dE/d\mathbf q$, the relaxation of $\mathbf q$ and the scan need it too.

## README

The feature table row `| **Spin spirals** at any wavevector | spiral_q | | ✓ |` needs no
change of its cells. The prose bullet "**Spin spirals** at any wavevector" (README line 341)
says "You need `nosym`."; replace it with "Symmetry is used, with the operations that leave
$\mathbf q$ unchanged." `.claude/rules/scope.md`'s spiral bullet also says symmetry is
refused "until the spin space group is written"; that clause becomes "symmetry beside an
augmented dataset, DFT+U, `lspinorb`, an explicit k-list, the site angular momenta, the
symmetrised projections or a change of `q`".
