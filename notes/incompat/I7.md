Status: done. The `nspin != 1` refusal of `require_a_sum_over_states_regime`
(`defuqumat/tddft/chi0.py`) is lifted for collinear `nspin = 2`; the spinor,
spiral, ultrasoft/PAW, metal, Hubbard and reduced-k refusals are unchanged, and
ALDA on a magnetized density is refused by name.

## What was missing, against the entry

The entry said "the spin-tensor kernel". In the code the missing piece was smaller
and somewhere else:

- `chi_0` itself needed only a sum over the two channels. `e^{-iG.r}` and `dH/dk`
  are diagonal in spin, so a pair is a pair of one spin, and the charge `chi_0` is
  `chi_0^up + chi_0^dn`. What the code did not have was the loop: `_occupied_bands`
  took `nelec / 2` for every channel and `independent_response` read channel 0 of
  the states, the eigenvalues, the weights and the velocity matrix elements. Now
  each channel has its own pair list from `occupied_counts` (QE's `nbnd_occ`), its
  own scissor cut and its own `wg`, which already carries one electron a band.
- The spin tensor is needed only by the **ALDA** kernel on a **polarized** density,
  where `f_nm` couples the charge to the magnetization and the Dyson equation is a
  2x2 block one (Elk's `tddftsplr.f90`). `rpa`, `lrc` and `bootstrap` act on the
  charge only and run. ALDA is refused on a moment above 1e-6 by
  `kernels.refuse_a_polarized_alda`, called in `alda_matrix` and in
  `run_absorption` before the empty states are paid for.
- One more defect the entry did not name: `alda_matrix` took the `jvp` of `v_xc`
  along a tangent of ones in every channel, which at `nspin = 2` is
  `f_upup + f_updn`, twice `dv/dn`. The tangent is now `1/nspin`; the unit test
  `test_the_alda_kernel_refuses_a_polarized_density_and_halves_the_tangent`
  checks the split density against the `nspin = 1` one to 1e-12 without an SCF.
- `run_absorption`, `_default_nbnd` and `_static_residual` read `nelec / 2` too;
  they now read `max(occupied_counts)`, and the Sternheimer solver masks the
  shallower channel itself.
