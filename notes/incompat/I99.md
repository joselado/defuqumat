# I99. Real-time propagation with the potential updated (`hxc`) x meta-GGA

Status: done for `nspin = 1` (tb09 and bj06, the only meta-GGAs here, both potential-only).
`nspin = 2` and spinors stay refused by name, and so does the frequency-domain hierarchy's
meta-GGA kernel, which the old guard hid and which now fires by itself.

The first round was refuted on reachability, and the fix round below answers it: every
`Calculator` route now reaches a meta-GGA.

## Fix round (2026-10-19): the front door

**What was refuted.** The verifier ran a tb09 `Calculator` on `si2-symmetric` (8 Ry) through
`get_scf` and then `get_realtime` with `'frozen'` and `'hxc'`, `little_group` off and on. All
four raised `NotImplementedError` from `workflows/nscf.py:263` ("pass tau = scf_result.tau").
The cause: no real-time workflow had a `tau` parameter, so `Calculator._call_options`, whose
`_STATE_ARGUMENTS` already maps `tau`, never forwarded `SCFResult.tau`, and `_solved_states`
built neither the fixed-density states nor `v_scf` with it. The refusal predates I99, but the
record's replacement sentence for the guide claimed the front door works, which was false.
The verifier also asked for a number on `tau`'s little-group completion, since the `[hxc]`
little-group tests are LDA and the dense reference has no symmetry in it.

**What changed.** `tau=None` is a parameter of `_solved_states`, `_occupied_states`,
`run_realtime`, `run_realtime_dielectric`, `run_hhg`, `run_harmonic_orders`,
`run_third_harmonic` and `run_nonlinear_spectrum`, passed to `fixed_density_states(tau=...)`
and to `calc.potential(..., tau=...)` for `v_scf`. Nothing in `calculator.py` changed: once
the parameter exists, `_call_options` forwards `SCFResult.tau` by itself. The propagation is
written `v_scf + U[rho(t), tau(t)] - U[rho0, tau0]` with `tau0` rebuilt from the propagated
states, so a real-time k-set that differs from the SCF mesh is stationary at the start as it
is for `rho`. A frozen run under a meta-GGA was blocked by the same missing argument and now
runs too.

**The number.** D22, CPU, one core each, harness `/l/ladovj1/review/incompat/I99-fix/frontdoor.py`
(logs `clean.log`, `mutant.log`, worktrees `w-inc-I99-fix` and `w-inc-I99-fix-mut`).
`si2-symmetric.in` with `ecutwfc = 8` and `input_dft = 'tb09'`, `get_scf(conv_thr=1e-10)`,
then `get_realtime` with `Sin2.from_intensity(5e11, 1.55, 2, (1, 0, 0))` on the 4x4x4 mesh,
`potential = 'hxc'`, the whole mesh (64 points, 1 operation) against the wedge (8
operations). The figure is `max |J_wedge - J_whole| / max |J_whole|`, with `max |J|` 5.96e-4.
The term switched off is a mutant in which `tau(t)` is not completed with the little group
(line 325 of `realtime/selfconsistent.py`, `complete(...)` removed), `rho(t)` unchanged.

| dt (a.u.) | clean | `tau` not completed |
|---|---|---|
| 0.2 | **1.10e-8** | 9.78e-2 |
| 0.1 | **1.13e-8** | 9.78e-2 |

The agreement does not move with `dt`, as it should not: the wedge is an exact reduction at
any step, and 1.1e-8 is the floor of the separate fixed-density solves at the members of a
star (the LDA test reads 6.2e-11 at 12 Ry). The mutant is 9.8e-2 away at both steps, so
the completion of `tau` is resolved nine million times over. The transverse current on the
wedge is 2.7e-20. The wedge took 58 s against 184 s at `dt = 0.2`. Through the same front
door the frozen run gives a current 10.4 per cent away from the `'hxc'` one, so the update
is a visible effect on this pulse.

The fast test `tests/unit/test_realtime.py::test_every_realtime_entry_point_takes_the_meta_gga_tau`
checks that each of the eight functions takes `tau` and that `_call_options` fills it from
the result. The fast unit file on D22 gave 15 passed (`fast.log`).

## The number

The check is the dense self-consistent solve of P136 (`realtime/dense.py:dense_first_order`),
extended to a kernel in `(rho, tau)`. Its `dtau_+1` is built by FFT from the dense
coefficients, as the states' part `sum w (conj(g_0).g_+1 + g_0.conj(g_-1))` with
`g = (k + G) u`, plus the explicit part from `k + kappa + G` at fixed states,
`sum w Re(conj(e.g_0) u_0)`. Its kernel is the `jvp` of `Calculation.potential(rho, tau=tau)`,
and Tran and Blaha's `c[rho]` moves inside that. The two routes share `H(k)` and the
potential's derivative. Nothing of the time stepping, of `kinetic_energy_density` or of the
nested `jvp` tower is shared. The setup is P136's: Gamma and `(0.25, 0.1, -0.05)` with
weights 1/2, `w = 0.05` and `eta = 0.01` Ha, `eta T = 20`, `J_(1,1)` along x, the frozen
potential at the superposition of atomic charges with the Thomas-Fermi `tau`. I ran on D22,
CPU, one core each (`/l/ladovj1/review/incompat/I99/run1.log`, `run2-*.log`, `run3-*.log`,
harness `harness.py` / `harness3.py` there). The convergence parameter is the number of
steps a period.

| cell | 400 steps | 800 | 1600 | ratio |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Si2, tb09, 5 Ry (`si2-tb09.in`, 12^3 grid) | **2.13e-4** | **3.76e-5** | **8.92e-6** | 5.7, 4.2 |
| AlAs, tb09 added, 4 Ry (`alas-shg.in`, 9^3) | **7.43e-5** | **1.52e-5** | | 4.9 |

The numbers are `|J_RT - J_dense| / |J_dense|`. They fall toward the midpoint rule's `dt^2`
(a ratio of 4). The GMRES residuals of the dense fixed point are 2e-13 to 5e-13.

These are the discrepancies with the term switched off (dense reference, relative to the full one):

| cell | `tau` held at `tau0` (the update without I99's term) | explicit `k + kappa` part of `dtau` dropped | frozen potential | `'hartree'` |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Si2 | **0.281** | 1.66e-3 | 0.419 | 0.460 |
| AlAs | **0.0217** | 7.63e-3 | 0.115 | 0.149 |

So on silicon the propagation resolves `tau(t)` 7500 times over at 800 steps, and the
explicit part 44 times over (190 at 1600). On AlAs the figures are 1400 and 500 times. The
explicit part does not vanish here because this two-point k-set has no `-k` partner, so the
ground state carries a paramagnetic current `j_p(r)` and `kappa . j_p` is first order. On a
time-reversal-symmetric mesh it would cancel at first order.

There is also the start at rest. With a zero kick, 100 steps of 0.2 a.u. move the updated
potential by 8.4e-8 Ry (Si2) and 6.2e-8 (AlAs), the Taylor step's own drift. A 1e-3/bohr
kick moves it by 5.9e-3 and 5.5e-3 Ry. `RealTimeResult.energy` is NaN and the warning is
issued.

The realtime fast tests on D22 (`-m 'not slow'` over the eight realtime test files) gave
21 passed (`run3-fast.log`).

## What was missing, against the entry

The entry named two things: "`tau(t)` propagated", and "no conserved energy" for tb09 and
bj06. The second was a diagnostic, not a blocker. The dynamics needs no energy. The run now
reports `energy` as NaN with a warning rather than a number that is the value of nothing,
which also makes `energy_gained` NaN. What was missing in the code was three things:

- `tau(t)` rebuilt at every step from the propagated states, at the kinetic momentum
  `k + kappa(t) + G` (the gauge-invariant `tau`, which moves with `kappa` at fixed states).
  It is completed with the field's little group like `rho`. `_Run.density` now takes
  `(parts, psi, kappa)` and returns `(rho, tau)` under a meta-GGA, and `U` is
  `calculation.potential(rho, 0.0, tau=tau)`.
- The reference `tau0` taken at `kappa = 0`, and the first potential of a run whose field
  is on at the first time (a kick) taken at that `kappa`. `rho` does not see `kappa`, so for
  every other functional this is `v_scf` as before.
- `c[rho(t)]`, recomputed inside the potential at each step. It needed no code, since the
  potential already builds it from the density it is given.

The hierarchy's own refusal (`realtime/hierarchy.py:hierarchy_linear_self_consistent`,
"the exchange-correlation kernel of a meta-GGA is not here") is unchanged. It is now
reached, and a unit test shows it fires. Lifting it needs the first-order `tau` inside
`_orders_at`'s cross density, a separate item.

What is left, and why:

- `nspin = 2`: the `(up, down)` channels go through the same code path, but no check was
  taken on them (`dense_first_order` is single-channel), so the guard refuses them by the
  conservative rule. The natural check is an unpolarized `nspin = 2` tb09 run against
  `nspin = 1` on the same k-set, to round-off.
- Spinors: `tau` is a 2x2 matrix in spin space there, and its update is not written.
- `propagate_orders_self_consistent` starts its tower at `v_scf`. That is exact when the
  shape is zero at the start (an `Adiabatic` at `eta T = 20` is 2e-9), not for a general
  pulse already on at `t = start`. `propagate_self_consistent` handles that case.
- The two sqrt factors in tb09 (`|grad rho|` in `c`, `sqrt(sigma)` in Becke-Roussel) have an
  undefined tangent where `grad rho = 0` exactly. On these grids the round-off keeps `sigma`
  nonzero and every `J` came out finite. A cell whose grid puts an exact zero there would
  give NaN in the orders route and in the dense reference, not a wrong number.

## Integration on `incompat-t3` (2026-10-20, branch `t3-I99`)

I98, already on `incompat-t3`, made `_Run`'s density return `(rho, becsum)` and its potential take
the pair, where this branch carries `(rho, tau)`. The merge combines them: the propagated state is
`(rho, becsum, tau)`, `becsum` empty without an augmentation and `tau` `None` without a meta-GGA
under `'hxc'`, and the potential is `v_scf + U[rho, becsum, tau] - U[rho0, becsum0, tau0]`. The
reference is taken at `kappa0` for an augmented dataset, whose states are first carried across the
start's step in `kappa` (I98), and at `kappa = 0` otherwise (this record's `tau0`); a meta-GGA run
starts at the potential of its first `kappa`, every other run at `(v_scf, ddd_scf)`.

Each side was written with the other's regime refused, so a meta-GGA with an augmented dataset under
`'hxc'` is neither side's. A plain ultrasoft dataset never reaches it, since the SCF refuses a
meta-GGA there (`Calculation._require_meta_supported`). PAW does, and it is refused by name in
`require_a_potential_mode` (its one-centre kinetic energy densities of `becsum(t)` are not written);
the unit test `test_a_meta_gga_is_updated_at_nspin_1_and_refused_elsewhere` checks that it fires.

`w-inc-t3-I99-mut`, where the `complete(...)` of `kinetic` is removed and `density`'s is kept):
