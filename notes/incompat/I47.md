## What was missing, as found

The entry said "the subgroup that keeps the exit plane in place, not the whole point
group". That is not what is needed. The term at the image `g k` of a wedge point is the
term at `k` of the image geometry, tip at `g^-1 r` and exit plane at `g^-1` of the exit
plane, which for a mirror through the slab is the plane on the other side. So the wedge is
completed with the whole group by evaluating it in every image geometry and averaging
(`workflows/transport.py:_vertical_wedge` and `_completed_on_a_wedge`): the operations are
grouped by where they put the exit plane (two places at most) and each group is one
`_assemble` over all its tip images. Only the unitary half of the group adds images; time
reversal leaves `|psi|^2`-built weights alone.

Still refused:

- a **spin-selective lead** (`spin=` or `tip_spin=`) on a wedge: the lead's acceptance is
  an axial vector the operations turn;
- an operation that **tilts the stacking axis** rather than keeping or reversing it;
- what `system/wedge.py:wedge_group` refuses (a magnetic group, a reduced set on a
  `nosym` run, a set the group does not tile);
- the **momentum-resolved** map (`transport.py:525`, `_refuse_a_k_set_this_cannot_sum`):
  its result is per `k_par`, so a wedge has to be unfolded to the whole grid with the
  images of the geometry per point rather than averaged, which is not written;
- the **ultracell** transport (`ultracell.py:329`), whose states are the folded ultracell
  set and which was not touched.
