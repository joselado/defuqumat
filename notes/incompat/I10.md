## What was missing

**The entry's stated term was wrong.** It said the spin axis on the strain coordinate's
`dpsi + ort` block and multipliers was missing. It was there on every array:
`_bare_strains`, `overlap_derivatives` and `_eigenvalue_response` loop over the channel and
`orthogonality_states`' einsum carries it, and the guard's own docstring said only that the axis
had never been run. The identity above passes with no code change beyond the guard.

**The term that was missing was found by the magnet, and it is the occupied block of `S'`.** At
`nspin = 2` the Sternheimer solver carries `max(nocc)` bands in both channels and masks the
deficient channel's extra bands (`projector_mask`). `orthogonality_states` built
`ort = -1/2 sum_m psi_m S'_mn` over all of them, so in O2's minority channel the empty pi*
(bands 6 and 7) entered the first-order state of the occupied sigma_g wherever
`<pi*|dS/d(eps)|sigma_g>` is nonzero. By symmetry that is the xz and yz shears only (d_xz and d_yz
couple Sigma_g to Pi_g), which is the pattern measured. The fix is
`defuqumat/response/strain.py:occupied_block`, applied to `S'` at its source on both routes
(`overlap_derivatives` and `chunked_strain.StreamedStrains.prepare`). It is an identity for every
`nspin = 1` insulator.

**The same construction is in the phonon** (`defuqumat/response/phonon.py:orthogonality_states`,
`chunked_phonon.py:510`, `nonlinear.py:635`): `-1/2 sum_m psi_m S'_mn` over the padded block. I2's
entry measures O2's `D_xx` off by up to 0.12 with the guards bypassed. The xz shear's coupling of
sigma_g to pi* has a counterpart for an x displacement, so this is the first thing to test there.
I did not touch it.

**Not lifted, and why.**
- **Electrostriction** (`electrostriction.py:969`, bare guard). With the guard bypassed on the
  nonmagnetic identity it matches `nspin = 1` (`dchi_dstrain` 4.5e-11, `m` 4.5e-11, `epsilon`
  2.1e-11, `run2-E.log`). That is the identity alone, though, and the third derivative's
  multiplier and `b`-partial terms on a magnet are unmeasured; the entry's "third derivative of
  `E_xc^LSDA` in two channels" stays the open question. I left it refused.
- **Piezoelectric tensor** (`piezo.py:667`, `piezo_ladder.py:210`): not run. Silicon's tensor is
  zero by symmetry, so the identity there would prove nothing; a polar cell is needed.
- **A GGA at `nspin = 2`** is refused by the new `require_a_measured_spin_regime`. The field
  response's NaN guard records that `dgcxc_spin`'s thresholds are not transcribed, and nothing has
  measured a GGA strain response at `nspin = 2`.
- **The elastic constants of a magnet** (`nspin = 2` with a nonzero `tot_magnetization`) are
  refused by name in `elastic.py:_require_a_measured_spin_state`. The identity cannot see a term in
  the magnetization, O2 is ultrasoft (augmented `C_ijkl` is refused on its own, P100), and no
  norm-conserving magnetic insulator is committed. The missing check is `C_ijkl` against a finite
  difference of the `nspin = 2` stress (T3).
  Test: `test_strain_spin.py::test_the_elastic_constants_of_a_magnet_are_refused`.

So the item is **partial** in this sense: of its four quantities, the strain response is lifted
for `nspin = 2` (LDA), on a magnet too, and the elastic constants for `nspin = 2` without a
magnetization, each with the numbers above. Electrostriction and piezo keep their refusals
untouched.
