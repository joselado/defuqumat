# I39. Real-time propagation x gamma-only storage

Status: done. The workflows propagate a `K_POINTS gamma` ground state on the whole sphere;
the refusal stays on the driver for a half-sphere `Calculation` handed to `propagate`
directly.

## The number

The two-atom silicon cell of `tests/regression/test_gamma_only.py` (LDA, `ecutwfc = 12`,
`nosym`, one atom displaced, SCF `conv_thr = 1e-12`), run once as `K_POINTS gamma` and once
as `K_POINTS automatic 1 1 1 0 0 0`, then `run_realtime` under a one-cycle `Sin2` pulse
(1e12 W/cm^2, 1.55 eV, polarization (1, 0.5, 0.2)), `dt = 0.1`, 400 steps, fixed-density
`conv_thr = 1e-12`. The identity is the explicit `k = 0` on the whole sphere, which is the
same physics; there is no external reference by construction, and none is needed.

- `max |J_gamma - J_k0| / max |J_k0|` = **1.841e-13** on the bare `run_realtime` route and
  **1.841e-13** through `Calculator.get_realtime`, with `max |J| = 1.578e-3` a.u. One run
  on D22, CPU, one core (`/l/ladovj1/review/incompat/I39/run1.log`).
- The band energy agrees to below 1e-10 Ha at every recorded time, the electron count is
  8 on both, and the norm drift is below 1e-8.
- The gamma arm's SCF did consume the half sphere (`calculation.gamma_only is True`,
  asserted), so the comparison is between two storages and not two full spheres.
- Control at `0feb4ee` (`/l/ladovj1/review/incompat/I39/run2-master.log`): the same test
  raised the refusal on the bare route, `real-time propagation with gamma-only storage is
  not implemented`, from `_solved_states` at `workflows/realtime.py:237`.

The alternative hypothesis reads differently: a half sphere propagated as if it were whole
misses the conjugate half of every state, an error of order one in the current, or fails
in `scatter_to_box_gamma`.

Test: `tests/regression/test_realtime_gamma.py::test_a_gamma_ground_state_propagates_as_the_explicit_k0`
(slow, about 100 s), tolerance 1e-10 on the current. The guard and the k-set are
`tests/unit/test_realtime.py::test_gamma_storage_is_refused_by_the_driver_and_taken_off_by_the_workflows`
(gate, no SCF, fails at `0feb4ee` on `assert not kset.gamma_only`).

## What was missing

The entry's term, "store the full sphere", is right in substance, but the machinery
already existed and nothing in the propagator needed writing. Every real-time workflow
(`run_realtime`, `run_realtime_dielectric`, `run_hhg`, `run_harmonic_orders`,
`run_third_harmonic`, `run_nonlinear_spectrum`) re-solves the occupied states from the
density on the k-set built by `_kset`, and the density lives on the dense G set, which
gamma-only storage never halves. So what was missing is one flag: `_kset` returned
`KPoints.gamma()` with `gamma_only = True`, a fresh `Calculation` then found the storage
consumable (norm-conserving, `nosym`, no U, collinear) and kept the half sphere, and the
guard fired. `_kset` now returns the set with `gamma_only = False` (`_whole_sphere`, the
same `dataclasses.replace` as `_without_gamma_storage`, keeping the weights and
`spin_normalized`).

The `Calculator` route never reached the guard: it hands the SCF's calculation to
`threaded_calculation`, whose `at_kpoints` rebuilds the sphere whole and resets
`gamma_only` (`_adopt_rebuilt_sphere`). That is read from the code; I did not run that
route at `0feb4ee`, since the bare route failed first in the control.

The check runs the frozen-potential route only. The potential updated in time
(`potential = 'hartree' | 'hxc'`, `realtime/selfconsistent.py`) and the frequency-domain
hierarchy take the same k-set from `_kset` and so no longer reach the guard either, but
neither was run from a gamma ground state, and the 1.84e-13 does not cover them.

Unlike STM and transport, which consume the SCF's own half-sphere states and so refuse,
real-time propagation never reads them. The refusal in `require_a_realtime_regime` stays,
reached now only by a half-sphere `Calculation` passed to `realtime.propagate.propagate`
or `_prepare` directly; its message says so. The sibling refusals (spiral, DFT+U, magnetic
field, saw-tooth) are unchanged and the spiral and DFT+U ones are asserted in the unit test.
