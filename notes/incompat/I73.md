# I73. Residual (Newton) solver x applied or constraining magnetic field: done

**The number.** A direct `pw.x` reference. `scf_solver='newton-krylov'` on QE's own
`test-suite/pw_noncolin/noncolin-constrain_atomic.in` (noncollinear bcc iron, 70 k-points,
`constrained_magnetization = 'atomic'`, `lambda = 0.005`), at `conv_thr = 1e-13`, `mixing_beta = 0.2`,
`forcing = 0.5`, `warmup = 6` (D22, worktree `w-inc-I73` at `611396bf`, log
`review/incompat/I73/harnessA.log`, one sample, 66 s on one core): total energy **-55.6905569479 Ry,
7.8e-8 Ry from `pw.x`'s -55.69055687** (`tests/data/qe/reference.out.pw_noncolin-noncolin-constrain_atomic:777`),
against the 3e-7 the mixing route is held to in the same file; constraint energy **0.00646111 against
the 0.00646103** `pw.x` prints last (`:409`), 8e-8 apart; moment (1.680, 0, 0.147) against `pw.x`'s
(1.68, -0.00, 0.15) (`:788`). Newton reached `dr2 = 7.5e-14` in 13 outer steps and 104 evaluations of `F`
(42 Jacobian actions, central difference), and the mixing loop it hands over to stopped after one
iteration, so the number is Newton's and not the loop's (asserted in the slow test since the fix round, see
"The fix round" below). The mixing route on the same input is 1.9e-9 from
`pw.x` in 47 iterations (`test_magnetic_constraints.py::_run`'s docstring). The alternative, a solver that
lost the penalty, would sit near the unconstrained moment, 3.06 mu_B on this cell (`fields.py:constraint_targets` docstring), and could not read any of these.

The other two penalty inputs of the same directory, through the same harness at `conv_thr = 1e-13`
(`notes/incompat/I73-harness/harness3.py`, D22 worktree `w-inc-I73-v3` at `9bed9c82`, one sample each):

| input, scheme | Newton total (Ry) | `pw.x` (Ry) | difference | moment against `pw.x` | outer steps, `F` evaluations | time |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `noncolin-constrain_angle.in`, `atomic direction` | -55.6996843373 | -55.69968434 (`constrain_angle:461`) | 2.7e-9 | (3.176, 0, 0) against (3.18, -0.00, 0.00) | 13, 105 | 47 s |
| `noncolin-constrain_total.in`, `total` | -55.5426612275 | -55.54266124 (`constrain_total:758`) | 1.3e-8 | (0.305, 0.407, 0.509) against (0.31, 0.41, 0.51) | 29, 285 | 369 s |

In both the mixing loop after Newton stopped after one iteration. The `total` case discriminates: its
moment is 0.72 mu_B where the cell left alone carries about 3. The `atomic direction` case does not: its
constraint energy at convergence is 5e-33, the same zero `pw.x` prints, meaning the constrained direction is
the one the cell takes anyway, so a solver that dropped the penalty would read the same number there.

**The 7.8e-8 on `atomic` is where the comparison sits, not a trend in `conv_thr`.** At `conv_thr = 1e-11`
and `1e-12` (`h3-atomic11.log`, `h3-atomic12.log`) Newton's last step overshoots both thresholds to the same
`dr2 = 3.7e-13` in 11 outer steps, and both read -55.6905569945, 1.2e-7 from `pw.x`; at 1e-13 (`dr2 =
7.5e-14`) it reads 7.8e-8. The mixing route's 1.9e-9 at 1e-13 against 4.3e-7 at 1e-11 is the same
first-order sensitivity of a penalised energy to the moment; `pw.x` prints eight decimals, so everything
under about 5e-9 is its printed precision.

`total direction` and `atomic texture` stay refused, and so does a `LOCAL_MAGNETIC_FIELDS` card. By the
argument below nothing is missing for them (a penalty's field is `jax.grad` of the penalty, a local field
is held fixed like the uniform one, and nothing in `run_scf` moves either), but no input in the test-suite
carries them and they are not measured, and a refusal is lifted here only with a number. What would lift
each is the identity used for the uniform field: Newton against the mixing loop on the same cell, for
instance `noncolin-constrain_total.in` edited to `total direction`, and `fe-fsm.in` with a local-field card
in place of `b_field`.

A second check, Newton against the mixing loop under a **uniform field** (`tests/data/qe/fe-fsm.in` with
its feedback constraint replaced by `b_field(3) = 0.01`, collinear, one k-point, `conv_thr = 1e-10`;
`harnessBC.log`, one sample): total energy **1.7e-8 Ry** apart, field energy 1.7e-8 Ry, moment 1.7e-6 mu_B,
Newton at 7 outer steps and 58 evaluations of `F` against the mixer's 14 iterations. The alternative is
the field-free cell (`harness2.log`, `w-inc-I73-v2` at `cb200beb`): -55.5742646 Ry and 2.500 mu_B against
-55.5734806 Ry and 2.698 mu_B under the field, so 7.8e-4 Ry and 0.20 mu_B, four and five orders above the
agreement.

**What was missing, and where the entry was wrong.** Nothing was missing for the regime the guard covered
most of. The entry said "a field that is a function of the state; the field secant sits outside the
density". `ScfResidual.step` builds its potential with `calculation.potential(rho)`, which already applies
`calculation.magnetic_field` at full strength (`field_scale = 1`), and a penalty constraint's field is
`jax.grad` of the penalty (`MagneticField.potential`), a function of the density. `run_scf` changes the
field in exactly two places (`driver.py`, after the mix): `field_scale *= field.reducebf`, and
`field.feedback(...)`, which is the identity outside `FEEDBACK`. So for a uniform `B_field`, a
`LOCAL_MAGNETIC_FIELDS` card and the five penalty schemes (`atomic`, `atomic direction`, `total`,
`total direction`, `atomic texture`) at `reducebf = 1`, `F` was already the loop's map, and the guard in
`make_residual` was refusing a regime with no term missing. The guard is narrowed to the two places the
loop moves the field, each with its own message, plus the three measured-nothing cases above, refused with
a message that says they are unmeasured rather than impossible.

**What stays refused**, each with its test in `tests/unit/test_residual_fields.py`:

- a feedback constraint (`fsm`, `atomic fsm`, `atomic fsm direction`): the field is a second unknown
  driven from the moment's error, so `F` at a fixed field is not the loop's map
  (`test_a_feedback_constraint_is_refused`). What would lift it is the field joining the state, as `ns`
  did for DFT+U, with Elk's update `B - tau (m(rho_out) - m_fix)` as its row of `F`; that is a design
  choice for the user (whether the secant update, which steps only on converged pairs, should then be
  replaced by the Elk row inside Newton) and was not taken here.
- `reducebf < 1`: the loop's fixed point depends on how many iterations the field had to fade, so no map
  of the density alone has it as its root (`test_a_decaying_field_is_refused`).
- `total direction`, `atomic texture` (`test_an_unmeasured_penalty_is_refused`) and a
  `LOCAL_MAGNETIC_FIELDS` card (`test_a_local_field_card_is_refused`): not measured, see above. Both tests
  hand `make_residual` a stand-in field, since both schemes need a noncollinear cell and the guard reads
  nothing but the field.
- a uniform field in a noncollinear run (`test_a_noncollinear_uniform_field_is_refused`) and a penalty in
  a collinear run (`test_a_collinear_penalty_is_refused`), added in the fix round: the same code, but only
  the collinear field and the noncollinear penalties are measured, and the one collinear penalty tried
  converged by neither route (below). A field together with a penalty never reaches the residual, since
  `build_system` refuses the pair at input as `pw.x` does
  (`test_a_field_together_with_a_penalty_never_reaches_the_residual`).
- `jvp='sternheimer'` with any field: `require_a_sternheimer_regime` still refuses it, because the response
  stack has no field term (`test_the_sternheimer_jacobian_still_refuses_a_field`). Every number in
  this record is from the default central-difference Jacobian action.
- `jvp='autodiff'` under any field (`test_autodiff_jacobian_is_refused_under_a_field`), refused again in
  the fix round at the top of `solvers.newton_krylov`, before the solver's first evaluation of `F` (the driver's warm-up
  mixing steps still run before it). It differentiates
  the same `F`, but nothing measures it: the verifier read it 0.53, 0.55 and 0.81 of `|J v|` off the central
  difference on the `fe-fsm` field cell at the mixing fixed point, against 2.0, 1.7 and 1.8 on the same cell
  without the field, while the central difference is stable to 2-5% under its epsilon. So the disagreement
  is autodiff's on this one-k-point smeared cell and existed before I73, not the field's, but no number backs
  the regime.

`test_a_field_the_loop_holds_fixed_builds_a_residual` builds the residual for a uniform field and for
`reducebf = 1.0` written out, and `test_a_noncollinear_penalty_builds_a_residual` for an `atomic` penalty
on a noncollinear silicon cell. The file holds thirteen tests, run on D22 with `-m 'not slow'`
(`review/incompat/I73/unit6.log`, worktree `w-inc-I73-v6` at `b2fad36b`) beside
`test_unreachable_combinations.py`, `test_magnetic_fields.py` and `test_sibling_refusals.py`: 105 passed,
1 deselected, no skips; the non-slow parts of `test_scf_solvers.py` and `test_magnetic_constraints.py`
(`unit7.log`, same worktree): 8 passed, 27 deselected, no skips. Earlier versions were `unit.log` to
`unit4.log`.

The `total` run printed one warning: 1 of 32 k-points failed on the Cholesky route of the eigensolver and
was re-solved with canonical orthogonalisation. The energy still landed 1.3e-8 Ry from `pw.x`. No pytest
setting turns a `UserWarning` into an error here, so the slow test does not fail on it.

The trap-check list was run against the diff. Nothing hit: the code change is a guard, every `np.asarray`,
`float` and `abs` added is in host-side test code, and the one refusal kept from elsewhere (the Sternheimer
field guard) belongs to the response stack, which has no field term. In the fix round the list was run again on its diff: the one
`np.asarray` added to library code reads `MagneticField.uniform` on the host inside `make_residual`, which
is never traced or differentiated, and the rest is guards and tests.

A collinear `atomic` penalty at `lambda = 0.05` on the one-k-point iron cell was tried as a third pair and
is not a check: the mixing loop does not converge on it in 300 iterations (6.4e-3 Ry), Newton stops at
1e-4 after 30 outer steps, and the loop after it converges in 20 iterations to 0.854 mu_B. The cell is
stiff under that penalty for both routes; it says nothing about the residual.

**The fix round.** A verifier refuted the two slow tests, not the physics: with `driver.make_residual`
handed a copy of the calculation with `magnetic_field = None`, so that Newton solves the field-free fixed
point, every assertion of both still passed, because the mixing loop that runs after the hand-over carries
the real field and finishes the job (`max_iterations` 200 and 300), and `solver.converged` holds since
Newton converged on its own residual. Both slow tests now assert that this loop took at most two
iterations. The same mutation through `notes/incompat/I73-harness/mut.py` (D22, `w-inc-I73-v6` at
`b2fad36b`, `review/incompat/I73/mut6-field-atomic.log` and `mut6-total.log`, one sample each):

| case | clean: loop iterations, result | mutant: loop iterations, result | mutant's other asserts |
|---|---|---|---|
| uniform field (`fe-fsm`, `b_field(3) = 0.01`) | 1, dE = -1.74e-8 Ry, dm = -1.74e-6 | 11, dE = 1.2e-9, dm = 1.2e-7 | all pass |
| `atomic` against `pw.x` | 1, E - pw.x = -7.79e-8 Ry | 14, E - pw.x = -3.31e-8 | all pass |
| `total` against `pw.x` | 1 (`h3-total.log`) | 41, E - pw.x = 1.17e-8 | all pass |

So the iteration count is the one assertion that reads differently under the alternative, and it fails
the mutant in every discriminating case. `atomic direction` is kept in the parametrization as a `pw.x`
regression only, and its docstring now says that it cannot discriminate (constraint energy 5e-33 at
convergence, so the field-free root is the same root).

The comment in the field test that called the total energy stationary was wrong: the reported total leaves
out the Zeeman term, and the verifier read dE = -dE_field to 1e-10 at `b_field(3) = 0.01` and `0.02`, so
`E + E_field` is the stationary sum and the E and E_field assertions are one first-order check, read
twice. The comment now says so. At `0.02` the pair still holds (dE = -4.75e-7 against the 1e-6
tolerance, dm = -2.4e-5 against 1e-4, one loop iteration).

**User guide**, `docs/guide/ground-state.md`, "Limits" note, last paragraph. No sentence there says the
Newton solver refuses a field (the closing list is about `"jvp": "sternheimer"` and stays true). Add after
"...and a magnetic field or a constrained moment.":

> The Newton solver itself runs with a uniform magnetic field (`B_field`) in a collinear run. It also
> runs with a constrained moment of the kind `atomic`, `atomic direction` or `total` in a noncollinear
> run. It gives an error for `fsm`, `atomic fsm` and `atomic fsm direction`, and for `reducebf` below 1.
> In these cases the field changes from one iteration to the next. It also gives an error for
> `total direction`, `atomic texture`, a `LOCAL_MAGNETIC_FIELDS` card, a uniform field in a noncollinear
> run, a constrained moment in a collinear run, and `"jvp": "autodiff"` with any field. These are not
> checked yet.

constrained moments" is about the physics, so no cell changes.
