## The feedback field (I73-feedback, 2026-10-17)

The user decided on 2026-10-16 that the Newton-Krylov solver carries the feedback field of a
fixed-spin-moment constraint in its solved state, as it carries the DFT+U `ns`. Branch
`incompat/I73-feedback` from `incompat-t2` at `e39c74f7`.

**What the fixed point is.** `run_scf` moves the field once an iteration, after the mix, by Elk's rule
`B <- B - tau (m(rho_out) - m_fix)` (`MagneticField.feedback`, `fsm_update = 'elk'`, the default), with
`tau = lambda` the gain and the moment read off the *output* density. Its fixed point is the pair
(`rho`, `B`) at which the density is self-consistent under `B` and the moment of that density is the
target. The residual now carries the driven leaf (`uniform` for `fsm`, `atomic` for the two per-atom
schemes) as the last block of `x`, and its row of `F` is that same rule as a function of the state
(`MagneticField.elk_driven`, with the error orthogonalised against the targets for `atomic fsm
direction`, exactly `_atomic_step`'s Elk branch). So the field rows of the residual are
`r_B = -tau (m - m_fix)`. The two candidates the task named, `m - m_fix` and the field update itself,
have the same root and differ by the factor `tau`, and the update is the one taken: `tau` is Elk's
estimate of `1/chi` (0.02 against the measured 0.022 on `fe-fsm.in`, `fields.py` `FSM_UPDATES`), so the
field block of the Jacobian, `-tau dm/dB`, sits near `-1`, the scale of the density block, and the
identity that `kerker_preconditioner` applies past the density is the right preconditioner for it. For
`atomic fsm direction` the rows along each target are identically zero; the Krylov vectors then never
move the parallel part of `B`, which the loop never moves either.

`unpack` keeps its four-tuple and the field block is read by `ScfResidual.unpack_field`; it is last in
the vector, so every other offset is unchanged. `run_scf` passes its own `field` (after
`_carried_driven_field`, so a field carried from a starting state is where Newton starts) and takes the
solved field back from `NewtonKrylovResult.field` before the loop, so the hand-over loop continues from
it rather than driving the field again from the input.

**The convergence measure.** `accuracy_of` adds `ScfResidual.field_accuracy(r) = |r_B|^2 / tau =
tau |m - m_fix|^2`, a field in Ry times a moment in mu_B squared, so a Rydberg number like `rho_ddot`.
Without it Newton stops wherever the density is self-consistent at the field it holds, which it is at
any field. At `conv_thr = 1e-11` and `tau = 0.02` it bounds the moment to about 2e-5 mu_B, where the
loop's own stop (`FSM_TOLERANCE`) is 1e-3 mu_B. **This is a choice and it shows in the raw total.** The
reported total leaves out the Zeeman term, and along the constrained family `dE/dm = B`, so a run that
stopped `dm` off its target reads `B . dm` off in `E`, first order. The mixing loop at its defaults stops
1.5e-4 mu_B off on `fe-fsm.in`, worth 1.7e-6 Ry; Newton at its defaults stops 7e-6 mu_B off, worth 8e-8
Ry. A measure that bounded the first-order error itself, `|B| |dm|`, would hold the moment to 1e-9 mu_B
at 1e-11; that is a choice for the user and was not taken here.

**The number.** Newton (`forcing = 0.5`, `warmup = 6`, defaults otherwise) against the mixing loop on the
same cell, the loop held to its own fixed point: `FSM_TOLERANCE` and `FSM_ANGLE_TOLERANCE` set to 1e-8
for the reference run only, both at `conv_thr = 1e-11` (D22, worktree `w-inc-I73-feedback-v3` at
`513aaaeb`, `review/incompat/I73-feedback/h3-fsm.log`, `h3-afsm.log`, `h3-afsmdir.log`, harness
`notes/incompat/I73-harness/feedback.py`, one sample each). "E at target" is `E + B . (m_fix - m)`,
which moves both runs to the target moment and leaves an error of order `dm^2/chi`.
