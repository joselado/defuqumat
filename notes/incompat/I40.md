by `Td` and time reversal (48 operations), the SCF at `conv_thr = 1e-11` with symmetry on.

| k-set | `max|sigma_wedge - sigma_whole| / max|sigma_whole|` |

| the wedge, completed by its group | **2.8e-10** (`optics-test.log`, D22, `w-inc-wedge-v8`) |

## What was missing, as found

The entry said "an axial-vector average of the antisymmetric part". What was missing is
the full group average of the rank-2 tensor `sigma_ab(omega)`, with each operation
including the time-reversed images: `response/conductivity.py:_wedge` returns the
`WedgeGroup` of `system/wedge.py` and the summed tensor is averaged over it. Under time
reversal `sigma_ab -> sigma_ba`, so on a non-magnetic wedge the antisymmetric part
averages to zero exactly; nothing axial-specific is needed. Still refused, by
`system/wedge.py:wedge_group`: a magnetic group (no magnetic cell measured against its
whole grid), a reduced explicit set on a `nosym` run, and a set the group does not tile
uniformly (a shifted grid).
