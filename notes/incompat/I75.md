# I75. `mixing_mode = 'ldos'` x tetrahedra, and as the warm-up of a Newton-Krylov solve: done

**The numbers.** All on D22, CPU, one core, branch `incompat/I75` at `c4e0e2b` (worktrees
`w-inc-I75` and `w-inc-I75-v2`, logs under `review/incompat/I75/`, harness `harness.py` there),
one sample each.

- Bloechl's tetrahedra under `ldos`, `tests/data/qe/al-tetrahedra.in` (`bulk1.log`): total
  **-4.1978985966 Ry** against `pw.x`'s **-4.19789860 Ry** (`tests/data/qe/reference.out.al-tetrahedra:202`),
  so within the printed digits (4e-11 from the printed value), and **2e-10 Ry** from this code's plain
  `anderson` run (-4.1978985968). It takes 7 iterations against `anderson`'s 5 and `pw.x`'s 5, as
  expected on a bulk metal, which has no sloshing to cure. Asserted by
  `tests/unit/test_ldos_mixing.py::test_tetrahedra_under_ldos_reach_pw_x` (at `TOTAL_ENERGY_RY = 1e-6`
  against `pw.x` and 1e-8 against the plain loop; 2.3 s on D22, so in the gate).
- The linear and optimised kinds on the same cell (no `pw.x` reference for them on this input):
  `tetrahedra_opt` -4.1660779055 under both `ldos` and `anderson`, `tetrahedra_lin` -4.1495861814 under
  both, so the same fixed point to below 1e-10 Ry, 6 iterations against 5.
- Kawamura's tetrahedra on the elongated ten-site cell, `tests/data/qe/al10-metal-tetra.in` (`al10-1.log`):
  **-41.8261345228 Ry** against `pw.x`'s **-41.82613452 Ry** (`reference.out.al10-metal-tetra:686`),
  3e-10 from the printed value, in **19 iterations** against 33 under this code's default mixing
  (`al10plain1.log`, -41.8261345250) and 55 in `pw.x`. This is the slow test
  `test_optimised_tetrahedra_under_ldos_reach_pw_x_on_ten_sites` (12.7 s), measured with the harness.
- The identity under the delta: the per-state weights `d wg_nk / d e_F` summed over states equal
  `tetrahedron_dos` at `e_F` to **rel 1e-10**, for all three kinds, at `nspin = 1` and at `nspin = 2`
  with two Fermi levels (`test_the_tetrahedron_ldos_integrates_to_the_tetrahedron_dos`, six cases). The
  two sides differentiate different functions (the corner weights against `_occupied_fraction`), and a
  stray `w_k` factor would read 2/nk times smaller.
- The Newton-Krylov warm-up under `warmup_mixing = 'ldos'`, aluminium with Gaussian smearing
  `degauss = 0.05` (`warm2.log`, one warm-up step): the Newton solve converges in 1 iteration (4 jvps,
  11 steps) and lands at **-4.1890549071 Ry**, **3.7e-9 Ry** from the mixing loop's -4.1890549108; the
  warm-up is screened, since the first Newton residual reads 1.57e-3 against 4.6e-4 after an `anderson`
  warm-up. With three warm-up steps (`warm1.log`) the cell is converged before Newton takes a step
  (0 iterations, 8.7e-10 Ry from the loop), which is why the slow test takes one. This is the slow test
  `test_the_ldos_warm_up_reaches_the_mixer_s_fixed_point`, measured with the harness. On an insulator
  the `ldos` warm-up is the `anderson` warm-up to the bit
  (`test_the_ldos_warm_up_on_an_insulator_is_the_anderson_warm_up`, in the gate).
- The preconditioner's clamp to `D >= 0` (Bloechl's correction and Kawamura's `wlsm` make the delta
  signed): the clamped share of the LDOS was **0.0** at every iteration of every bulk run above, and
  at most 2.2e-4 on the slab below.
- Gate (`gate2.log`, `-m 'not slow'`): 3772 passed, 2 failed, 73 skipped. The two failures are
  `tests/unit/test_check_guide.py`, which needs `yaml`, absent from D22's venv; the base gate
  (`review/incompat/night4/gate.log`) has the same two.

**What was not settled.** `benchmarks/al-slab.in` under `tetrahedra_opt` with `ldos` (`slab1.log`) did
not converge in 100 iterations (accuracy 3.6e-4 Ry, stalled from iteration 7 on). The old guard's
comment says plain `anderson` did not converge on that cell in 200 iterations either
(`notes/VACUUM-MIXING-NEXT.md`), so this is the cell, not the delta, but it means there is no measured
case where tetrahedra plus `ldos` beat smearing plus `ldos` beside vacuum. The guide sentence below says
only what is measured.

**What was missing, and where the entry was wrong.** The entry said "a smooth Fermi-level delta for
tetrahedra; a Gaussian substitute is unmeasured". No width and no Gaussian are needed. The tetrahedron
weights are a piecewise polynomial in `e_F`, so the method has its own delta: `d wg_nk / d e_F`, one
`jax.jvp` of `tetrahedron_weights_at` in `e_F` (`defuqumat/scf/tetrahedra.py:tetrahedron_fermi_weights`).
Its sum over states is the tetrahedron DOS exactly, and it carries no `w_k` factor (the zone measure is
already in through `spin / ntetra`), unlike the smeared branch. `Calculation.ldos_weights` uses it under
every tetrahedron kind, at one or two Fermi levels, and builds `_tetrahedra` itself when a geometry
change has dropped it (`test_the_tetrahedron_ldos_is_built_without_the_occupations_first`). The
`run_scf` guard is gone.

For the warm-up the entry was right about what was missing: the warm-up had no LDOS to hand the
preconditioner. `ScfResidual.step_states` now returns the eigenvalues beside `F(x)` and the states
(`step` calls it and drops them, so `jvp` differentiates the same map), and `_solve_residual` builds the
LDOS at `e_F` from each warm-up step's own states and hands it to a real-space `ldos_preconditioner` at
`mixing_beta`. With no states at `e_F` the LDOS is zero and the step is the plain one.

**What stays refused**, each with its test in `tests/unit/test_ldos_mixing.py`: the Newton-Krylov solve
itself under tetrahedra, which is a different guard (`residual.py`, "has no differentiable form here"),
so `warmup_mixing = 'ldos'` with tetrahedra still refuses there
(`test_newton_krylov_still_refuses_tetrahedra`); `ldos` in a response loop and in the ultracell
(`test_places_without_an_ldos_refuse_the_mode`). The test `test_tetrahedra_are_refused` is replaced by
the checks above.
