## What was missing, as found

The entry said "nothing symmetrises a point-sampled sum of `|psi(r)|^2`". That was true,
and the advisor's first suggestion (symmetrise a 3D field per energy) is not needed. The
local density of states at a point is a scalar field and `|psi_{gk}(r)|^2 =
|psi_k(g^-1 r)|^2`, so the whole zone's spectrum at `r` is the wedge's averaged over the
images `g(r) = r M + t` of the tip (`workflows/stm.py:_tip_orbit`). Time reversal leaves
`|psi|^2` unchanged, so only the unitary half of the group adds points; the cost is the
number of operations times the tip points, which is about what the whole grid costs in
k-points.

Still refused (by `system/wedge.py:wedge_group`): a magnetic group, under which a spin
channel or a magnetization is not a scalar; a reduced set on a `nosym` run; a set the
group does not tile. The unit test `test_a_wedge_with_no_group_to_complete_it_is_refused`
fires the second.
