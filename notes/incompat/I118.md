# I118. STM image, STS and tunnelling maps x applied magnetic field

**Status: done for the STM image (`run_stm`, `get_stm`) and the tunnelling spectrum
(`run_sts`, `get_sts`, at tip points or on a plane, the map at every energy), collinear
(`nspin = 2`) and noncollinear (`nspin_mag = 4`), under an applied field, uniform
(`B_field`) or on an atom (`LOCAL_MAGNETIC_FIELDS`, `System.atomic_b_field`).** The
refusal stays, by name, for a **constrained moment** (`constrained_magnetization` other
than `'none'`, whose field is a Lagrange multiplier: the same argument holds, but no
number was taken) and for the vertical transport
(`run_vertical_transport`), the momentum-resolved transport (`run_momentum_transport`)
and the QPI map (`run_qpi`), which share the guard and were not measured (see "What
stays refused").

## What was missing: nothing; the refusal was a misreading

The guard (`defuqumat/workflows/stm.py:_refuse_what_has_no_fermi_level`) said "the
field's energy is outside the reported total (PLAN.md P18), so the Fermi level this would
put the tip at is not the field-free one the image would be read as". The entry copied
that as the missing term ("the tip energy"). Neither half holds once the code around the
guard is read:

- The field's *energy* is left out of `total_energy` (QE's and Elk's convention,
  `scf/fields.py`), but nothing in the image reads the total. The *eigenvalues*, the
  wavefunctions and `fermi_energy` are those of the Hamiltonian with the field in it
  (`add_bfield` sits inside `v_of_rho`, here `Calculation.potential`), and the occupations
  count the electrons on them. The tip at that level sees the sample in the field, which
  is what a microscope in a field measures. Reading it as the field-free image is the
  reader's mistake, and the result now says it is a field run (`STMImage.field_scale`,
  `STMSpectrum.field_scale`: `None` without a field, the scale the SCF ended with
  otherwise).
- The re-solve on a denser k-set (`grid=`) already took the pair `(magnetic_field,
  field_scale)` from the result (`fixed_density_states(..., field=, field_scale=)`), and
  `field_scale` is the scale the final states were solved at: `reducebf`'s decay sits below
  the convergence `break` (`scf/driver.py`, `field_scale *= field.reducebf`).
- The ultracell's own image (`workflows/ultracell.py:_refuse_what_has_no_tip_energy`)
  never refused a field, for this reason; its docstring is corrected.

The field acts on the spin only (QE's `B_field`, `E = -int B . m`), so the image is the
Zeeman-split sample and carries no orbital (Landau) effect of the field. That is the
convention of every field run here, not something this item adds.

What changed in the code: `_refuse_what_has_no_fermi_level` takes `field_measured`, which
`run_stm` and `run_sts` pass; a constrained moment is refused by its own message whatever
the caller; the field refusal is reworded for the three callers that keep it; the image
and the spectrum carry `field_scale`.

## The numbers

Cell: `tests/data/qe/h-sheet-field.in` (new), the hydrogen sheet of `h-sheet.in` at
`celldm(1) = 4.0` (not 5.0), `nspin = 2`, `starting_magnetization = 0`, `degauss = 0.05`,
4x4x1 `nosym`, `conv_thr = 1e-12`, the field along z set by `System.with_b_field`. At 5.0
bohr the sheet is close to a Stoner instability (`m/B` = 17.5, 23 and 55 at `B` = 1e-4,
1e-3 and 1e-2 Ry), so the weak-field limit is not analytic on any useful range; at 4.0 it
is a plain paramagnet, `m/B` = 7.91, 7.91 and 8.10 at the same fields. Tip plane at
crystal height 0.72 (about 2.6 bohr above the sheet), (12, 12) points; spectrum at two
tip points, (0, 0, 0.72) and (0.5, 0.5, 0.72), nine energies `E_F(0) + [-0.1, 0.1]` Ry.
Spin channel: `spin="up"` (collinear) or `(0, 0, 1)` (spinor). Every distance is
`max|X(B) - X(0)| / max|X(0)|`. Harness `notes/incompat/I118-harness/harness.py`, log
`harness.log` beside it, run on this workstation pinned to cores 4-7, one thread, 11 s
(collinear) and 18 s (noncollinear) wall. Triton was not needed.

**1. The weak-field limit, the entry's check.** The convergence parameter is `B`. A
paramagnet's charge channel is even in `B` and its spin channel odd, so the distances fall
as `B^2` and `B`. Collinear (the noncollinear rows agree to the fourth digit):

| B (Ry) | E_F - E_F(0) | image charge | image spin | spectrum charge | spectrum spin |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0005 | +1.215e-05 | 3.693e-04 | 2.021e-02 | 4.880e-04 | 1.628e-02 |
| 0.001 | +4.907e-05 | 1.471e-03 | 4.107e-02 | 1.947e-03 | 3.274e-02 |
| 0.002 | +1.956e-04 | 5.784e-03 | 8.436e-02 | 7.706e-03 | 6.584e-02 |
| 0.004 | +7.618e-04 | 2.167e-02 | 1.741e-01 | 2.959e-02 | 1.306e-01 |
| 0.01 | +4.103e-03 | 9.221e-02 | 4.102e-01 | 1.486e-01 | 2.884e-01 |

Local exponents `log(d2/d1)/log(B2/B1)`:

| pair | image charge | image spin | spectrum charge | spectrum spin | E_F |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5e-4 to 1e-3 | 1.994 | 1.023 | 1.996 | 1.008 | 2.014 |
| 1e-3 to 2e-3 | 1.976 | 1.039 | 1.985 | 1.008 | 1.995 |
| 2e-3 to 4e-3 | 1.906 | 1.046 | 1.941 | 0.988 | 1.962 |
| 4e-3 to 1e-2 | 1.580 | 0.935 | 1.761 | 0.864 | 1.838 |

The exponents approach 2 and 1 as `B` falls, and the deviation shrinks with `B`, which is
the next order. `charge/B^2` reads 1477, 1471, 1446 and `spin/B` 40.4, 41.1, 42.2 at the
three smallest fields, so both coefficients are converging. The floor is the image at
`conv_thr = 1e-10` against `1e-12`: 2.1e-6 at `B = 0` (3.1e-6 noncollinear), 1.1e-5 in the
charge and 4.9e-5 in the spin at `B = 0.01`, far under the smallest distance read
(3.7e-4). (`1e-11` stops at the same SCF iteration as `1e-10` at `B = 0.01` and as `1e-12`
at `B = 0`, so it reads 0 or the same number and adds nothing.)

**Why this alone is not a check, and what is.** Every candidate error (a tip at the wrong
level, states without the field) is itself small in `B`, so a limit that tends to the
field-free image would also be read with such an error. Two arms read differently:

- *The tip at the field-free level*, the entry's own worry: the field run's image at
  `energy = E_F(0)` instead of `E_F(B)` is 4.06e-2 away from the right one at `B = 0.01`.
- *States without the field*: at `B = 0.01` the stored states' image against a re-solve
  on the same 4x4x1 k-set (`grid=(4, 4, 1)`, `conv_thr = 1e-11` for the band solve) under
  the field the SCF ended with: **8.98e-7 in the charge, 4.04e-6 in the spin**, and the
  re-solve's Fermi level is the SCF's to 1.2e-9 Ry (noncollinear: 7.96e-7, 3.65e-6,
  2.3e-8). The same re-solve with the field dropped from the Hamiltonian at the same
  density (`System.with_b_field(0)`, `magnetic_field=None`): **7.07e-2 in the charge and
  3.33e-1 in the spin**, Fermi level 2.7e-3 Ry lower. Collinear only: a spinor system
  without the field has `nspin_mag = 1` at `starting_magnetization = 0` and cannot hold
  the field run's four-channel density, so that arm reads nothing there.

**2. A local field, the spectrum on a plane, and the tests' own route** (second round,
`harness_local.py`, log `harness_local.log`):

- *A field on the atom* (`atomic_b_field = ((0, 0, 0.01),)`, the uniform field off,
  `constraint = 'none'`, moment 0.065): the stored states' image against the re-solve
  under the field, **3.84e-6 in the charge and 1.73e-5 in the spin**, tip energy 2.1e-7 Ry
  from the SCF's; with the field dropped, **3.38e-2 and 1.96e-1**, Fermi level 1.2e-3 Ry
  lower. The agreement is four times looser than the uniform field's, at the same
  `conv_thr`, and still four orders under the off arm.
- *The spectrum on a plane* (`height = 0.72`, `shape = (6, 6)`, five energies, the map at
  every energy), uniform field: distances 4.93e-4 and 2.00e-2 at `B = 5e-4`, 1.97e-3 and
  4.02e-2 at `1e-3`, exponents **2.000 (charge) and 1.008 (spin)**. It is the tip-point
  route through `_tip_points`, so it adds no new code, only the exercise of the map form.
- *Through `Calculator.get_stm`*, the slow test's route: re-solve on 8.977e-7 and
  4.043e-6, tip energy 1.2e-9 Ry, the same digits as the direct harness, and the
  calculator's SCF is the same object before and after the four calls, so the test's
  `1e-5` bound is not sitting on a second SCF's `conv_thr` floor (1.09e-5 between `1e-10`
  and `1e-12`).

A trap found on the way, in the tests rather than the code: a `Calculator` built from a
`System` (as `with_b_field` requires) does not read the input's `&electrons`, so its SCF
runs at the package's default `conv_thr`, and at that default the stored states and the
re-solve differ by 5.7e-3 with the Fermi level 3.2e-4 Ry apart. Passing `conv_thr` to
`get_scf` alone is not enough either: `get_stm` then finds a cache keyed by other options
and runs a second SCF at the default, and the exponents came out 2.47, 1.03, 0.07, 1.59.
The tests pass `conv_thr=1e-12` as the calculator's default.

## What stays refused

A constrained moment, in every caller of the guard (`NotImplementedError` naming
`constrained_magnetization`). The vertical transport, the momentum-resolved transport and the QPI map call the same
guard and keep the field refusal (reworded: "this quantity of a run with an applied
magnetic field is refused ... the same reading has not been measured here"). The physics argument above applies to them unchanged, but no number was
taken: an attempt to add the vertical transmission map to the harness (substrate plane at
crystal 0.28, tip at 0.72, (8, 8), `incoherent=False`) gave a field-free map whose maximum
was so small that every relative distance overflowed (1e42 to 1e223) and a spin-tip map
identical to zero, which is a set-up problem of that harness on this cell (the sheet in
`h-sheet.in` is the transport cell, at 5.0 bohr) and was not chased. The spin-spiral and
`tot_magnetization` refusals of the same guard are untouched and their tests still pass.

## Guide and README

`docs/guide/stm.md`, the STM image's "Limits" note, delete:

> A run in an applied magnetic field also gives an error, because the energy of the field
> is not in the total, so its Fermi level is not the one of the run without the field.

and put in its place:

> A run in an applied magnetic field works. The image and
> the spectrum use the states and the Fermi level of the run in the field, which is what
> a microscope sees in a field. `image.field_scale` says that the run had a field. The
> field acts on the spin only, so the image shows the Zeeman splitting and no orbital
> effect of the field. A run with a constrained moment
> (`constrained_magnetization`) still gives an error, because it has not been checked.

`docs/guide/stm.md`, the transport's "Limits" note, replace

> A spin spiral gives an error, because the spectrum samples each state on one plane-wave
> list, and so do a fixed `tot_magnetization` and an applied field, as for the image.

with

> A spin spiral gives an error, because the spectrum samples each state on one plane-wave
> list, and so does a fixed `tot_magnetization`, as for the image. An applied field also
> gives an error here. The image takes a field, but the transmission in a field has not
> been checked.

`README.md`: no cell changes. The rows "Scanning-tunnelling microscopy images" and
"Tunnelling spectra dI/dV" carry no regime note, and their `QE`/`Elk` ticks do not depend
