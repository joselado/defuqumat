## The number

`hierarchy_self_consistent(order=3)` against `propagate_orders(..., potential=...)`, the
propagation with the potential updated in time, on AlAs at 4 Ry, Gamma and `(0.25, 0.1, -0.05)`
at the superposition of atomic charges (the cell of `tests/regression/test_realtime.py`), the
same occupied states in both, `w = 0.05`, `eta = 0.01` Ha, `eta T = 20`, the field along `x`.
Relative to the largest component of each order, one sample each on D22 (one core):

| (n,m) | hartree, 400 steps | hartree, 800 | hxc, 400 | hxc, 800 | frozen (hxc, 800) | hxc without K2, K3 (800) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| (1,1) | 1.36e-5 | 8.3e-7 | 1.64e-5 | 3.7e-6 | 3.5e-3 | 3.7e-6 |
| (2,2) | 4.0e-6 | 1.3e-6 | 1.96e-5 | 3.5e-6 | 1.74e-2 | 5.21e-3 |
| (2,0) | 1.74e-5 | 3.3e-6 | 2.98e-5 | 5.3e-6 | 1.94e-2 | 5.57e-3 |
| (3,3) | 3.03e-5 | 9.8e-6 | 3.31e-5 | 5.2e-6 | 4.96e-2 | 1.35e-2 |
| (3,1) | 2.63e-5 | 3.7e-6 | 4.66e-5 | 7.6e-6 | 4.18e-2 | 2.29e-2 |

The convergence parameter is the propagation's steps a period (`dt`). Doubling it lowers the
discrepancy by 3 to 16, the midpoint rule's `dt^2` (a factor 4) on top of the start transient,
which is the same pattern as the frozen comparison of P137 (`test_the_orders_are_the_dense_hierarchy`:
1.4e-5 to 3.0e-5 at 400, 4.9e-6 to 7.6e-6 at 800). The first-order entries reproduce P136's
own numbers against the dense self-consistent solve (1.36e-5 and 1.64e-5 at 400, 8.3e-7 and
3.7e-6 at 800), which says the first-order part of the hierarchy is exact at this level and
what is left is the propagation's. The test tolerance is 2e-5 of each order's largest component
at 800 steps, twice the worst converged entry (9.8e-6).

What the agreement would read with the new terms off: the same run at `dv = 0` (frozen) is
3.5e-3 to 4.96e-2 away for `'hxc'` and 3.7e-2 to 1.8e-1 for `'hartree'`, and the `'hxc'`
hierarchy without the nonlinear exchange-correlation kernel (`nonlinear_kernel=False`) is 5.2e-3
to 2.3e-2 away at orders 2 and 3, against 3.5e-6 to 7.6e-6 with it. So the check sees the
induced potentials by a factor of 3000 and more, and the nonlinear kernel by 700 to 3000.

Cost on one D22 core: the hierarchy 48 s for all three runs (hartree, hxc, hxc without K2/K3),
32 to 36 outer GMRES products over the three orders (the first run of the three carries the compilation, 34 s against 3 to 4 s), outer residual 7e-9 to 1e-8; the propagation 85 to 108 s at 400 steps and 151 to 235 s at
800 steps.

## What was missing, as found in the code

The entry names "the induced potentials at 2w and 3w, each a fixed point per frequency, and the
velocity gauge's second-order density artefact". Read against the code, the missing pieces were
three, and only the first is in the entry:

1. The induced potential of every order and harmonic, `dv^(p)_m`, in the right-hand side of
   `c^(n)_m` as `dv^(p)_m' c^(n-p)_(m-m')` with weight one, the cross terms at order 2
   (`dv^(1) c^(1)`) and order 3 (`dv^(1) c^(2)`, `dv^(2) c^(1)`) included. `_orders_at` took one
   `dv_+` at first order only (the guard at its `order != 1`).
2. The density of every order and harmonic, `sum w conj(c_(n1,m1)) c_(n2,m2)` over
   `n1 + n2 = N`, `m2 - m1 = M`, where `_orders_at` returned the first-order cross density only.
3. The nonlinear kernel, not in the entry: `dv^(2) = K d2 + (1/2) K2[d1, d1]` and
   `dv^(3) = K d3 + K2[d1, d2] + (1/6) K3[d1, d1, d1]` for `'hxc'`. The real-time comparator has
   it by itself (its nested `jvp` goes through `calculation.potential`), so without it the
   hierarchy disagrees by 5e-3 to 2.3e-2 (the last column above). It is taken in time: at
   `2N + 1` phases the lower orders' densities are real, the `lam^N` coefficient of
   `U[rho0 + sum lam^n d_n(theta)]` is `N` nested `jvp`, and a discrete Fourier sum gives its
   harmonics exactly. The Hartree potential has none.

The orders are solved one after the other (no order feeds back to a lower one), each a GMRES
fixed point on the real and imaginary parts of `dv^(N)_M` for `M >= 0`, with `dv^(2)_0` kept
real. `hierarchy_linear_self_consistent` is now `hierarchy_self_consistent(order=1)`. Against
the old implementation (master 6c5b3a36 in `/l/ladovj1/w-inc-I102-base`) on the 4 Ry cell, `J_(1,1)`
at 0.02, 0.05 and 0.11 Ha with both kernels agrees to 2e-13 of `|J|` or better (`first-old.log`
against `first-new.log`).

**The wedge, at orders 2 and 3** (the path the lifted workflow guard opens, which the cell above
does not reach, having no symmetry). On AlAs at 6 Ry, 2x2x2, the field along [111] (six
operations in its little group), 1.5 eV, `eta = 0.3` eV, `get_nonlinear_spectrum(order=3)` on the
field's wedge against the whole unshifted mesh (`little_group=False`): 1.1e-10 to 6.0e-9 of each
order's largest component with the Hartree kernel, 8.8e-11 to 4.0e-9 with `'hxc'`, inside the outer
tolerance of 1e-8, while the update moves the components by 1.0e-2 to 1.6e-1 and 7.7e-3 to 9.3e-2.
This checks the symmetrisation of the densities of every order and of the nonlinear kernel's phase
samples. `k_batch=None` (one `vmap` over the wedge) against one k-point at a time: 5e-14 to
3e-11. The warning fired in all six runs. 35 to 38 outer products; 10 to 65 s a run. This ran at
406afca3; the propagation comparison above ran at b59b800d, whose hierarchy differs from the final
code only in compiling the nonlinear kernel once per order instead of once per frequency.

The artefact is **not a missing term**. The velocity gauge's frozen-sphere density
`sum_k w d^2 rho_k/dkappa^2` (P136: 7 per cent of rho on silicon's 2x2x2, falling by about three
a mesh step) is in the dynamics itself, so the real-time route has it in the same way, and the
two routes agree with it in. What it limits is a physical reading of `chi^(2)` and `chi^(3)` with
local fields on a coarse mesh. So it is a k-mesh convergence of the physics, and the lifted route
now warns about it by name (`run_nonlinear_spectrum`, `order >= 2` with the potential updated)
instead of refusing. The real-time route `get_harmonic_orders(potential='hxc')` does not warn.
