# I104. Any Sternheimer response x saw-tooth or Berry-phase field: partial, in the sense I36 was (the guard is lifted only for the caller that has its number; the dynamical matrix at `Gamma` lifted under `tefield` and `dipfield` for a fixed-occupation `nspin = 1` insulator on the whole sphere; every other response, and `lelfield` everywhere, still refused)

**The number.** There is no `ph.x` reference to take: `PHonon/` and `LR_Modules/` never read
`tefield` or `dipfield` (grep of QE 7.5, 2026-10-19; `lelfield` appears only in
`LR_Modules/non_scf_ph.f90:41`). So the check is an identity. A column of the force constants is
minus the derivative of the forces with respect to one coordinate, and the forces under the field
are validated against `pw.x` on these same inputs to 3e-7 Ry/bohr
(`tests/regression/test_sawtooth.py`). The cell is QE's ultrasoft CO molecule,
`tests/data/qe/efield-co.in` and `efield-co-dipole.in`, with two edits: `eamp = 0.01` instead of
0.001, so that the field's own effect on the matrix is above the tolerance, and real masses
(15.999, 12.011) instead of the files' 0.0. `pw.x` replaces a mass `<= 0` by its table value
(`PW/src/input.f90:1915-1918`), and this code does not, so the mass-weighted matrix is not finite and
the first D22 run died in `eigh` on it (`check1.log`). Not fixed here; it is a crash, not a wrong number. Every SCF at `conv_thr = 1e-13`, the response at
`tr2 = 1e-24`, `threshold = 1e-12`. D22, worktree `w-inc-I104` at `8d6f23ab` (the code is unchanged
since, only docstrings and tests), logs `review/incompat/I104/check2.log` and `check3.log`, one sample each
(the run is deterministic: the matrices of the two logs agree to every printed digit).

`compute_forces` subtracts the net force (QE's `sumfor`), so the central difference obeys the
translation sum rule exactly while the Sternheimer column carries the FFT grid's residue on a
molecule in a box (5.2e-3 Ry/bohr^2 on the C `z` column, 3.8e-3 on the O `z` one). The comparison
removes the column's mean over atoms first, which is the same projection. The convergence parameter is
the step `h`; residual `max|proj(D) - FD|` in Ry/bohr^2:

| column | `h = 0.02` | 0.01 | 0.005 | 0.0025 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `tefield`, C `z` | 1.6e-3 | 4.1e-4 | 1.1e-4 | **8.6e-6** |
| `tefield`, O `z` | 1.6e-3 | 5.1e-4 | 2.7e-4 | **1.5e-6** |
| `tefield`, C `x` | 8.3e-5 | 3.3e-5 | 2.6e-5 | (not run) |
| `tefield` + `dipfield`, C `z` | 1.6e-3 | 3.8e-4 | 1.1e-4 | **8.1e-5** |
| `tefield` + `dipfield`, O `z` | 1.6e-3 | 5.1e-4 | 2.5e-4 | **8.2e-5** |
| `tefield` + `dipfield`, C `x` | 8.2e-5 | 4.1e-5 | 2.6e-5 | (not run) |

The `z` columns fall roughly as `h^2` (factor 3.9 and 3.6 between steps on C `z`), which is the
anharmonicity of the CO bond (force constants 1.85 to 1.87 Ry/bohr^2, stretch at 1889 cm^-1). Under
`dipfield` the residual stops at 8e-5 instead of falling as `h^2` from 1.1e-4, which reads as a floor
rather than truncation. The floor is inferred, not measured: `test_sawtooth.py` has the two force
routes 2e-7 Ry/bohr apart under `dipfield` and 2e-8 without, and over `h = 0.0025` these give 8e-5 and
8e-6, which is where the two cases land (8.1e-5 and 8.6e-6). The direct measurement would be the same
stencil repeated at `conv_thr = 1e-11`; it was not run, because the third D22 run went to the raw-force
comparison after the first was lost to the masses. The transverse columns stay at 2.6e-5 by 0.005; they are 0.05 Ry/bohr^2 and were not taken
further. Without the projection (the raw force, `forces + total_before_correction/nat`) the residual is
1.5e-4 to 2.4e-4 at every step and does not fall, because the grid residue in the force is not smooth
in a sub-grid displacement; that is the reason for comparing the projected columns. The projected
comparison is blind to an error that shifts a column by the same amount on every atom, and the raw one
is what bounds such an error: at most 2.4e-4 Ry/bohr^2, with or without `dipfield`.

**What the agreement would read with the field or the term absent.** Same cell at `eamp = 0`: the
projected C `z` column moves by **4.9e-3** Ry/bohr^2 and the O `z` one by 1.2e-3 (C `x` by 6.6e-3),
against a residual of 8.6e-6 and 1.5e-6, so the check sees the field. Under `dipfield`, the matrix
with the new term switched off (`_core_charge_response` returning `None`, what master would give with
the guard lifted) moves the C `z` column by **0.60** Ry/bohr^2 raw (1.01 on O `z`) and misses the
central difference by **0.12** after the projection, where with the term it agrees to 8.1e-5. The
transverse column does not see the term (3.8e-10), as it should not: the dipole correction acts along
`edir` only.

**What was missing, against the entry.** The entry said "unchecked against any reference", and that
is right without `dipfield` and wrong with it. Read off `scf/sawtooth.py`'s single energy
`E_f(rho, tau)`:

- Without `dipfield` the potential `e2 eamp saw(x) d` depends on neither the density nor the
  positions. It sits in the ground state's `v_scf`, which the Sternheimer operator and the frozen
  `dV_bare` carry; the screening `jvp` of `v_of_rho` gets a zero tangent from it; the ions' term is in
  `forces/energy.py:frozen_energy` as `e_sawtooth`, so the frozen Hessian has it. Nothing was missing
  except the number.
- With `dipfield` the potential is `e2 (eamp - D) saw(x) d`, `D = ion_dip(tau) - el_dip(rho)`. The
  density half of `dD` is in the screening `jvp`. The ions' half, `d ion_dip/dtau` in `dV_bare`, was
  missing: `phonon._bare_displacements` freezes `v_scf`, and the slot that adds an explicit position
  dependence of the potential at a frozen density, `phonon._core_charge_response` (the `addcore` term),
  returned `None` whenever the dataset has no core charge. It now fires under `dipfield` too, with its
  body unchanged (one `jvp` of `at_positions(pos).potential(rho).v_scf`). That is the whole code change
  in the physics; the rest is the guard.

**The guard.** `require_a_sternheimer_regime` takes `sawtooth_ok` (default `False`); only
`phonon.dynamical_matrix` passes `True`, and with it the Berry-phase field is still refused
(`scf/sawtooth.py:refuse_berry_field`, split out of `refuse_sawtooth`). The matrix then refuses, under
the field, every sub-regime that was not run with it (`phonon._require_a_measured_sawtooth_regime`): a
metal, `nspin = 2`, a spinor, half-sphere storage (`K_POINTS gamma`), and the route that walks the k
axis a chunk at a time (its own assembly in `chunked_phonon.py`). An unshifted k-mesh is admitted
and was not measured (the check has one k-point); nothing in the field couples to `k`, which is the
argument for admitting it, and it is the one admitted regime without its own number. `nosym` is forced by `tefield`, so a
shifted automatic grid stays refused by `efield.require_a_symmetrisable_response`. The dielectric
tensor, Born charges, strain response, piezoelectric tensor, Raman tensor, electrostriction,
`make_sternheimer` (and through it the phonon at `q`, `phononq.py:1941`, and the electron-phonon
coupling built on it) and the Newton solver's Sternheimer Jacobian keep the saw-tooth refusal
unchanged, with its message now pointing here. What would lift each is the same kind of identity: for
the dielectric tensor and Born charges a finite difference in `eamp` of the dipole and of the forces.
Not decided here, and left as it was: `ph.x` would in principle run a `tefield` ground state without
`dipfield` (the saw-tooth is in `vltot`) and miss the term above with it, so a `ph.x` reference would be
one for the plain field only.

**Tests.** The entry's test (`tests/unit/test_sawtooth.py::test_gate_is_refused`) is about `gate` and
never reaches this guard; the item had no test. New, not slow, `tests/unit/test_sawtooth_response.py`
(9 tests, 22 s with the neighbouring files on D22, `unit1.log`): the default still refuses `tefield`,
`lelfield` is refused with and without `sawtooth_ok`, only `response/phonon.py` passes
`sawtooth_ok=True` (read from the source), a real `Calculation` under the field is refused by the default,
and the matrix refuses a metal, `nspin = 2` and half-sphere storage under the field. Run on D22 with
`tests/unit/test_sawtooth.py`, `test_partial_phonon_refusals.py`, `test_sibling_refusals.py`,
`test_partial_walked_phonons.py`, `test_robust_mixing.py`: 134 passed. New, slow,
`tests/regression/test_sawtooth_phonons.py`: the two `z` columns of both cases against the central
difference at `h = 0.0025` to 2e-4 (largest measured 8.2e-5), the field visible (4.9e-3 > 2e-3), and
the column without the ions' dipole missing the forces (0.12 > 2e-3). Not run (slow); its numbers are
the harness's (`review/incompat/I104/harness3.py` and `harness.py` on D22, the same computation).
