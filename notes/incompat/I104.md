# I104. Any Sternheimer response x saw-tooth or Berry-phase field: partial, in the sense I36 was (the guard is lifted only for the caller that has its number; the dynamical matrix at `Gamma` lifted under `tefield` and `dipfield` for a fixed-occupation `nspin = 1` insulator on the whole sphere; every other response, and `lelfield` everywhere, still refused)

**The number.** There is no `ph.x` reference to take: `PHonon/` and `LR_Modules/` never read
`tefield` or `dipfield` (grep of QE 7.5, 2026-10-19; `lelfield` appears only in
`LR_Modules/non_scf_ph.f90:41`). So the check is an identity. A column of the force constants is
minus the derivative of the forces with respect to one coordinate, and the forces under the field
are validated against `pw.x` on these same inputs to 3e-7 Ry/bohr
(`tests/regression/test_sawtooth.py`). The cell is QE's ultrasoft CO molecule,
`tests/data/qe/efield-co.in` and `efield-co-dipole.in`, with two edits: `eamp = 0.01` instead of
0.001, so that the field's own effect on the matrix is above the tolerance, and real masses
(15.999, 12.011) instead of the files' 0.0. `pw.x` replaces a mass `<= 0` by its table value
(`PW/src/input.f90:1915-1918`), and this code does not, so the mass-weighted matrix is not finite and
the first D22 run died in `eigh` on it (`check1.log`). Not fixed here; it is a crash, not a wrong number. Every SCF at `conv_thr = 1e-13`, the response at
`tr2 = 1e-24`, `threshold = 1e-12`. D22, worktree `w-inc-I104` at `8d6f23ab` (the code is unchanged
since, only docstrings and tests), logs `review/incompat/I104/check2.log` and `check3.log`, one sample each
(the run is deterministic: the matrices of the two logs agree to every printed digit).

`compute_forces` subtracts the net force (QE's `sumfor`), so the central difference obeys the
translation sum rule exactly while the Sternheimer column carries the FFT grid's residue on a
molecule in a box (5.2e-3 Ry/bohr^2 on the C `z` column, 3.8e-3 on the O `z` one). The comparison
removes the column's mean over atoms first, which is the same projection. The convergence parameter is
the step `h`; residual `max|proj(D) - FD|` in Ry/bohr^2:

The columns 0.02 to 0.005 and 0.0025 are the implementer's (`check2.log`, `check3.log`); 0.0035
and 0.00125 are the verifier's, at `1bf5e801`, the same physics code
(`/l/ladovj1/review/incompat/I104-ver/ver.log`, one sample, deterministic). From 0.02 to 0.005 the
`z` columns fall roughly as `h^2` (factor 3.9 and 3.6 between steps on C `z`), which is the
anharmonicity of the CO bond (force constants 1.85 to 1.87 Ry/bohr^2, stretch at 1889 cm^-1).
Below about 0.0035 bohr they do not: the residual goes up and down with `h` (plain field, C `z`:
1.1e-4, 8.6e-6, 1.4e-4 at 0.005, 0.0025, 0.00125), because the central difference of the
projected forces carries a deterministic error of about 3.5e-7 Ry/bohr that is rough in the
position. `conv_thr` does not move it: the `dipfield` C `z` column at `h = 0.0025` reads 9.3e-5 at
`conv_thr = 1e-11` against 8.1e-5 at 1e-13. Moving `emaxpos` to 0.3 gives the same picture (3.6e-6
at 0.0025, 1.7e-4 at 0.00125). So the small entries at `h = 0.0025` (8.6e-6, 1.5e-6, 3.6e-6) are
zero crossings of that roughness, not the agreement, and `dipfield` is not less accurate than the
plain field.

To take the truncation out without going into the roughness, the Richardson pair

The C `z` column agrees to 2e-5. The O `z` column keeps an offset of up to 1.9e-4 (1.5e-4 to
1.9e-4 at these two pairs, 8.4e-5 to 1.1e-4 at the verifier's 0.016/0.008 pair at `eamp = 0.01`,

arms, the same with and without `dipfield`, so it is not the field's and it
is not the new term's (which is 0.12, below). Its origin was not looked for; the O `z` column also
carries the larger grid residue of the two before the projection. **The demonstrated agreement is
about 2e-4 Ry/bohr^2 (largest 1.9e-4), one part in 10^4 of the force constants, with or without
`dipfield`.** The transverse columns stay at 2.6e-5 by 0.005; they are 0.05 Ry/bohr^2 and were not
taken further.

Without the projection (the raw force, `forces + total_before_correction/nat`) the residual does
not fall with `h`, because the grid residue in the force is not smooth in a sub-grid displacement;
that is the reason for comparing the projected columns. The projected comparison is blind to an
error that shifts a column by the same amount on every atom, and the raw one is what bounds such an
error: over every step and arm logged it is at most 6.3e-4 Ry/bohr^2 (plain field, O `z`, `h =
0.01`; 5.3e-4 at `h = 0.00125`, 4.1e-4 at `emaxpos = 0.3`), with or without `dipfield`.

**What the agreement would read with the field or the term absent.** Same cell at `eamp = 0`: the
projected C `z` column moves by **4.9e-3** Ry/bohr^2 and the O `z` one by 1.2e-3 (C `x` by 6.6e-3),
against an agreement of about 2e-4, so the check sees the field (25 times the agreement). Under `dipfield`, the matrix
with the new term switched off (`_core_charge_response` returning `None`, what master would give with
the guard lifted) moves the C `z` column by **0.60** Ry/bohr^2 raw (1.01 on O `z`) and misses the
central difference by **0.12** after the projection, where with the term it agrees to about 2e-4.
The miss does not depend on the step: 0.1252, 0.1240, 0.1237, 0.1237, 0.1235, 0.1236 at `h = 0.02`,
0.01, 0.005, 0.0035, 0.0025, 0.00125 (the term-off column of `check2.log`, projected, against the
logged differences; arithmetic, no new run). The
transverse column does not see the term (3.8e-10), as it should not: the dipole correction acts along
`edir` only.

**What was missing, against the entry.** The entry said "unchecked against any reference", and that
is right without `dipfield` and wrong with it. Read off `scf/sawtooth.py`'s single energy
`E_f(rho, tau)`:

- Without `dipfield` the potential `e2 eamp saw(x) d` depends on neither the density nor the
  positions. It sits in the ground state's `v_scf`, which the Sternheimer operator and the frozen
  `dV_bare` carry; the screening `jvp` of `v_of_rho` gets a zero tangent from it; the ions' term is in
  `forces/energy.py:frozen_energy` as `e_sawtooth`, so the frozen Hessian has it. Nothing was missing
  except the number.
- With `dipfield` the potential is `e2 (eamp - D) saw(x) d`, `D = ion_dip(tau) - el_dip(rho)`. The
  density half of `dD` is in the screening `jvp`. The ions' half, `d ion_dip/dtau` in `dV_bare`, was
  missing: `phonon._bare_displacements` freezes `v_scf`, and the slot that adds an explicit position
  dependence of the potential at a frozen density, `phonon._core_charge_response` (the `addcore` term),
  returned `None` whenever the dataset has no core charge. It now fires under `dipfield` too, with its
  body unchanged (one `jvp` of `at_positions(pos).potential(rho).v_scf`). That is the whole code change
  in the physics; the rest is the guard.

**The guard.** `require_a_sternheimer_regime` takes `sawtooth_ok` (default `False`); only
`phonon.dynamical_matrix` passes `True`, and with it the Berry-phase field is still refused
(`scf/sawtooth.py:refuse_berry_field`, split out of `refuse_sawtooth`). The matrix then refuses, under
the field, every sub-regime that was not run with it (`phonon._require_a_measured_sawtooth_regime`): a
metal, `nspin = 2`, a spinor, half-sphere storage (`K_POINTS gamma`), and the route that walks the k
axis a chunk at a time (its own assembly in `chunked_phonon.py`), and, since the fix round, a PAW
dataset, and DFT+U, which `require_a_sternheimer_regime` already refused upstream (the check is ultrasoft without a Hubbard term). An unshifted k-mesh is admitted
and was not measured (the check has one k-point); nothing in the field couples to `k`, which is the
argument for admitting it, and it is the one admitted regime without its own number. `nosym` is forced by `tefield`, so a
shifted automatic grid stays refused by `efield.require_a_symmetrisable_response`. The dielectric
tensor, Born charges, strain response, piezoelectric tensor, Raman tensor, electrostriction,
`make_sternheimer` (and through it the phonon at `q`, `phononq.py:1941`, and the electron-phonon
coupling built on it) and the Newton solver's Sternheimer Jacobian keep the saw-tooth refusal
unchanged, with its message now pointing here. What would lift each is the same kind of identity: for
the dielectric tensor and Born charges a finite difference in `eamp` of the dipole and of the forces.
Not decided here, and left as it was: `ph.x` would in principle run a `tefield` ground state without
`dipfield` (the saw-tooth is in `vltot`) and miss the term above with it, so a `ph.x` reference would be
one for the plain field only.

## Fix round (2026-10-20)

The verifier (`/l/ladovj1/review/incompat/I104-ver/ver.log` on D22, at `1bf5e801`) confirmed the
physics digit for digit (the term moves C `z` by 0.601, the miss without it 0.1235, the field's
effect 4.90e-3) and refuted four precision claims. No new SCF was run in this round: the physics
code the numbers were taken on is unchanged, and every new figure above is either the verifier's
log or arithmetic on the logged columns of `check2.log`.

1. *"The identity holds to 8.6e-6 and 1.5e-6 without `dipfield` and 8.2e-5 with it, and the
   residual falls with `h`."* Refuted: at `h = 0.00125` the plain field reads 1.4e-4 on both
   columns, so the small entries at 0.0025 are zero crossings of a deterministic roughness of
   about 3.5e-7 Ry/bohr in the force difference, which `conv_thr` does not move (9.3e-5 at 1e-11
   against 8.1e-5 at 1e-13). Restated as about 2e-4 Ry/bohr^2 with or without `dipfield`, with the
   Richardson pair at two step pairs as the convergence picture (C `z` 1.4e-5 to 2.5e-5, O `z` 1.5e-4
   to 1.9e-4, the same in both arms), in the record, the two docstrings of `response/phonon.py`,
   the regression test's comments and the guide sentence (now "to about 2e-4 Ry/bohr^2").
2. *The noise floor `2e-7/h` inferred from the gap between the two force routes.* Refuted both ways
   (it predicts 1.6e-4 under `dipfield` at 0.00125, measured 1.7e-5; 1.6e-5 without, measured 1.4e-4).
   Deleted from the record and the test.
3. *The raw-force bound of 2.4e-4.* Refuted (5.3e-4 measured). Restated as the largest raw residual
   logged over every step and arm, 6.3e-4 Ry/bohr^2 (plain field, O `z`, `h = 0.01`, `check3.log`).
4. *`COLUMN = 2e-4` set from one step.* Now 4e-4, from the roughness (up to 1.7e-4) and the
   Richardson offset (up to 1.9e-4); still 12 times under the field's effect and 300 times under the
   missing term. The term-off miss, the new term switched off, does not depend on the step: 0.1252
   to 0.1235 from `h = 0.02` to 0.00125.

Two of the verifier's "uncovered, not blocking" points are closed as guards rather than claims:
the matrix under the field now refuses a PAW dataset by name (it was admitted with no number), and
a DFT+U guard was added beside it. DFT+U was not an opening: `dynamical_matrix` already refuses it
upstream in `require_a_sternheimer_regime` (`sternheimer.py:1653`, "Sternheimer response with a
Hubbard U is not implemented"), with or without the field, so the new DFT+U branch is a second,
unreachable guard, exercised only on the stand-in of the unit test (corrected at integration from
the verifier, `/l/ladovj1/review/incompat/I104-ver2/guards.log`). Admitted and unmeasured, beside
the unshifted k-mesh: a norm-conserving dataset, taken as the special case of ultrasoft, and a
dataset with a core charge under `dipfield` (the CO pseudopotentials have none, so the combined
core-charge and ion-dipole term is not run). New fast tests in `tests/unit/test_sawtooth_response.py` call
`_require_a_measured_sawtooth_regime` directly on a stand-in for the measured regime: it passes, and
it refuses the walked route, a spinor, PAW and DFT+U, each by its words. The unshifted k-mesh stays
admitted and unmeasured, as stated under "The guard".
