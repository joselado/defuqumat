# I24. Site angular momenta x spin spiral: done (norm-conserving)

**Measured.** NiI spiral at `q = b3/4` against the four-cell supercell at one
density: `<S>`, `<L>` and the Löwdin charge of each of the eight supercell atoms
equal the spiral's on its nickel or iodine, turned about `z` by `-q . R_j`, to
**2.1e-10** (nickel `<S>` = 0.344 hbar, iodine 7.3e-3; `<L>` quenched to 2e-10,
as it must be without spin-orbit coupling). The turn of the opposite sense
misses by 0.69. One D22 run (`review/incompat/spiral/v3-nii.log`). Test:
`tests/regression/test_spiral_supercell.py::test_the_spiral_site_moments_are_the_supercell_moments`
(slow). The entry said "Test: none"; the old refusal was a plain
`NotImplementedError` with no test, and it is now replaced by the check.

**What was missing.** As the entry said, projections on both spheres, and less
than it implied: `build_atomic_projectors` on the calculation's own
`basis_kpoints` already builds the orbitals on the doubled `2 nk` list. The
site matrix now projects the up component on rows `[:nk]` and the down on rows
`[nk:]`. The result is the laboratory site matrix of the atoms in the home
cell, not a rotating-frame one, because the pair of projections is the
projection of the laboratory spinor.

**Still refused**: an ultrasoft or PAW spiral (`S` on each sphere, no check),
and a spiral from a streamed store, since the projection blocks are built on one
list of `nk`. On a spiral the orbitals are built whole rather than a block of
k-points at a time, so its peak is the whole `(2 nk, npwx, natomwfc)` set, twice
the non-spiral whole build.

**User guide**, `docs/guide/spin-orbit.md`, Limits: replace "A spin spiral also
gives an error, because its two spinor components do not share one set of
atomic orbitals, and so does a run with `K_POINTS gamma`." with "A
norm-conserving spin spiral works, and each spinor component is projected on
the atomic orbitals of its own sphere. An ultrasoft or PAW spiral gives an
error, and so does a run with `K_POINTS gamma`." `docs/guide/spirals.md`:
remove "no site angular momenta," from the last Limits sentence.
