# I3. Gamma phonon x nonmagnetic spinor: partial, the refusal stays

Status: **partial**. The spinor assembly of the `Gamma` dynamical matrix is written and passes
the identity and the AlAs check, and the refusal stays, because spin-orbit bismuthene, the one
cell on which the spin-orbit terms are large enough to resolve, does not agree with `ph.x`, and
I could not find the reason within the stop rule (three D22 runs).

## What was missing, and where the entry was wrong

The entry said "the caller's assembly on a two-component state". That is right in kind and the
assembly turned out to need three concrete things in `defuqumat/response/phonon.py`, everything
else being shape-agnostic already or made spinor-aware by the Born charges (`_raw_mixed_state`,
`_multiplier_response`, the bare perturbation through `at_positions`, and the loop's stop, which
was already `tr2 / npol` as `dfpt_kernels.f90:435` has it):

- `noncollinear=True` on `require_a_sternheimer_regime` in `dynamical_matrix`;
- `spinors=True` on both `frozen_energy` calls in `_force_constants` (the functional has had a
  spinor branch since P46, and the default `False` refused it);
- `qq_so` and the `(nbnd, 2, npwx)` reshape in `overlap_derivatives`, which contracted the scalar
  `qq` on an `npwx`-long band.

Two refusals were added beside it: the walked (k-chunked) route refuses a spinor by name, since
`chunked_phonon.py` has its own collinear `qq` and no spinor energy; and a textured spinor
(`nspin_mag = 4`) refuses for the dielectric tensor's reason (5.3 per cent from `ph.x` on
`i-atom-soc`). The new `_require_no_spinor` refuses the nonmagnetic spinor too, quoting the
numbers above.

What would lift it, ranked:

1. bismuthene's DFPT at `tr2 = 1e-18` and 1e-20 against the same finite-difference row: if
   D(0z, 0z) walks toward 0.0444 the discrepancy is the loop's stop, not the spinor;
2. the same finite difference against DFPT on a scalar ultrasoft insulator with vacuum, a GGA and
   a core charge, which says whether the 11 per cent belongs to the spinor at all;
3. `ph.x` on bismuthene at a tighter `tr2_ph` (a T4 run, its output committed under
   `tests/data/qe/`).

## User guide and README

Nothing changes now, since the refusal stays. Once it is lifted, the sentence to change is in
`docs/guide/phonons.md`, the Limits note, lines 386 to 388:

> The phonons, the Raman tensors, the strain response, the elastic constants and the
> electrostriction tensors give an error for a noncollinear or spin-orbit run, and for
> `nspin = 2`.

and it would become

> The phonons at $\Gamma$ work for a noncollinear or spin-orbit run without a net
> magnetization. The Raman tensors, the strain response, the elastic constants and the
> electrostriction tensors give an error for a noncollinear or spin-orbit run, and so do the
> phonons with a magnetization. All of them give an error for `nspin = 2`.

The README feature-table row `| **Phonons at $\Gamma$** | calc.get_phonons() | ✓ | ✓ |` needs no
cell change; its spinor regime would be stated in the guide page only.
