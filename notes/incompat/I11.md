## What was missing

**One call.** The entry named "each caller's assembly on a two-component state, and the elastic
constants' first-order spinor wavefunctions". The assembly needed nothing: the bare perturbation,
the solve, `dvscf` and the eigenvalue response already go through the spinor Hamiltonian and
`solver.response_density`. What was wrong was the **strained density**:
`density_of_strained_states` and `_frozen_density_response.mixed` called the collinear `sum_band`
directly, which a spinor of length `2 npwx` cannot go through. Both now take
`strain._smooth_density`, which is the spinor branch of `SternheimerSolver.density_parts` at the
strained cell. The elastic constants needed `energy_at(..., spinors=True)` when the run is a spinor
(`elastic.py`), and the first-order spinor wavefunctions were already the strain response's
`dpsi`.

**Still refused, by name, in `strain.require_a_measured_spin_regime`.**
- A noncollinear magnetization (`nspin_mag = 4`). The magnetization would have to be symmetrised
  as an axial field under a rank-2 perturbation, and that has never been run.
- An ultrasoft or PAW spinor. `overlap_derivatives` and `mixed_becsum` contract `vkb` with one
  `npwx` block, and the spinor `S'` and `becsum` in the strain coordinate are not written.

The walked route of the elastic constants (`_walked_elastic_constants`) was refused for a spinor
in `4c51e43` and is lifted in the final commit on the measured 4.6e-14 against the whole route
(`run1-ABWE.log`). No committed test asserts the walked spinor route.
`test_streamed_strain.py` holds it for `nspin = 1`.

So the item is **partial** in this sense: the strain response and the elastic constants are
lifted for a spinor with no magnetization (norm-conserving, with and without SOC), each with the
numbers above. Electrostriction and piezo keep their refusals untouched.

**Electrostriction is not lifted.** With the bare guard bypassed, the spinor run fails in the
electrostriction assembly's own density (`Incompatible types for broadcasting: input
type=complex128[380] and requested type=complex128[190]`, `run2-E.log`): it calls the collinear
`sum_band` on spinors, the same defect the strain response had. The fix is probably the same one
call, but it is unmeasured. Piezo: not run.
