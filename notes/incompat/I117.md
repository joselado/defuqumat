# I117. Ultracell noncollinear seed x compensated (antiferromagnetic) reference

**Status: done, for a collinear compensated reference.** `run_ultracell` turns a
compensated reference about its Neel axis. **Kept refused: a canted or noncoplanar
compensated reference** (`lambda_2 / lambda_1` of `int m m^T` above
`SEED_COLLINEAR_TOL = 1e-2`), which has no single axis to turn from. **Also kept, and not
a refusal: the field's own seed on a compensated reference** (`seed_from_field`) is still
skipped with a reason, and `True` raises, because turning the Neel vector onto the field
is not how an antiferromagnet answers a field (spin flop).

## The number

Measured on Triton, `batch-csl`, 4 CPUs. The first run was job 21118738 at `f93ccf76`,
with `neel_axis` put into the driver by the harness (`--patch` route). The second was job

The setup:

- **Unit cell.** Two hydrogens 5.5 bohr apart along `x` in an `(11, 5.5, 5.5)` bohr box,
  noncollinear, starting moments `+z` and `-z`. It converges, in 11 iterations, to the
  collinear antiferromagnet: net moment 1.9e-6 against an absolute 0.805, and
  `lambda_2 / lambda_1 = 5.6e-12`. The axis is `+z` to 3e-6.
- **Ultracell.** `(3, 1, 1)` at kgrid `(1, 2, 2)` on the Kramers-closed default basis,
  seeded with `s(x) = (cos 2 pi x/3, sin 2 pi x/3, 0)`.
- **Reference.** The explicit six-atom supercell, seeded per species with what
  `R(+z -> s)` does to the two sublattices. It converges to a flat 120-degree pattern:
  steps of 120 to 3e-7 degrees, cone 1.5e-12 degrees.
- **Compared.** The energy per unit cell, and the site moments (the magnetization in a
  1.5 bohr sphere about each atom, the same function on both boxes).

| `nbnd` | `E` above the supercell, Ry/cell | largest site error / largest site moment | iterations |
|---|---|---|---|
| 8 | 6.21e-6 | 3.53e-4 | 8 |
| 16 | 8.46e-7 | 4.58e-5 | 8 |

Both columns are positive and fall with `nbnd`, the method's one convergence parameter,
by 7.3x in energy and 7.7x in sites. This is the variational truncation approaching the
supercell from above.

**What would read differently without the term.** Before this change the run is refused,
so the right comparison is a wrong axis. The control is the same ultracell seeded with
`-s`, which is exactly what the opposite sign convention of the axis does. It gives:

- the same energy, -1.8706149000204224 against -1.870614900020409;
- site error 2.0 against the supercell;
- site error 3.5e-4 against the reversed supercell.

So the site check sees the sign of the axis, where the energy and the Fourier norm that
the ferromagnetic helix tests compare are both blind to it. An axis read from the net
moment (the old code's direction of a 1.9e-6 residue, along `(-0.66, -0.47, ...)` here)
would have put the texture on a cone, not on the supercell's flat helix.

## What was missing, as found in the code

The entry said "a per-site or staggered reference axis". That is not needed for a
collinear antiferromagnet. The existing pointwise rotation `R(e -> s)` already turns the
`+e` sublattice onto `s` and the `-e` one onto `-s`. The only missing piece was `e`. It is
now the principal eigenvector of `M = int m m^T dr` (`seed.neel_axis`, which
`reference_axis` returns).

The sign of `e` flips the whole seeded texture, so it is fixed by a convention. It is the
sign of `m . e` at the first atom, in the unit cell's order, whose own grid point carries
`|m . e|` above `SEED_NET_TOL` of the largest on the box. So `s` is the direction of the
first magnetic atom. `run_ultracell` passes the atoms' grid points as `sites`; without
them `neel_axis` raises.

A ferromagnet's axis is its net moment, unchanged. The non-convergence advice for a
compensated reference now names a Neel seed (`_noncollinear_advice`).
