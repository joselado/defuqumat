# I8. Sum-over-states `chi_0` x spinor (nonmagnetic, with and without spin-orbit)

Status: done for a nonmagnetic spinor (`noncolin`, `nspin_mag = 1`), with and without
spin-orbit coupling. A spinor carrying a moment (`nspin_mag = 4`) and a spin spiral stay
refused, each now by its own message.

## What was missing, and where the entry was wrong

The entry put the guard at `chi0.py:346` (the `noncolin` branch) and said the spiral branch
at `:352` was unreachable because `noncolin` fires first. **Neither branch was reachable**:
a noncollinear `Calculation` reads `nspin = 4`, so the collinear `nspin != 1` refusal fired
first on every spinor, magnetic or not, spiral or not (measured on four cells:
`si-epsilon-unshifted-nosym` with `noncolin`, `i-atom-soc`, `h-chain-spiral`,
`se-trigonal-soc`, all refused with "restricted to nspin = 1 ... nspin = 4").

The missing term was as the guard said, two-component pair densities with a spin trace,
plus one bookkeeping fault the entry did not name:

- `_pair_terms`: the states are reshaped to `(nbnd, npol, npwx)`, both components go
  through the one `fft_index` at `k`, and the pair density is
  `sum_s conj(u_i,s) u_j,s`. For `nspin_mag = 1` this charge-charge block is the whole
  response the Coulomb and unpolarized kernels see. The head needed nothing:
  `VelocityOperator` is a `jvp` of the spinor `H(k)`, `dvan_so` included.
- **The occupied count was `nelec / 2` in four places** (`chi0._occupied_bands`,
  `workflows/tddft.run_absorption`, its `_default_nbnd` and `_static_residual`'s slice of
  the occupied states handed to the Sternheimer referee). A spinor band holds one electron,
  so all four now take `scf/occupations.occupied_counts(calculation)[0]`. Left as it was,
  the referee would have been handed half the occupied states and the residual would have
  read as a plausible band truncation.

The guards now read: spiral first (it carries a moment, so it would otherwise reach the
`nspin_mag` message), then `nspin = 2`, then a spinor with `nspin_mag != 1` ("textured
spinor": the exchange-correlation kernel couples the charge to the magnetization response,
and the Sternheimer referee refuses the same regime, P83). Unit tests in
`tests/unit/test_tddft_machinery.py` assert each fires on a cell that is that regime
(`i-atom-soc`, `h-chain-spiral`, `h-chain-afm`) and that the two nonmagnetic spinor cells
are accepted. An RPA `chi_0` of a textured spinor would in principle be right with the same
charge-only pair density (Hartree sees only the charge), but it has no referee here and
stays refused.
