# I91. Shift current x ultrasoft or PAW: partial, the refusal stays

**The number.** On a frozen sphere at the generic point `k = (0.11, 0.23, 0.31)` (crystal) of
ultrasoft AlAs (`alas-piezo-tiny.in`, 159 plane waves) and its PAW twin
(`alas-piezo-tiny-paw.in`), `r^{c;a}` (`a = x`, `c = y`, the 8x8 valence-conduction block,
relative error on the elements above 1 per cent of the largest) from the sum rule, against a
parallel-transport central difference of the generalised dipole whose phases come from the
overlap primitive `<psi(k)|S(k, k')|psi(k')>` with `q_ij(k' - k)`. D22, CPU, one core, one sample
each (`review/incompat/I91/h-v4-*.log`, `h-v5-*.log`; worktrees `w-inc-I91-v4`, `-v5`).

| | step 1e-3 | 5e-4 | 2.5e-4 |
|---|---|---|---|
| ultrasoft, with the new term, `nbnd` = 159 (complete) | 4.07e-3 | 4.11e-3 | 4.12e-3 |
| ultrasoft, the term off (bare `d^2H/dk^2`) | 5.29e-2 | 5.26e-2 | 5.25e-2 |
| ultrasoft, `K` products kept, `L` zeroed | 8.59e-3 | 8.86e-3 | 8.93e-3 |
| PAW, with the new term | 4.07e-3 | 4.11e-3 | 4.12e-3 |
| norm-conserving AlAs (`alas-raman.in`, 158 plane waves), the control | 1.64e-3 | 4.09e-4 | 1.02e-4 |

In the band count, at step 1e-3 (ultrasoft; PAW the same to two figures): 1.01e-1 at 20 bands,
4.87e-2 at 60, 3.79e-2 at 100, 4.03e-3 at 159 with the term; 1.07e-1, 4.86e-2, 3.92e-2, 5.27e-2
without it. So the term is worth a factor of 13 at completeness, the residue falls off a cliff at
completeness only with it, and it does **not** fall with the step, where the norm-conserving
control falls as the step squared. A floor of 4.1e-3 that the difference cannot see as its own
error is a missing piece, not a convergence, so the refusal stays and the item is **partial**.

**What was built and checked**, each with its own number:

- `augmentation_quadrupole` (`pseudo/augmentation.py`), `quad^{ac}_ij = int Q_ij r_a r_c`,
  `L = 0` and `L = 2` radial moments of `qfuncl` with the angular integral by quadrature on the
  package's own harmonics; against `-d^2 q_ij(b)/db_a db_c` of `augmentation_at_q`: 4.0e-6 at
  `h = 2e-2`, 1.0e-6 at `1e-2` (max element 3.3e-2), both datasets
  (`tests/unit/test_photocurrent_machinery.py::test_the_augmentation_quadrupole_is_the_second_order_of_q_of_b`,
  fast, about 3 s each).
- `VelocityOperator.projector_second` (atom-centred `d^2 beta/dk_a dk_c`, the nested `jvp` with
  the three structure-factor terms put back) and `VelocityOperator.augmentation_second`,
  `L^{ac} = <psi|T^dag d_a d_c T|psi>`; against the second difference in `k'` of the overlap
  primitive at frozen coefficients, eight bands, all nine pairs: 2.34e-7 at `h = 2e-3`, 5.85e-8 at
  `1e-3` (max element 3.1e-2), ratio 4.0, the same on PAW; `K` (the control) 4.6e-8 and 1.2e-8.
- `velocity.generalised_second`: `<Psi|d^2 H_AE/dk_a dk_c|Psi>` with `H_AE = T^{-dag} H T^{-1}`,
  `w = h^{ac} - K^{a dag} h^c - h^c K^a - K^{c dag} v^a - v^a K^c - (L - K^c K^a)^dag E - E (L - K^c K^a)`,
  three of the products sums over the bands held. Exact to 1e-10 against `jax.hessian` on a
  six-state model with an invertible `T(k)` on a fixed space; misses by order one with `L` or
  with `K` and `L` zeroed
  (`::test_the_generalised_second_derivative_is_exact_over_a_complete_basis`, fast).

**What was missing, as found in the code, and where the entry was wrong.** The entry named
`d^2(beta)/dk_a dk_b` of a bare projector. That is part of it: the object the sum rule needs is
the second derivative of the orthonormal states' Hamiltonian, which carries `L^{ac}`, and `L`
needs, besides `d^2 beta`, the **augmentation charge's quadrupole**, which nothing built
(`augmentation_dipole` reads only `L = 1` of `qfuncl`), and the products of `K` with the bare
`dH/dk` and with itself, where `T^{-1}` survives and is removed only by inserting the
completeness of the bands. All three are now built.

**What gives the 4.1e-3 floor, as far as it is measured.** Two things it is not: the
difference's step (flat from 1e-3 to 2.5e-4) and the normalisation of the dense generalised solve
(`psi^dag S psi` to 2.2e-15). What its class is, is measured: the frozen-state derivative of `K`
minus `K^{a dag} K^c`, which is the curvature a frame on the sphere would have, differs from the
PAW-form `L` by 0.16 against `max|L| = 0.031`, and that difference is exactly
`<psi|(d_a T)^dag (d_c T)|psi>` in PAW form minus the same product summed over the 159 bands. So
most of the norm of `(d_c T)|psi>` lies outside the span of the sphere's own eigenstates: **once
`T` moves with `k`, the intermediate space of the sum rule is not the frozen sphere**, which is
why no `nbnd` completes it and why the norm-conserving control, whose `H(k)` maps the sphere into
itself, has no floor. That closed-frame curvature is also not symmetric in `a, c` (1.9e-2), so the
PAW-form connection is not the connection of any frame on the sphere, and using it in place of
`L` gives 4.8e-3 to 5.1e-3, no better. What I have not shown is that this accounts for the whole
4.1e-3 rather than part of it, and that is the open question.

**A trap the first harness hit**, worth keeping: `augmentation_connection` reads the
calculation's own projectors, so a `VelocityOperator(calculation, ..., kcart=k')` mixes `vkb` at
two k-points and the connection at `k'` is wrong at first order. The difference reference built
that way read 0.55 at completeness on both datasets; built on `calculation.at_kcart(k')` it reads
4.1e-3. The shift current's own walk is not affected, since each chunk's operator gets its own
rows' `kcart`.

**The wiring exists and is set aside.** Commit `e22badfc` on this branch has `shift_current`
walking the generalised elements in four stages (tangents, connections, bare second elements,
`L`) with the generalised second derivative built inside each band count's term, PAW's
`ddd_paw` threaded through `shift_current` and `run_shift_current(becsum=...)`, and the guard
narrowed to spinor runs. The final commit restores `defuqumat/response/photocurrent.py` and
`defuqumat/workflows/photocurrent.py` to master except the refusal's message, so the
norm-conserving path is untouched; the machinery in `velocity.py`, `efield.py` and
`pseudo/augmentation.py` stays, tested, and is reached by nothing in production.
