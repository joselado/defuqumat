# I6. Shift current x `nspin = 2`

Status: done. The refusal at `defuqumat/response/photocurrent.py:shift_current` is lifted for
a collinear `nspin = 2` insulator with fixed occupations, and a new sibling refusal covers the
one regime the fix does not, a fixed filling that stops inside a degenerate shell.

## The numbers

Both measured on D22, CPU, one core, through the slot queue, worktree `/l/ladovj1/w-inc-I6-v2`
at commit `e41fedd` (one run each, the identities are exact so there is no median to take).

- **Unpolarized crystal, `nspin = 2` against `nspin = 1`** (the entry's check): AlAs
  (`alas-shg.in` at `ecutwfc = 10`, whole 2x2x2 unshifted mesh, `tot_magnetization = 0`), nine
  bands (a clean cut, gap to the tenth 4.9e-3 Ry, P139), `nw = 4` up to 0.6 Ry, broadening
  0.01 Ry: `max|sigma|` = 5.66e-5 A/V^2 and the two runs differ by **6.4e-10 of it**, asserted
  below 1e-7. This is the same level as SHG's 1.4e-9 on the same cell (P139), and is the two
  SCFs' convergence, not the assembly.
  Test: `tests/regression/test_spin_channels.py::test_the_shift_current_of_two_unpolarized_channels_is_the_scalar_run`.
- **A magnet against itself written as spinors** (what the entry calls "on a magnet"): AlAs at
  6 Ry with `tot_magnetization = 2` (five electrons up, three down), whole 2x2x2 mesh, twelve
  fixed-density bands a channel, made into spinors `(u, 0)` and `(0, u)` with the noncollinear
  potential built from `(n, 0, 0, m)`, which is P140's construction: `max|sigma|` = 1.08e-5 A/V^2
  and the collinear channel sum against the spinor route differ by **1.6e-16 of it**, asserted
  below 1e-9. The spinor route has one Hamiltonian on `2 npwx` and no channel axis, so it shares
  the assembly and nothing of the fold.
  Test: `tests/regression/test_spin_channels.py::test_the_shift_current_of_a_collinear_magnet_written_as_spinors_is_itself`.

Both tests are `slow` (they run two SCFs each), 24 s together on D22 with a warm kernel cache,
76 s cold. `tests/regression/test_photocurrent.py`, `tests/unit/test_photocurrent_machinery.py`,
`tests/unit/test_second_order_warnings.py` and `tests/unit/test_walked_sum_over_states.py`:
42 passed, no skips, on `afa007a` (worktree `/l/ladovj1/w-inc-I6`), which differs from
`e41fedd` only by a print in a test.

The nonmagnetic identity alone could not have read differently under a channel mispairing,
since its two channels are the same arrays (channel 0 counted twice gives the `nspin = 1`
answer exactly). That is why the magnet is there; the entry's wording "the per-channel sum
against SHG's own channel sum" does not name a comparison that exists, since SHG and the shift
current are different tensors, and P140's spinor construction is what P139 used for SHG's
channel sum on a magnet, so it is taken as the intended check.

## What was missing

The entry's "the loop over two channels whose currents add" is right in kind, but there was no
loop to write: `VelocityOperator` already builds one Hamiltonian per channel and returns
`(3, nspin, chunk, nb, nb)` and `(3, 3, nspin, chunk, nb, nb)`. The assembly threw the second
channel away in four places: `first_of` and `second_of` sliced `[:, 0]` and `[:, :, 0]`, the
occupations were taken as `wg[0]`, and the energies as `eigenvalues[0]`. The fix keeps the
channel axis and folds it into the k axis of the contraction with `jnp.tile` of the weights,
exactly as `shg.second_harmonic` does (`shg.py`, the `contraction` stage). The intermediate sum
of the generalised derivative then runs over one channel's bands, which is right, since the
channels do not couple at a frozen collinear potential.

**What lifting it exposed.** At `nspin = 1` with fixed occupations the filling cannot stop
inside a shell of an insulator; at `nspin = 2` a `tot_magnetization` can (three electrons in a
channel whose valence top is a triplet at Gamma). Every pair carries `f_n - f_m`, so the tensor
would then depend on the eigensolver's rotation inside the shell. This is refused by name in
`photocurrent._require_a_gap_at_the_filling` (gap across each channel's cut below
`EMPTY_ETHR_FLOOR = 1e-5` Ry, the threshold `workflows/realtime._solved_states` uses), checked at
`nspin = 2` only. Tests: `tests/unit/test_photocurrent_machinery.py::test_a_filling_that_cuts_a_shell_is_refused`
and its control `::test_a_filling_at_a_gap_is_not_refused`. Note that `get_shg` and
`get_optical_conductivity` at `nspin = 2` do not have this refusal (I did not touch them); that
is a gap in those two, not in this item.

The step-1 test that fired the old guard (`tests/unit/test_shift_current_spin_guard.py`,
commit `24d14e3`, passed locally, no SCF) was removed in the fix commit, since the guard no
longer exists.

## User guide

Page `docs/guide/nonlinear.md`, the "Limits" note, first paragraph. Replace

> It also gives an error for a spin spiral, DFT+U, `nspin = 2`, a symmetry-reduced k-set and a metal, where the partly filled bands give transitions of zero energy and every denominator of the sum diverges. A spinor run works.

with

> It also gives an error for a spin spiral, DFT+U, a symmetry-reduced k-set and a metal, where the partly filled bands give transitions of zero energy and every denominator of the sum diverges. A spinor run works, and so does a collinear spin-polarized run (`nspin = 2`), where the two channels add. With `nspin = 2` it gives an error when `tot_magnetization` makes a channel stop inside a degenerate level, because the result then depends on an arbitrary choice inside the level.

## README

The feature-table row "**Shift current**" (`calc.get_shift_current()`, QE cell `¹³`, Elk cell
empty) does not carry the spin regime, so no cell changes. Neither QE nor Elk has the
quantity in this regime (`pw.x` has no photocurrent; Elk unread, as the entry says).
