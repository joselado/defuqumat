So the PAW deficit falls the way the other two datasets' do, about a quarter left per step of two
divisions (ratios 0.24, 0.23, 0.20), and at `8 8 8` the dataset moves it by less than a tenth of a
point. The k-mesh is the convergence parameter; at `12 12 12` the response is 1.4e-05 relative
from `pw.x`.

**What the reference is worth**, measured rather than assumed (all `pw.x`, 30/240 Ry unless said):

- cutoff: 0.693273 at 25 Ry, 0.693240 at 30 (5e-05 relative). A 35 Ry `pw.x` run
  (`review/incompat/I89/pwx35.log`) was still queued for a slot when this was written and was not
  read; nothing here depends on it. The response's `12 12 12` rung
  scatters by 0.05 per cent across 25, 30, 35 Ry (0.693264, 0.693625, 0.693404).
- string mesh: 21 strings over 12x12 reads **0.693927** against 0.693240 at 15 over 8x8, +0.10 per
  cent. This is the largest error in the reference and it was not converged further (stop rule).
- ground state on `8 8 8` with half the step (`E[1,2] = 0.0025`): 0.693321, +0.012 per cent.
- this package's own Berry-phase finite difference on the same strings (`piezo_measure.py paw`):
  **0.693263** at 25 Ry and 0.693230 at 30, 1.4e-05 from `pw.x`, the per-`gdir` phases equal to six
  decimals (0.247098 and 0.253922). And its PAW Berry phase against `pw.x` string by string on the
  unstrained cell at `sic-berry.in`'s settings (`reference.out.alas-paw-berry`): strings to
  **3.2e-06** (`pw.x` prints five decimals), electronic phase 2.4e-06, `P` **1.0e-07** e/Omega bohr,
  energy 3e-09 Ry.

**The A/B the task asks for ("the term switched off").** No term was missing, so the A/B is the one
the sibling refusal defines: `method='zstar_eu'` with `require_a_norm_conserving_transcription`
patched out in the harness, which has everything except the PAW one-centre cross term with the
field's `dbecsum`. It reads **5.3e-04 C/m^2 above autodiff at every mesh from 8 to 12** (7.5e-04 at
4; 5.34e-04 at 30 Ry and 5.27e-04 at 35 Ry on `12 12 12`), against 1e-07 for the same pair on
ultrasoft. That is 0.08 per cent, **below the 0.10 per cent the reference's string mesh is worth**,
so the Berry comparison does not resolve this one term: at 25 Ry the `12 12 12` rung happens to land
on `pw.x` with it and 0.075 per cent off without it, but the string error is larger than that
difference. What the comparison does check is the PAW tensor as a whole, the dataset with the mesh,
and that it would also have passed at `8 8 8` with the cross term absent (0.94 against 0.86 per
cent) is said here and in the docstrings. The term is carried because the taped route
differentiates the energy, whose one-centre part is a function of `becsum`.
