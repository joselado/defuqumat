# I36. Ultracell x a reference converged under a magnetic field or constrained moment

Status: partial. A collinear unit cell converged under a fixed applied field (`B_field`, a
`LOCAL_MAGNETIC_FIELDS` card) runs on the ultracell and converges to the supercell under the
same field. Only a uniform `B_field` was measured; a `LOCAL_MAGNETIC_FIELDS` card is the same
fixed potential through the same code path, and is not measured on its own. A penalty constraint, a feedback scheme (`'fsm'`, `'atomic fsm'`, `'atomic fsm
direction'`), `reducebf` below 1, and a spinor reference under any field refuse by name; the
feedback scheme and `reducebf` refusals are new in this unit.

## What was there before this unit

`ec98a1f` of the previous batch carried the reference's final field into every frozen solve
and the reference potential, subtracted it from `dV` and paired its Zeeman energy into
`deband`, and lifted the refusal for an applied field **and for the feedback schemes and
`reducebf`**. Its check never closed: `review/incompat/ultracell/r5-field.log` has the null
exact and the supercell stalled (3.7e-6 Ry after 300 iterations at beta 0.3). So master
carried a lifted refusal with no number. The entry's missing term ("the final field carried
into `dV`") is what `ec98a1f` wrote.

## The number

`tests/regression/test_ultracell_regimes.py::test_a_field_converged_reference_converges_to_the_supercell_under_it`,
the partly polarized hydrogen lattice (`H.pz-vbc.UPF`, `nspin = 2`, Gaussian 0.02 Ry) under
`B_field(3) = 0.02`, a 2x1x1 ultracell on `(2, 2, 2)` with a `0.05 cos` scalar modulation,
against the explicit 2x1x1 supercell under the same field and modulation. D22, commit 126d6ec
(the measured path is unchanged in 18cc163), single runs:

- The tiled null (`r1-field.log`): `dV` 2.2e-16, one iteration, `E + E_field` -1.46e-12 Ry from
  the unit cell's, `E_field` -0.0126570876 against -0.0126571270 Ry. This is the identity that
  says the field is carried: it cancels out of `dV` and stays inside every eigenvalue.
- The supercell under the field converges with `mixing_mode = 'adaptive'` in 39 iterations to
  4.6e-12 Ry; Anderson stalls (3.7e-6 Ry at 300, beta 0.3) and `'robust'` did not close in
  300 (`r1-field.log`).
- The ultracell loop stalls under Kerker-Anderson at every rung (dr2 2.9e-5, 3.7e-7, 4.0e-7 Ry
  after 800 iterations at beta 0.2, `r3-field.log`) and converges with `mixing_mode =
  'adaptive', kerker = False` in 70 to 71. The soft direction is the size of the moment: the
  mean moment density is 0.00380 in the tiled unit cell and 0.00451 in both the supercell and
  the converged ultracell, an 18 per cent change under the modulation.

`r3-field.log`, the ladder against the supercell (charge and moment relative to their largest
Fourier components; free energy `E + E_field` per cell above the supercell's):

| nbnd | charge | moment | free energy above the supercell |
|---|---|---|---|
| 8 | 6.504e-4 | 8.982e-4 | +9.121e-6 Ry |
| 16 | 2.025e-4 | 2.853e-4 | +2.189e-6 Ry |
| 24 | 1.447e-4 | 1.229e-4 | +8.838e-7 Ry |

All three fall with `nbnd`, both densities are below 5e-4 at 24, and the free energy is above
the supercell's at every rung, which is the variational bound for the functional the loop
minimises under a fixed field. The test now mixes both sides adaptively (as the harness did)
and asserts all three trends and the bound. It was not rerun as the committed test body: that
would have been the fourth D22 run of this check, past the stop rule; the harness
(`review/incompat/ultracell2/h36c.py`) ran the same computation with the same settings.

## What was missing, and what was wrong

The term was there. What was wrong was the scope of the lift: a feedback scheme's converged
field is held fixed on every copy, which makes the ultracell the supercell **under that field**,
while the supercell under the same scheme moves its field until the modulated cell is on its
target. On the measured cell the modulation moves the moment by 18 per cent, so these are
different states at first order, not a convergence detail. `reducebf` is the same argument:
the supercell's field decays along its own iterations and ends at its own scale. Both now
refuse, with that argument in the message (`defuqumat/ultracell/driver.py:run_ultracell`). A
penalty and a spinor reference under a field keep their refusals. Whether the user wants the
ultracell to run a feedback scheme on the box itself (re-adjusting the field to the
ultracell's own moment, which is what the supercell does) is a design question left open.

## User guide

`docs/guide/ultracell.md`, the Limits note. The sentences

> A ground state converged under a magnetic field or a constrained moment gives
> an error, because its eigenvalues carry the field the SCF ended with while the
> long cell rebuilds its potential from the density alone. For a field, you
> converge the unit cell without it and give it with `magnetic_field` in the
> long cell instead.

become

> A collinear ground state converged under a fixed magnetic field (`B_field` or
> a `LOCAL_MAGNETIC_FIELDS` card) runs. The code keeps the same field on every
> copy of the unit cell, so the result is the supercell under the same field.
> A constrained moment gives an error. This covers the penalty schemes, the
> fixed-spin-moment schemes `'fsm'`, `'atomic fsm'` and `'atomic fsm
> direction'`, and `reducebf` below 1. In a supercell these schemes change the
> field when the modulation changes the moment, and the long cell would keep the
> field of the unit cell. A noncollinear ground state converged under a field
> also gives an error. For these cases, you converge the unit cell without the
> field and give it with `magnetic_field` in the long cell instead.
