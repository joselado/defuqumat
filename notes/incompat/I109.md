# I109. Source-free exchange-correlation field x potential-only meta-GGA: done (refusal lifted; projection exact on the sphere, box residue falls with `ecutrho`, BJ06 converges under `'grad_m'`)

**First, the guard fired.** The entry said "Test: none". A test calling
`refuse_source_free(SimpleNamespace(noncolin=True, spiral_q=None), get_functional("TB09"))` raised
the "potential-only meta-GGA" `NotImplementedError` before anything changed (run locally,
2026-10-19). It is now `test_the_doors_now_open_and_the_ones_that_stay_shut`, which asserts the
opposite for TB09 and BJ06 and that the three sibling refusals still fire.

**The number.** `div B_xc = 0` after the projection, with the meta potential inside the projected
field.

*Hand-built texture* (`tests/unit/test_source_free_field.py`, two Gaussian moments along z and x,
`tau` Thomas-Fermi-like, 10 bohr cube): `int |div B|^2` on the sphere at 40 Ry, unprojected against
projected, is 0.209 against 1e-31 (TB09, `'kubler'`) and 0.141 against 1e-31 (TB09, `'grad_m'`); the
scalar part, `etxc` and Tran-Blaha's `c` (`meta_c`) are bit-identical with the projection on and off.
The box residue (the approximation, components outside the dense sphere, which the projection does
not touch), projected over unprojected:

The run that converges under both corrections stacked, BJ06 with `'grad_m'`, is source-free on the
sphere to 3e-31 of its unprojected divergence, its site moments are 0.736, 0.665, 0.651, 0.723 mu_B,
and its forces are refused (by the meta-GGA's message, which fires before the `nosource` one).
TB09 with its cell-average `c` does not converge on a chain in vacuum with or without `nosource`
(the average of `|grad rho|/rho` is dominated by the vacuum), so it says nothing about the
combination; BJ06's fixed `c = 1` is why it was added. Under `'kubler'` and `nosource` BJ06 does not
converge either, which is the same branch that stalled under PBE (`I108.md`); `'kubler'` is the
`nosource` default (the question in `I108.md`). For a meta-GGA that question is not the same as
for PBE: the combination was refused until now, so a `'kubler'` default under `nosource` preserves
no existing run, and it is the branch that did not converge on the one measured cell (BJ06
`'kubler'` oscillating at 1e-1 against `'grad_m'` converged in 117). The conservative argument for
keeping `'kubler'` does not apply here and the choice is open; the code keeps `'kubler'` until the
user decides.

**What was missing, and where the entry was wrong.** Nothing. The entry's "Missing: unread
(message: both are potentials with no energy functional behind them)" was the refusal's argument,
and it is a caution rather than a missing term: `nosource` alone is already a potential with no
energy functional behind it and is allowed. A run with both is a fixed point of a potential with
two corrections, as a run with either is. In `v_of_rho` the meta potential is added to `v_xc`
before the projection on both paths (TB09 is not `is_gradient`, so it reaches the final
`if source_free:`; a meta-GGA that also had a gradient part would reach the one inside the
`nspin == 4` gradient block, also after `v_meta`). The consumers that need a stationary point refuse
each correction by its own name: `forces/energy.py:reject_potential_only` (meta first, then
`nosource`), the phonon at `q` (`response/phononq.py`, `source_free`), the transverse susceptibility
(`tddft/spinchi0.py`, `source_free`), and the torque (`scf/spin_torque.py`, which refuses a meta-GGA
because its potential needs `tau`, so no torque number exists for this item). The `is_meta` branch
of `refuse_source_free` is removed and replaced by a comment saying why.
