TB09 with its cell-average `c` does not converge on a chain in vacuum with or without `nosource`
(the average of `|grad rho|/rho` is dominated by the vacuum), so it says nothing about the
combination; BJ06's fixed `c = 1` is why it was added. Under `'kubler'` and `nosource` BJ06 did not
converge on this one cell (oscillating at 1e-2 to 1e-1 after 300); that does not generalize, since on
the verifier's chain it converged in 160 steps and on Ni in 29. `'kubler'` is the slow branch under
PBE as well (`I108.md`), and it is the `nosource` default (the question in `I108.md`). For a meta-GGA that question is not the same as
for PBE: the combination was refused until now, so a `'kubler'` default under `nosource` preserves
no existing run, and on the chains it is the slower branch (BJ06 `'kubler'` oscillating at 1e-1 after 300 on the
implementer's chain against `'grad_m'` in 117, and 160 against 46 on the verifier's), while on Ni the
two are level (TB09, 22 steps each). The conservative argument for
keeping `'kubler'` does not apply here and the choice is open; the code keeps `'kubler'` until the
user decides.

**What was missing, and where the entry was wrong.** Nothing. The entry's "Missing: unread
(message: both are potentials with no energy functional behind them)" was the refusal's argument,
and it is a caution rather than a missing term: `nosource` alone is already a potential with no
energy functional behind it and is allowed. A run with both is a fixed point of a potential with
two corrections, as a run with either is. In `v_of_rho` the meta potential is added to `v_xc`
before the projection on both paths (TB09 is not `is_gradient`, so it reaches the final
`if source_free:`; a meta-GGA that also had a gradient part would reach the one inside the
`nspin == 4` gradient block, also after `v_meta`). The consumers that need a stationary point refuse
each correction by its own name: `forces/energy.py:reject_potential_only` (meta first, then
`nosource`), the phonon at `q` (`response/phononq.py`, `source_free`), the transverse susceptibility
(`tddft/spinchi0.py`, `source_free`), and the torque (`scf/spin_torque.py`, which refuses a meta-GGA
because its potential needs `tau`, so no torque number exists for this item). The `is_meta` branch
of `refuse_source_free` is removed and replaced by a comment saying why.
