# I27. Meta-GGA x spin spiral: done (norm-conserving, `noncollinear_gga = 'kubler'`)

**Measured.** The hydrogen chain at `q = b3/4` under Tran-Blaha (`input_dft =
'tb09'`, `noncollinear_gga = 'kubler'`), stretched to 5.2 bohr, against the
90-degree four-cell supercell diagonalised at the spiral's density **and**
`tau` unfolded onto it, with the spiral re-solved at the same `(rho, tau)`:
the lowest twelve levels of the supercell's one k-point equal the spiral's
four k-points together to **4.8e-14 Ry** (sixteen: 8.6e-12). Dropping the
transverse `tau` moves them by **0.54 Ry**, so the per-component `tau` is what
is being checked. One D22 run (`review/incompat/spiral/v3-meta.log`). Test:
`tests/regression/test_spiral_supercell.py::test_the_meta_gga_spiral_is_the_supercell`
(slow).

Two things learned on the way. At the inputs' 5.0 bohr spacing the unit cell's
dense grid is 15 along the chain and the supercell's 64, not 60, which put
**4.2e-3 Ry** into the same comparison (first run); the test stretches the chain
to 5.2 bohr, where the grids are 18 and 72. And the tb09 spiral SCF on this cell
does not converge in 100 iterations; the identity does not need it, since both
cells are diagonalised at one input.

**What was missing.** The entry's term, a lattice-periodic `tau` from two
components on two spheres, was right. `spinor_band_kinetic_density` now takes
`kplusg` as `(2, npwx, 3)` beside the spiral's `(2, npwx)` FFT index and
differentiates each component with its own `k +- q/2 + G`; the cross term then
carries the same `e^{-i q.r}` as the transverse magnetization and is stored in
the same rotating frame, and `Calculation.kinetic_energy_density` stacks the
doubled `kplusg` into that layout. Under `'kubler'` the resolution onto the
local axis is pointwise and frame independent.

**Still refused** (`scf/driver.py:Calculation._require_meta_supported`):
the default `'grad_m'` (its meta-GGA branch does not carry the laboratory
gradient `i (G - q)` of the transverse pair; `scf/potential.py` keeps its own
guard), and PAW (one-centre `tau` on a spiral, no check).

**User guide**, `docs/guide/spirals.md`, Limits: replace "A spiral with a
meta-GGA gives an error under either `noncollinear_gga`, because the two spinor
components live on different spheres and their kinetic energy density is not
periodic." with "A spiral with a meta-GGA works on a norm-conserving dataset
with `noncollinear_gga = 'kubler'`, and each spinor component gives its kinetic
energy density on its own sphere. Under `'grad_m'`, the default, and on a PAW
dataset it gives an error."
