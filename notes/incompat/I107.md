# I107. Source-free exchange-correlation field x PAW: partial (nothing lifted; sized as part of a phase, not physics; refusal kept)

**No number.** The refusal stays, and nothing was measured against a reference: the work the
lift needs is past this unit's stop rule (about 90 minutes), and a lifted refusal without a
number is not acceptable. What this record holds is the sizing as read from the code, which
is sharper than the entry's. Labelled partial as I60 was (the PAW one-centre kernel sized, not
written): the refusal is about size, not physics, so it is not blocked in the I29 sense.

**What is missing, as found in the code.** The entry's "one Poisson equation for `div B`
across the smooth and the one-centre parts" is right about the shape and understates what is
absent. `nosource` is `P = 1 - grad (lap)^-1 div`, which is nonlocal, and a PAW field is the
smooth field on the grid plus, inside every sphere, the all-electron minus the pseudo
one-centre field. `P` of the sum is not the sum of `P` applied to the smooth part alone, so
`project_source_free` on the grid (`defuqumat/scf/sourcefree.py`) is a projection of nothing
in particular for a PAW run. Three pieces are missing, and one is not:

- **The divergence of the sphere field.** The one-centre potential is held as `(4, nlm, mesh)`
  multipoles per atom (`paw/onecenter.py:_exchange_correlation`); the divergence of an
  lm-expanded *vector* field couples `l` to `l +- 1`, so it needs the vector gradient on the
  angular quadrature (the scalar gradient exists, `onecenter_gradient_correction`) and one
  more `l` than `lmax_rho`.
- **The compensation term.** `div (B^1 - B~^1)` has, in general, nonzero multipoles beyond
  the monopole, so its Poisson solution leaks out of the sphere as multipole tails. They
  have to be carried to the grid as a compensation term with the same multipoles, exactly
  the device `paw/hartree.py` uses for the density, and subtracted on the sphere.
- **The one Poisson solve over both**, smooth plus compensation on the grid, residual with
  zero multipoles on the radial mesh.
- **Not missing: the step after.** `ddd_paw` is a contraction of whatever one-centre
  potential it is handed (`onecenter_species`, "the same tensor contracted against the
  potential"), and Tran-Blaha's one-centre potential, which is also not the derivative of
  an energy, already goes through it. So a projected sphere field would reach the
  Hamiltonian without new machinery.

That is `AUGMENTATION-NEXT.md` §1f's "part of a phase", confirmed. The check, once built:
`div B = 0` of the combined field on a radial-plus-grid quadrature, the
norm-conserving limit (a PAW dataset whose one-centre fields cancel), and a T4 against Elk's
`nosource` (Elk on D22 is in `/l/ladovj1/review/harmonics-elk/bin/`, LAPW, so only a moment
or a torque compares, not an energy).
