# I115. Ultracell transport, `exit_region = 'volume'` x ultrasoft or PAW

**Status: done, for the spinless, the collinear (`nspin = 2`) and the spinor
(scalar-relativistic) ultracell on the ultrasoft and the PAW silicon datasets.** The
refusal `_refuse_an_augmented_volume_diagnostic` is gone. **Kept by name: `lspinorb`**
(spin-orbit coupling on a fully-relativistic dataset) with `exit_region = 'volume'` on an
augmented dataset, raised in `_ultracell_overlap_operator`: the ultracell runs spin-orbit
coupling, but the spin-off-diagonal blocks of `qq_so` vanish on every dataset measured
here, so the `abij` contraction with them is held only by the random-matrix unit test
against the unit cell's block form, not by a physics identity. Not measured, so no
discrepancy is quoted; `exit_region = 'plane'` still runs there.
The guards that stay are the ones that were already there and are not this item's: a tip
or a plane inside an augmentation sphere (still refused with `exit_region = 'volume'`, now
asserted in the slow test), a stacked ultracell and a k-set the transmission cannot sum.

## What was missing, and what was written

With the exit region widened to the whole ultracell, the transmission's Gram matrix is
`<Psi|S|Psi>` over the `N Omega` box, and `S` in an ultracell state's plane-wave
representation was not written: the state spans `N` folded spheres, each with its own
projectors. It is now `defuqumat/ultracell/augmentation.py:ultracell_overlap`,

    S = 1 + sum_{a,R} sum_ij q_ij |beta^{a,R}_i><beta^{a,R}_j|,

with `R` over the `N` unit-cell translations, the projector of copy `R` on the plane wave
`k0 + Q + G` being the unit cell's `vkb(k0 + Q)` times `e^{-i(k0+Q).R} / sqrt(N)`. The
copy sum is written out, so that the cross-`Q` term vanishing (`sum_R e^{i(Q'-Q).R} =
N delta_QQ'`) is computed rather than assumed. `workflows/ultracell.py:_ultracell_overlap_operator`
builds the folded-k `Calculation` for the projectors (the result does not carry it), checks
that its folded box indices are exactly the states' (`_box_indices` against
`states.box_index`, a `RuntimeError` otherwise, since a mispaired sphere would read as a
few-per-cent Gram error indistinguishable from the missing term), and hands
`apply_s(psi, ik0)` to `TransportGeometry.apply_s`. The plane and every norm-conserving
case keep `apply_s = None`. Cost: 0.5 to 0.6 s for the whole volume transmission on the
sheet, the projectors of one `k0` (`N npwx nkb` complex) at a time.

## The number

Cell: `tests/data/qe/ultracell-sheet-paw.in` (Si sheet in vacuum, `ecutwfc = 20`,
`ecutrho = 4 ecutwfc`), two-cell ultracell `(2, 1, 1)` at `kgrid = (2, 4, 1)`, 12 bands,
under a modulation `0.05 cos(2 pi x / 2)` so that the states mix `Q` and a cross-`Q`
augmentation term would have something to carry. Two independent checks, each against an
identity that does not use `S`:

1. **The Gram matrix `C^dagger S C` at every `k0` against the identity.** The identity
   holds only if the frozen unit-cell states are `S`-orthonormal at each `k0 + Q`, the
   envelope vectors are unitary, the `(Q, p)` reconstruction is right and the cross-`Q`
   copy sum vanishes; none of the four is assumed by the operator.
2. **The `exit_region = 'volume'` transmission against `run_ultracell_stm`'s image** at
   the same tip plane (height 0.85, `(12, 4)` points, `E_F`, width 0.05), which is the
   Tersoff-Hamann limit the norm-conserving diagnostic is checked against
   (`test_ultracell_stm.py`), relative to the image maximum (4.18e-3).

"S off" is the same run with `_ultracell_overlap_operator` patched to return `None`, which
is the naive lift: the plain `sum_G c* c`.

The spinor sheet is magnetic (`starting_magnetization = 0.5` along x), so that the spinor
ultracell is not refused as a closed Kramers set; its dataset is scalar-relativistic, so
`qq_so` is spin-diagonal there. **The `nspin = 2` rows are a check of the two-block
indexing, not of a moment**: the collinear sheet converges essentially unpolarized (`E_F`
moves by 7e-7 Ry from the spinless run, and the S-off columns agree to three digits), so
what they show is that both spin blocks reach `apply_s` with the right `k0` and the
spin-independent projectors of the folded set.

**Read aloud.** With `S` the whole-cell diagnostic is the image to 3e-14 to 1e-13 on every
regime and both datasets, and the Gram matrix is the identity to 3e-15 to 8e-14; without
it the diagonal of the Gram matrix is short by 0.28 (the augmentation charge of the
silicon dataset inside a 12-band set) and the image misses by 2.8e-2. So the term carries
a factor of 1e11 to 1e12 in the check. **The convergence parameter does not move the
discrepancy off round-off**: at `states_conv_thr = 1e-8` and at 1e-11 (1e-10 for the
spinor) both checks sit at 1e-14 to 1e-13. The Gram error with `S` is a few 1e-15 at 1e-8 and a
few 1e-14 at the tighter threshold in every regime; that is more Davidson steps
accumulating more round-off, both at the floor, not the identity degrading, since the
image error moves the other way (1e-13 to 3e-14). This is expected rather than suspicious,
because both identities are exact for any converged frozen basis (the envelope solve is a
dense Hermitian eigenproblem in an `S`-orthonormal basis, whose unitarity does not depend
on how tight the frozen states are), so the threshold only reshuffles round-off; and the
S-off column, unchanged to three digits across thresholds, shows the term is not a
convergence effect either.
