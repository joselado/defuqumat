# I4. Phonon of a metal at q = 0 through the q != 0 route

Status: done for `q = 0` on the whole (not k-chunked) route; still refused at a nonzero
reciprocal lattice vector `q = G`, on the k-chunked route at `q = 0`, and for the
electron-phonon coupling at any `q = G`, `0` included.

## What was missing

The entry's "ef_shift" is right but is one of three terms of `dfpt_kernels.f90` under
`lmetq0`, all now in `phononq.screening_loop_at_q`:

1. `ef_shift` (`:359-365`): each mode's response density takes `def ldos` before it is
   screened, `def = -int drho / N(ef)`, complex as `efermi_shift.f90:10` declares it. `ldos`
   is the two-sphere solver's own `local_density_of_states`, over the rows cut at
   `nbnd_occ`, which is `localdos.f90:122`'s range.
2. `dvscfp - def` (`:542-551`) on each mode's `dV_scf`. Nothing in the dynamical matrix reads
   it; it is what `elph` would read, and since `elph` refuses `q = 0` the term is transcribed
   arithmetic and is checked by no number here.
3. `ef_shift_wfc` on `dpsi`, the term the assembly (`response_force_constants`) consumes.
   It adds `psi_k` to `dpsi` index by index, which is right only where the `k + q` sphere is
   the `k` sphere.

Item 3 is why the lift stops at `q = 0`: at `q = G != 0` the perturbation is lattice periodic
and the level moves too (`ph.x` never meets it, its `lgamma` is `xq = 0` only), but `dpsi`
lives on the literal `k + G` sphere, and adding `psi_k` to it needs the Miller-index map
between the two. The refusal there now names that term. The k-chunked route
(`StreamedDisplacementsAtQ`) has no `ldos` or `ef_shift_wfc` pass at `q` and refuses a metal
at `q = 0` by name. `electron_phonon_at_q` used to inherit its `q = 0` refusal from the
dynamical matrix; it now has its own guard at every `q = G`, since the coupling at `Gamma`
has no reference here and its acoustic modes have omega = 0.

## User guide

Page `docs/guide/phonons.md`, "Limits" note. Replace

> For a metal they give an error at $\mathbf q = 0$ or at a reciprocal lattice vector,
> because only there the Fermi level moves, and you use `get_phonons()`.

with

> For a metal they work at $\mathbf q = 0$, where the Fermi level moves and the code moves it
> as `ph.x` does. At a reciprocal lattice vector other than zero they give an error, and you
> use $\mathbf q = 0$ instead. On the k-chunked route, which a GPU takes in
> `memory_mode = 'memory'`, a metal at $\mathbf q = 0$ gives an error, and you use
> `get_phonons()`.

The electron-phonon sentence of the same note ("at $\mathbf q = 0$") and the paragraph
above the linewidth figure ("$\Gamma$ itself is missing, because in a metal the phonon at
$\mathbf q = 0$ moves the Fermi level, which this route does not include") are about the
coupling, which is still refused at $\mathbf q = 0$; the second's reason is now wrong and
reads better as: "$\Gamma$ itself is missing, because the coupling at $\mathbf q = 0$ gives an
error: its acoustic modes have zero frequency, and we have no reference for it."
