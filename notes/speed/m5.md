## What the code is

- `defuqumat/pseudo/projectors.py`: `atom_channel_groups(atom_of_channel)` (cached, static) and
  `block_diagonal_form(left, d, right, atom_of_channel)`.
- Atoms are grouped by the number of channels `nh` of their block (not by species label), so two
  species with the same `nh` share one batch. Per group: the columns are a slice and reshape of
  `vkb` to `(npwx, na, nh)` when the group's channels are one contiguous run in atom order, and a
  static gather otherwise (a copy of the same `(npwx, na nh)` size). I read `_build` of the
  projector core: columns are atom-outermost then the species' channels, so they are contiguous
  per atom, but **not** grouped by species in general (an input that alternates species
  interleaves them), which is why the grouping is by `nh` with the gather as the fallback.
- `D` blocks are gathered from the dense (traced) matrix with static indices inside the call
  (`nat nh^2` entries, negligible); gathering "at build time" is not possible because the
  ultrasoft `deeq` is rebuilt every iteration.
- Contraction: `w = einsum("aij,gaj->gai", blocks, right)`, then `sum(real(left * w))` over atoms
  and channels. This keeps the index structure of the dense form (`sum_ij left_i D_ij right_j`);
  the plan's `"gai,aij->gaj"` read literally is the transpose, equal only for a Hermitian D.
  The largest intermediate is `w`, `(npwx, nkb_group)` by construction.
- The four sites moved together: `operator.py` `diagonal`, `overlap_diagonal`, and
  `noncollinear.py` `diagonal`, `overlap_diagonal`. `hubbard/operator.py:91` (the dense
  contraction on `v_ns`) is out of scope and unchanged.
