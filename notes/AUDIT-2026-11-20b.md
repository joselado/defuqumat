# What ten more readers found, second round of 2026-11-20

## What this is

The reading audit of 2026-11-15 (`notes/AUDIT-2026-11-15.md`) read the whole package at
commit `b5b88c5c`, but some subsystems were read only in part. The first round of
2026-11-20 (`notes/AUDIT-2026-11-20.md`) read only the code written since then. This
second round reads, at commit `696cef9f` on branch `audit-fixes-1120`, the parts that the
earlier readers left open: the first third of the SCF driver, the mixing, the hot path of
the Hamiltonian (for speed only), the eigensolvers, PAW and the augmentation, the static
response at Gamma, forces and stress, the noncollinear exchange-correlation with the
symmetrisation of the magnetization, and the parsing of the inputs. One more slice,
`fixes-review`, reads the fixes of the first round committed up to `696cef9f`.

The code was cut into 10 slices, one reader each, all on Opus 5.5. Each reader looked for
bugs and for optimizations, quoting the code path and naming an input that reaches it
without being refused upstream. There was no skeptic stage, so no finding here has been
tried by a second agent, and the severity and the confidence are the reader's own.

Nothing was run, by the user's instruction. The 1084 shell commands in the readers'
transcripts were searched afterwards for `python`, `pytest`, `ssh` and `tools/`. The four
that matched are `cat` and `grep` over `tools/` and the test-suite, and none executes
code. So every entry is a claim about what the source says, not a measurement.

The line numbers are those of `696cef9f`. If a line no longer holds what an entry
describes, grep for the symbol.

### 1. `defuqumat/forces/spiral.py:159`: dE/dq of a norm-conserving DFT+U spin spiral silently leaves out the Hubbard energy

*bug, severity high, confidence high, slice `forces-stress`.*

Since I28, a norm-conserving spiral with a Hubbard U runs an SCF under nosym. Its wfcU is built on both spheres, k+q/2 and k-q/2 (driver.py _build_hubbard_projectors, the spiral branch). E_U[ns] is part of the total energy that the SCF minimised. ns is measured through projectors on the shifted spheres, so E_U depends on q at frozen states. spiral_energy (lines 159-221), q_dependent_energy, q_independent_energy and q_global_energy contain no hubbard_energy term. The frozen branch of at_spiral_q (driver.py 4874-4958) also does not rebuild wfcU at the traced q. _require_a_differentiable_spiral (spiral.py 594-638) refuses a field, band precision and symmetry, but not is_hubbard. The force and the stress refuse a DFT+U spiral in forces/energy.py reject_spinor_spiral, and notes/incompat/I28.md lists only those two as still refused. The dE/dq route never calls that function. The result has two silent effects. SpiralGradient.gradient lacks dE_U/dq. SpiralGradient.total_energy differs from the SCF total by E_U, and no caller compares the two (workflows/spiral.py has no check). relax_spiral_q then gives BFGS the SCF total, which includes E_U, together with a gradient that lacks it. The line search and the stopping test therefore act on inconsistent data, and the run converges to a q where the U-free partial gradient vanishes.

**Reached by.** notes/incompat/I28.md's NiI chain spiral (nii-chain-spiral.in, nosym, norm-conserving) with a HUBBARD card (U 4 eV on Ni-3d), then Calculator.get_spiral_relaxation(), or get_spiral_scan(..., gradients=True), or compute_spiral_gradient(calculation, result) directly.

**Suggested fix.** Either refuse is_hubbard in _require_a_differentiable_spiral, pointing at I28, or add hubbard_energy(occupation_matrix(psi, w)) with wfcU rebuilt at the traced kcart. That means a separable ns per chunk, as forces/chunked.py does it, pulled back with dE_U/dns.

**How to confirm.** grep -n 'hubbard' defuqumat/forces/spiral.py   (no hits); sed -n 594,638p defuqumat/forces/spiral.py   (no is_hubbard check); sed -n 30,40p notes/incompat/I28.md   (only force and stress are listed as refused). Numerically, on that cell: compare compute_spiral_gradient(...).total_energy with result.total_energy. They should differ by result.energy_terms['hubbard']. Then compare gradient against a central difference of the SCF E(q) at +-1e-3.

### 2. `defuqumat/scf/driver.py:4999`: at_spiral_q(rebuild_basis=True) rebuilds the spheres but keeps the old wfcU, so a DFT+U spiral scan or q-relaxation uses stale Hubbard projectors

*bug, severity high, confidence high, slice `forces-stress`.*

The rebuilding branch of at_spiral_q (driver.py about 4960-5022) builds new basis_kpoints (k +- q'/2), a new plane-wave basis padded to new widths, a new fft_index, new sticks and a new projector core. It never assigns moved.wfcU. A grep of the wfcU assignments in driver.py finds 3459 (setup), 3919 (at_positions), 4263 (at_strain), 4402 (at_kpoints), 4661 (at_rows) and 4796 (at_kcart), and none between 4806 and 5023. at_kpoints rebuilds wfcU and its comment says why: without it, 'a band-structure run ... would apply the Hubbard potential through the old k-points' projectors, which is silently wrong rather than an error'. A moved spiral keeps wfcU built on the old spheres. Its rows are the old k +- q/2, and its plane-wave axis is the old sphere's Miller list. occupation_matrix (hubbard/occupations.py) contracts that wfcU against the new states along the plane-wave axis. If the new padded npwx differs, this is a shape error at trace time. If it is equal, ns and V_U are built from projectors indexed against the wrong G-vectors, which is silently wrong. run_spiral_scan moves every point through base.at_spiral_q(q, widths=scan_widths), and relax_spiral_q moves through calculation.at_spiral_q(...) after each step (workflows/spiral.py 322 and about 728). Neither refuses a Hubbard U.

**Reached by.** A norm-conserving DFT+U spiral under nosym (I28's NiI chain with U), then Calculator.get_spiral_scan([q1, q2, ...]) or get_spiral_relaxation(). In relax_spiral_q the padded npwx of a nearby q often equals the previous one, which gives the silent case. A scan whose union widths exceed the base sphere gives the crash.

**Suggested fix.** At the end of the rebuilding branch, add `if self.hubbard is not None: moved.wfcU = moved._build_hubbard_projectors()`, as at_kpoints does. Add a test that a DFT+U spiral moved to q' and back reproduces the energy of a fresh Calculation at q'.

**How to confirm.** awk 'NR>=4806 && NR<=5023' defuqumat/scf/driver.py | grep -n wfcU   (no hits); grep -n 'wfcU =' defuqumat/scf/driver.py; sed -n 4396,4403p defuqumat/scf/driver.py (at_kpoints' rebuild and its reason); grep -n 'at_spiral_q' defuqumat/workflows/spiral.py.

### 3. `defuqumat/scf/mixing.py:344`: rotate_moments drops the Anderson history at every turn under the default rho_ddot fit

*bug, severity medium, confidence high, slice `mixing`.*

AndersonMixer.rotate_history (mixing.py:330-349) resets the whole history whenever self._fits is non-empty: 'A metric's fit vectors are in that metric's own layout, which this does not unpack, so a history carrying them is dropped instead.' Since P146 (RHO_DDOT_FIT = True, driver.py:645), run_scf installs mixer.metric for every Anderson run (driver.py:9042-9046). So _fits fills on every mix, and every Route C turn (driver.py:10540 mixer.rotate_history(turn_packed, ...)) is a reset. The call site says the opposite: 'Dropping the history instead was measured and is worse (PAW nickel: dr2 swinging up to 5e-3 after each reset, against 2e-4 turning it)' (driver.py:10535-10539). PLAN.md P122 (line 23208) claims equivariance 'after rotate_history(R) to 1e-12 (the flat fit's Gram matrix is invariant under a common spin rotation)'. Those were measured on the flat fit, before P146. tests/unit/test_rotating_moments.py:61-80 uses a bare mixer with no metric, so it cannot see this. Nothing in run_scf forces the flat fit under rotate_moments; fit_in_rho_ddot is read only at driver.py:8910 and 9042. The reset is not needed. rho_ddot is invariant under a common spin rotation: the charge is untouched, the magnetization weight is flat over the three Cartesian components at nspin_mag = 4, and ns_ddot is a Frobenius norm under rotate_ns's unitary.
