# Implementer p2 (route P, step 2)

The record of this step is the second half of `plans/orbital-field/route-p.md`, "Route P, step 2",
the file the design and the crosscheck stage name: every number there has its D22 log under
`/l/ladovj1/review/mag/p2/`. Branch `mag-p2`, from step 1's tip 84253a62; D22 worktree
`/l/ladovj1/w-mag-p2` (detached at the bundled tip), probes `plans/orbital-field/mag_route_p2.py`
and `plans/orbital-field/mag_tr.py`, the big-job runner `/l/ladovj1/review/mag/p2/big.sh`.

The crosscheck stage reads `plans/orbital-field/route-p-q{1,...,6}-full.npz` (the full Hamiltonian,
GIPAW-dressed projectors, two-grid local term, centred window, the shared zero-field potential).

## For `docs/d22-notes.local.md` (the Record stage)

- A probe outside the worktree runs through `slot.sh` by its absolute path with `.` as the
  subdirectory (`slot.sh /l/ladovj1/w-mag-p2 . LOG /l/ladovj1/review/mag/p2/mag_tr.py`); relative
  data paths then resolve in the worktree.
- `big.sh` takes `big.lock` per job, so a chain of big jobs is a shell loop of `big.sh` calls
  (`/l/ladovj1/review/mag/p2/chain_big.sh`); another agent's big job can slip in between two of them.
- `slot.sh` passes the caller's environment: `MAG_OUT=/l/ladovj1/review/mag/p2` reached the probe.
- The 24-atom diamond SCF at 60 Ry (ndim 16200, 72 k, 52 bands) took 950 s on four cores at a
  load of 4 to 6 and about 3500 s on one slot core at a load of 10 to 16; peak RSS 3.5 to 3.7 GB, so
  it fits the 6 GB slot.
- The worktree `/l/ladovj1/w-mag-p2` is removable once `mag-p2` is merged.
