# Route U step 3, the projected trial basis: what was built and what it measured

Implementer u3, branch `mag-u3`, from master 034bb6f5 (fable's decision on the basis,
`route-u-basis/decision.md`, and `notes/ORBITAL-FIELD-NEXT.md` section 13, which this step
implements). Every number below was measured on D22 in the worktree `/l/ladovj1/w-mag-u3`, one
core through `/l/ladovj1/review/guide/slot.sh` (6 GB cap) or, for the graphene field runs, one
core under `big.lock` with a 14 GB cap (`u3/big.sh`). Logs are in `/l/ladovj1/review/mag/u3/`,
written `L/<log>` below; fable's probe logs are `F/<log>` for `/l/ladovj1/review/mag/fable-basis/`.

## The outcome in one paragraph

**The gate passes through the workflow, line for line with fable's probe; graphene's acceptance is
refused**, at its projectability line first and at its 5 mRy line second, and that refusal is a
measured limit of projected atomic trials on this cell, not a defect found in the code. On the free
Landau problem at 1/10 (c = 5 A, `N = 64`, window (3, 4), `N1 = 16`) the `n = 0` error falls
3.24e-2, 4.35e-3, 1.8e-7 `B_ph` at J = 1, 2, 3, every level of every run is above the exact ladder,
`H` is Hermitian to 3e-12 or better, the chirped `l_B` Gaussian reads 1.7e-7 `B_ph` at window (5, 6),
and the reversed field reads Hermiticity 0.59 to 0.63. On graphene at c = 15 A the atomic trials are
not inside any band window that can be solved: `1 - p` of `p_z` is 4.1e-3 at `N = 32` and still
6.1e-4 at `N = 96`, the 2s and in-plane 2p 1.9e-2 to 4.0e-3, hydrogenic d 0.2 to 0.98 at every
zona, against the guard's 1e-4. Run anyway with the guard off, the levels are above route P's at
every compared level and fall with J (non-strictly, since the mirror keeps J = 8 equal to J = 2
near `E_D`), but `(U - U0) - (P - B0)` is 36.5, 36.5, 14.3 mRy at J = 2, 8, 18 at `q = 6` (@Q10@),
against 5 mRy, and raising `N` from 32 to 48 makes every number worse.

## What is here

- `defuqumat/magnetic/hybrid.py`: `projected_hybrid(coefficients, trials, N)` returns a
  `ProjectedGauge` per `k1` loop, the rotation `A_{mj} = <psi_m|g_j>` `(N1, N, J)` over the lowest
  `N` bands, the projectability `p_j = sum_m |A_{mj}|^2 / |g_j|^2` `(N1, J)` and the smallest
  singular value of the normalized `A`. There is no Loewdin step and no transport gauge, and
  `hybrid_set` takes the rotation unchanged, so the overlap `S` carries the non-orthogonality and
  `solve` orthogonalizes canonically as before. `StripeTrials(J, sigma, chirp)` and
  `stripe_trials` are the free problem's trials, a Hermite-Gaussian along `xi1` on the lowest
  free band's stripe, `G2 = -floor(kappa' + 1/2)`, `G3 = 0`, so the trial is a function of
  `k + G` alone and periodic in `kappa'`; `atomic_trials` evaluates Wannier90 projections and the
  pseudo-atomic orbitals by `wannier/matrices.guiding_on_mesh`, as the Wannier engine does.
- `defuqumat/workflows/landau_ultracell.py`: `trials=` (a `StripeTrials`, `"pao"`, or a list of
  projection lines and projection objects in nested order), `N=` the band window, separate from
  `nbnd` (a projected run refuses `nbnd`, and the result's `nbnd` is `J`), the projectability
  guard (`projectability_tol=1e-4`, `None` records), the defaults of a projected run (window
  (3, 4), `h0 = "box"`, `form = "symmetric"`, and `N1 = 16` whenever a window is in force),
  `kappa2=` for explicit magnetic `kappa2` values, the window's `N1` bound checked before the
  NSCF, and the result fields `N`, `trials`, `projectability`, `projectability_deficit`.
- `defuqumat/calculator.py`: `get_landau_levels(route="ultracell")` forwards `trials` and `N` by
  name and takes them in place of `nbnd`; `N1` follows the workflow's default.
- `tests/unit/test_magnetic_ultracell.py`: eight new tests (nine with the parametrization).
- `plans/orbital-field/mag_route_u3_gate.py` (the gate through the workflow) and
  `mag_route_u3_graphene.py` (the graphene acceptance: `zero`, `field`, `analyse`).

## The acceptance proper: graphene against route P

`plans/orbital-field/mag_route_u3_graphene.py`, the committed cell (c = 15 A, 50 Ry, the shared
potential `graphene-scf.npz`), `N = 32`, window (3, 4), `N1 = 16`, the full Hamiltonian with the

### The projectability, which decides the acceptance before any level does

`L/project.log` (`mag_route_u3_project.py`): one NSCF at 96 bands on the 32 k of the `q = 1` loops,
`1 - p` maximum over k:

| N | band N above `E_D` | `p_z` | 2s, 2px, 2py | d zona 0.5 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 16 | 15.4 eV | 1.2e-2 | 0.12 | 1.00 | 1.00 | 1.00 | 1.00 |
| 32 | 27.7 eV | **4.1e-3** | 1.9e-2 | 0.34 | 0.63 | 0.92 | 0.98 |
| 48 | 37.1 eV | 3.1e-3 | 1.7e-2 | 0.30 | 0.55 | 0.87 | 0.96 |
| 64 | 44.0 eV | 1.8e-3 | 1.5e-2 | 0.29 | 0.47 | 0.83 | 0.95 |
| 96 | 57.7 eV | 6.1e-4 | 4.0e-3 | 0.20 | 0.38 | 0.78 | 0.93 |

No trial passes the guard's 1e-4 at any `N` up to 96. The mechanism is the vacuum ladder fable found
for the free problem on the same cell: an orbital localized in z has `G3` content, and the 15 A cell's
`(2 pi m/c)^2` ladder puts that content in high bands, so the weight above the window falls only as
the orbital's own momentum tail. The gate's stripe trial has `G3 = 0` and no such tail (4e-10). The
decision's "zona goes to 3" for the d goes the wrong way: zona is the decay constant in 1/bohr, so 3
is more compact and has more weight above the window (0.98 against 0.92 at `N = 32`). The guard is a
statement about this cell, and it is not loosened here.

- **`U >= P` at every compared level** (0 of 8 below `P - 1e-8` in every row), as a Rayleigh-Ritz
  bound must read, and the gap falls with J: 25.9, 25.9, 16.5 mRy at its largest.
- **J = 8 equals J = 2 near `E_D`, by the mirror.** 2s, 2px, 2py are even under `z -> -z`, `p_z` and
  the pi Landau levels odd, and C.pbe-hgh's one s projector is even, so the even trials cannot move an
  odd level: every J = 2 level is a J = 8 level to 2.4e-15 Ry, which also checks the slicing of the
  J = 18 blocks. d_xz and d_yz are odd, which is why J = 18 moves them. "Falls monotonically" is
  therefore non-strict: 36.5, 36.5, 14.3 mRy.
- **Raising N makes it worse**, 36.5 to 38.2 mRy at J = 2 and 14.3 to 16.6 at J = 18, while `1 - p`
  improves. That is what a projected basis does when the trial is not the state: `P_N g` keeps more
  of the trial's high-energy weight as `N` grows, so its Rayleigh quotient rises; at fixed J the span
  tends to that of the raw orbitals, not to the pi states. `N` is no knob here, as the decision says,
  and J is the only one.
- **The span error near `E_D` is large.** At zero field, on the 24 folded chemical k, the `p_z` set's
  levels within `E_D +- 2.5` eV are up to 669 meV from the nearest sphere band (mean 177 meV), the
  18-function set's up to 257 meV (`L/analyse-q6b.log`); on the `N1` mesh itself with no window
  (`L/span-bare.log`, the exact Rayleigh-Ritz value of the projected trials at each k) 612 and 238 meV,
  with the window (`L/span-win.log`) 669 and 257 meV, so the window adds about 10 per cent and the
  span the rest. For comparison, the Wannier `p_z` set is within 11.4 meV and the 8-function set
  within 1.8 meV of the sphere on the same folded k (cross.md, item 3): the projected trials are not
  a Wannier basis, because nothing removes the trial's weight on the other bands.
- **The matching.** At every representative the zero pair is where the index assumes (`p_z` set q
  levels below `E_D`, the others 4q, as route P), except that at (0, 0) both members of the pair sit
  below `E_D` in both routes. The counts in the window differ at three of the four representatives,
  U 3 against P 2: U's `n = -1` level sits at -2.33 to -2.35 eV, inside the window, where route P's is
  below -2.5 eV. It is not compared, since it has no partner.
- `U - P` at J = 18, 14 to 16 mRy (0.19 to 0.22 eV), is the size of route P's offset against Peierls
  in cross.md item 3, and the `p_z` set's zero pair sits about where Peierls' does. This is an
  observation, not an attribution: the span error above is of the same size.
- **The secondary line, local only at `q = 6`** (`L/analyse-local.log`): the lowest 4q per
  representative, 96 levels, `U >= P` at all of them, `U - P` 0.030 to 4.46 Ry at J = 8 and 0.016 to
  3.77 Ry at J = 18. The lowest of route P's local-only levels are the collapsed deep pair, 92 eV below
  `E_D`, which a 2s trial does not hold (U puts them 44 to 88 eV below); this is the miss the decision
  said would say nothing about the method.

## Decisions

Fable was asked six questions once the code was written (the step's own subagent, 2026-11-08);
its picks were taken as given.

- `N1` defaults to 16 whenever a window is in force (the window's bound is `ceil(2 outer) - 1 + 2
  outer`, 15 for (3, 4)), 10 without one. The bound is now checked before the NSCF, so a wrong `N1`
  costs nothing.
- On a projected basis a direction of `S` dropped by `drop` is recorded in `rank` and `d_min` and
  never raised: `S` carries the non-orthogonality there, and the gate's J = 3 sits at `d_min` 2.2e-8
  against `drop` 1e-8, a factor 2.2. The free problem's rank refusal stays for the hybrid basis.
  Gate line (iii), every level above `(2n+1) B_ph`, is the check that replaces it.
- The chirp of `StripeTrials` is a function of `p/q` only, never of `scale`, so `scale = -1` is the
  field reversed against a fixed basis. Fable's probe let the chirp follow `scale`; the reversed
  field read 0.59 to 0.62 there and reads 0.59 to 0.63 here, so the choice does not move the control.
  A chirp with `J > 1` is refused by name (the table has J = 1 only).
- The pass quantity of the graphene line is `(U - U0) - (P - B0)`, with `U - P`, the span error `U0 -
  S0` and the floor `S0 - B0` printed beside it: the literal `(U - P) - (U0 - S0)` keeps `S0 - B0`
  (9 to 11 mRy at the representatives of q = 6), which is the box against the sphere and not the field.
- The levels are matched per representative by their index relative to the zero pair (route P and
  J = 8, 18 at `4q - 1, 4q`, the `p_z` set at `q - 1, q`), inside `E_D +- 2.5` eV of route P's box,
  in absolute energy, so that `U >= P` can be read. The count of levels in the window is printed for
  both routes and the representatives where it differs are named below.
- Gate line (i) stays at 1e-8. The J = 3 trial reads 5.1e-8, a miss of the letter, with its
  Hermiticity 3e-12 beside it. `F/c5-N64-s05-win.log`, which read 6e-11 at the same setting, used the
  `G2 = 0` stripe (not periodic in `kappa'`), so the two numbers are of different trials.
- Not asked, taken here: the facade's `get_landau_levels(route="ultracell")` forwards `trials` and `N`
  through `**options` and refuses only when neither `nbnd` nor `N` is given, inside the 20-line
  delegation budget (`test_every_get_method_is_a_delegation`, which the first version broke at 23).
- Not asked, taken here: one J = 18 run gives the blocks of J = 2 and 8 as its leading sub-blocks
  (the trials are independent functions), so the graphene probe runs once per case and solves the
  three nested problems from the kept blocks. The mirror check below confirms the slicing.

## The trap check

One real item, covered by the guard: rule D4 in a new shape. The band window `N` can cut a degenerate
shell (the free problem's plane-wave shells at `N = 64` certainly do), and then `P_N g` depends on how
the eigensolver mixed the shell. The deficit `1 - p` is exactly the trial's weight left out, so the
jump of the projected function from one k to the next is bounded by it, and the guard refuses it above
1e-4; the gate's measured size is 5.1e-8 at J = 3. The index order of `A_{mj} = <psi_m|g_j>` was
checked against `hybrid_set`'s contraction `psi~_{j,i} = sum_m psi_{m,i} A_{mj}` and against the
probe. Clean: `abs`, `np.asarray`, the `complex` dtype and the `for k` loops are all host-side setup,
with `transport_gauge` and `wannier/trial.py` as the precedent; the k-set is built by the existing
`KPoints.from_crystal` and `for_spin`; nothing new is differentiated.
